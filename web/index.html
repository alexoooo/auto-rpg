auto‑rpg

You give rough directions. The character decides how to follow them — and its stats are what make that decision, not a difficulty slider.

  • clickGo there — a standing order, not a waypoint.
  • right clickEsc Stand down — hold this ground.
  • F Free will — no order at all; the character decides for itself.
  • RRestart the room.

A click is a standing order, so the character acts on it at its next decision — up to 12 ticks away for this one. The marker stays dim until it does, and that delay is the intellect stat.
