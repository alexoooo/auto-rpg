A click is a standing order, so the character acts on it at its next decision — up to 12 ticks away for this one. The marker stays dim until it does, and that delay is the intellect stat. Send something in and it will break off the walk to fight: a visible enemy outranks your order, and that is the whole idea. Lose, and the room stays as it is — send in a warrior or a scout and watch the same fight go differently on the same stats.
