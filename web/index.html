Controls

  • clickGo there — a standing order, not a waypoint.
  • right clickEsc Stand down — hold this ground.
  • F Free will — no order at all; the character decides for itself.
  • SB Send in a skitterer or a brute — the fight starts itself.
  • 12 Choose which of the two things you are carrying is in your hand. The swap costs real ticks and is refused mid-cut, so it is a decision and not a menu.
  • CVX Take the feet, the attack, or the choice of what to hold — any of them alone, or all three. Taking the attack takes the choice with it.
  • WASDmouse Move and aim, once you have taken them. Click to cut along the line you are pointing at — one click, one attack, with a windup you can be punished during. Holding a guard instead of a blade is a matter of what is in your hand, not a modifier key — see the Loadout panel.
  • wheel Zoom, between the whole room and close in over the character's shoulder.
  • TabOpen and close this panel.
  • RRestart the room.

A click is a standing order, so the character acts on it at its next decision — up to 12 ticks away for this one. The marker stays dim until it does, and that delay is the intellect stat. Send something in and it will break off the walk to fight: a visible enemy outranks your order, and that is the whole idea. Lose, and the room stays as it is — send in a fighter or a rogue and watch the same fight go differently on the same stats.
