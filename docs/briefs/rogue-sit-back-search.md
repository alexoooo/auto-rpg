# Brief: the Rogue's sit-back, searched

The first message of a run ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#the-rules)), as written; a run starts on it once the owner approves it.

1. Request: "stand-up for all". This run: the Rogue (`workshop-rogue`), the table's `stand-up` row, by one search of her sit-back, and nothing else.
2. Question: which of a search's three ends ([the skill recipe](../recipes/skill.md#3-a-native-controller-that-succeeds-on-some-cases)) does a search of the joint angles her sit-back drives toward reach: better (her row comes down), flat (a plateau, ten generations without a better best, and nothing above her record) or unfinished (its time ends first)? The report names the one, and never calls an unfinished search flat.
3. Search ([how](../reference/rising.md#one-technique-alone)): `node research/skills/skill-search.mjs sit-back workshop-rogue --out .tmp/skill-search/workshop-rogue.json --generations 1`, whose first candidate is her record as it stands. Read that generation's seconds, then go on with the same command and `--resume --patience 10 --generations <n>`, n the total of generations, the first counted, that fits 45 minutes of search; it ends before n only on a plateau. What it moves, its trials and its score are the tool's and stay as they are; no hand-made variant is played or installed.
4. Confirm: the same command with `--confirm .tmp/skill-search/workshop-rogue.json` in place of `--out`. It is played whichever way the search ended. Where it keeps the start, nothing is installed and the run ends there: the report gives the best score of each generation, and how the start's and the best's fresh trials ended (`each`), by technique and loadout.
5. Where it keeps the best: `--confirm ... --write` puts it in her record (`assets/skills/recovery/staged/sit-back.json`); then `node research/skills/recovery/edge-profiles.mjs --model workshop-rogue --write`; then one commit on main holding the record and the rebuilt profiles together, before the row is read: a reading is kept only where the commit holds its code unchanged.
6. Measure: `npm run evaluate -- table --body workshop-rogue --skill stand-up`, before (15.16, 23.61, 24.53, 13.94, 18.08 s, saved, not replayed) and after with `--fresh`. The record is kept where the row's lines show a cell better and none worse; else it is reverted by a new commit, the profiles with it.
7. A kept record is checked before it is reported ([the skill recipe](../recipes/skill.md#the-claim)): a fresh agent, in Node alone, reads the row's saved lines and outcomes and plays the held-out falls once (`npm run evaluate -- rising-target option --protocol fresh`). Their saved reading (`66a46676`) is older than the row's (`91ab9291`), so a difference there may be partly other code: the report says so where it leans on it. Nothing is opened or checked in a browser.
8. Changed: her sit-back record and her profiles. No other body, no Mind, no drill, no table code. The sit-back search is played here for the first time (`research/skills/skill-search.mjs`, `TechniqueCase.configs` in `src/evaluation/technique.ts`): a defect in it is fixed at its cause and named in the report; any other need for code bodies share stops the run with a report.
9. Machine time: 90 minutes of play in all, the search's 45 and the held-out falls included. Stops at the row read, at a confirm that keeps the start, or at the budget with what is unread reverted. An unfinished search's out file is kept as the recipe says (`research/runs/sit-back-search/workshop-rogue.json`, its `README.md` beside it) whatever the confirm kept; a flat or better one's is deleted with `.tmp/skill-search/`. No second search and no next item.
10. `npm run check`, `npm test` and `npm run build` passing; committed on main, not pushed. One report: which of the three ends the search reached, its generations and seconds, the confirm's two scores, the row before and after, the fresh agent's words, each defect fixed, anything done beyond the question, and what is unchecked.
