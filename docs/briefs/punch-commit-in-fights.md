# Brief: what a fight asks of the punch that the drill does not

The first message of a run ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#the-rules)), as written; approved by the agent the owner set to drive [the Warrior's punch into fights](../roadmap.md#now), and run as written.

1. Request: the owner's goal, "get skills into a good state, and then use them to have interesting fights in the Arena", driven as the Warrior's punch into fights; the owner: "keep going and iterate towards the bigger goal". Why: in the Strike drill's fights the punch lands 2 of 8 reached (`fight`) and 13 of 42 (`fights`), as at the goal's start. Of the 29 `fights` trials not landed and reached, 20 are the punch's own: 14 commit and miss or hit elsewhere (P), 4 fall by its learned stance requests (S), 2 by its learned arm pushes (A) ([the notes](../plans/2026-11-10-warrior-punch.md#why-the-punch-does-not-land-in-fights)). In the drill the same punch lands most of what it begins. [The skill recipe](../recipes/skill.md#an-options-code): "A situation the game puts the skill in is a case of its drill; one played only in research is a gap, closed by adding it to the drill."
2. Question: what is the state at the punch's commitment in those 20 fight trials that the punch's drill cases do not hold, and is that why they fail? The punch's drill cases are its table readings' and their training twins': `still`, `walk` and their `-twin`s, the Warrior, empty hands. The state is read at the first step of the committed drive, as the network reads it (`punchFeatures`, `src/skills/strikes/learned-punch.ts`): every input its layout names, by the layout's names, a left hand's mirrored as `punchChannels` mirrors it, vectors kept whole. Beside them, what the executor's admission reads (`src/skills/strikes/straight-punch.ts`, `strikeWindow`): the target's place against the head along, across and in height, the facing error, the striker's centre speed and stance phase; and, as context, the foe's centre velocity. The action's start and the drill's takeover are kept as times. Where a trial's failure is an earlier punch of the other hand (the notes name two), that punch is the one read. Each is read in the 20 trials and in every begun trial of those drill cases at `HEAD`. A quantity is named as the gap where the fight trials lie outside the drill's range of it; the report gives, beside the ranges, each fight trial's nearness to the nearest drill trial over all the inputs together, each scaled by its drill spread, since a mix of values each inside its range can be outside the drill. It is named as the cause where a fight trial changed only there lands or keeps its feet: the trial replayed with the striker or foe set, at the step before its first committed drive, its course until then unchanged, into the drill's range on that one quantity, in a run-local copy, the installed network kept, never through `tuning` (any `tuning`, `{}` included, drops it). Every other input that change moves is reported with it. A failure with no such quantity is named as what it is.
3. Start on a clean working copy at `HEAD` on main, `.tmp/punch-commit/` made. Nothing else heavy runs; port 5180 is left alone. The fight trials are replayed as the last run did (its method is in commit 8e109070's message): a script of the run's own, `.tmp/punch-commit/diagnose.mjs`, takes the `fights` protocol's Warrior conditions from the strike evaluation, builds each world as `playTrial` (`research/drill-trials.mjs`) does, wraps the reader, and first checks each replays to its saved outcome label (`assets/evaluations/strike.json`); one that does not is left unresolved and reported. The drill trials are taken by `drillTrials(cellOf({ body: "workshop-fighter", skill: "punch" }), "cell")` and its `"twin"`, read the same way.
4. Changed: nothing under `src/`, `assets/` or `research/`; no skill, drill or judge tuned, fixed or widened. A gap found is reported with its quantity, its range in each, and its code; closing it is the next brief's.
5. Committed on main, not pushed, one commit: the finding in the punch's notes, a section of its own, "What a fight asks that the drill does not", and this brief deleted. It is prose: `node --test tests/documents.test.mjs`, `git diff --check`, the line-ending gate. The per-trial table goes in the commit message.
6. Measure: for each quantity, its range over the drill's begun trials and its value in each of the 20, and how many of the 20 lie outside, printed by `node .tmp/punch-commit/diagnose.mjs`. Beside it, how many of the 20 a one-quantity change turns into landed (P) or kept on their feet (S, A).
7. Machine time: the 20 fight trials, a few seconds each, and the drill's cases (a few hundred begun trials) read once, minutes; the confirmations a few dozen replays. Budget: ninety minutes of wall-clock time from the first play, every diagnostic replay among it, each checking the clock before it starts, a defect's checks and fingerprints apart from it; a trial under way at the end finishes, and the landing of 5 and the report follow.
8. Not read by this run: the foe's guard and blows (G, B), the drill's footwork (D), the foe's step (T), the unreached moments; the club and sword; the Rogue and Skeleton as strikers (the drill's cases strike them as targets); Option Mind's choice and footwork; profiles; 480 Hz; any reel. Each named gap names one fight trial and the `fights` Ratings link for the owner to watch.
9. A defect met on the way (a trial faulted, a replay that errors; not a punch that fails, which is the question) is fixed at its cause, in a commit of its own, under `npm run check`, `npm test` and `npm run build`. Before it, `node scripts/fingerprint.mjs --json .tmp/punch-commit/fingerprint-before.json`; after it, `--compare` with that file, its lines read, not its exit code alone. No pose moved and no case new or gone: the run goes on. Otherwise the fix stays committed, no further diagnostic trial is played, and the run reports.
10. Stops when 2 is answered, when each of the 20 has its gap or is left with none its trace shows (landed as partial), or at the budget; no next item, nothing built or tuned. One report covers: each quantity's ranges, drill and fight; the 20 by gap and cause; the confirmations, with what each changed and what came of it; the code each gap lies in; what is unchecked. `.tmp/punch-commit/` is deleted once the report is written.
