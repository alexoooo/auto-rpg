# Brief: the Skeleton's roll, searched until it is stuck

The first message of a run ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#the-rules)), as written; approved by the agent the owner set to drive the stand-up goal, and run as written.

1. Request: "stand-up for all"; of the search, "i need it to keep running unless it gets stuck". This run: the Skeleton (`crypt-skeleton`), the table's `stand-up` row, by searching its roll onto its front (`staged-roll`) until the search is stuck, and nothing else. Why the roll: on its row's 80 falls (`ee5c9cd2`, `research/runs/stand-up-fail/`) 28 of its 38 falls not stood end with the roll unfinished, 21 still on the back; holding a club or a sword alone, about half its rolls time out or are unfinished.
2. Question: run to its plateau (thirty generations without a better best), does the search end better (its best beats the roll it plays now and raises its row), flat (nothing beats it), or with its best confirmed and then not kept by its row or its held-out falls (7)? The report names the one ([the skill recipe](../recipes/skill.md#3-a-native-controller-that-succeeds-on-some-cases)).
3. First, on the code as committed: `npm run evaluate -- rising-target option-prior --protocol fresh --json .tmp/skill-search/held-out-before.json` (make the folder first; no `--write`), its held-out falls on the record it plays now, for 7. Then the search ([how](../reference/rising.md#one-technique-alone)): `node research/skills/skill-search.mjs roll crypt-skeleton --out .tmp/skill-search/crypt-skeleton-roll.json --patience 30`. It starts from the Skeleton's step-up record and its own roll poses and moves their joint angles and each pose's time (`SEARCHES.roll`), 90 trials a candidate, at 120 Hz, the one rate the technique world plays (480 Hz is reported unchecked). It has no time limit and no count of generations: it ends itself, its last line saying `"ended":"plateau"`. It is started in the background, its console output kept in `.tmp/skill-search/`, and waited for however many hours, never stopped for being long; no hand-made variant is played or installed.
4. Cut before its plateau, the same command with `--resume` goes on from its out file; cut before its first generation is written, it begins again. A run that cannot go on copies the out file to `research/runs/roll-search/crypt-skeleton.json` with a `README.md` there saying what it is and how to resume it, and reports the search unfinished, never flat.
5. Confirm, once: `node research/skills/skill-search.mjs roll crypt-skeleton --confirm .tmp/skill-search/crypt-skeleton-roll.json --write`. A trial that throws stops it with nothing written: a defect (9). Where it keeps the record (`"kept":"record"`) nothing is written, the search is flat, and the run ends there, reporting each generation's best and the record's and the best's fresh trials (`each`) by start and loadout. Where it keeps the best, it has written it into the Skeleton's step-up record (`assets/skills/recovery/staged/step-up.json`, the rest as it was); then `node research/skills/recovery/edge-profiles.mjs --model crypt-skeleton --write`, its profiles for the game's Mind, which the row does not read; then one commit on main holding the record and the profiles, before the row is read.
6. Measure: `npm run evaluate -- table --body crypt-skeleton --skill stand-up --fresh`, waited for to its exit, its console output kept in `.tmp/skill-search/row.txt` (its `replaced` lines are printed only then); a play cut by evaluate's budget is the same command again. Before, saved (`eab9dd5a`): 13.705 s (empty hands), 28.939 (club), 25.935 (buckler and club), 22.649 (sword), 16.412 (buckler and sword). Then, on that commit, `npm run evaluate -- rising-target option-prior --protocol fresh --json .tmp/skill-search/held-out-after.json`.
7. Kept only where the change raises the row ([what a change is kept on](../recipes/skill.md#what-a-change-is-kept-on)): a `replaced` line reads better and none worse, and on its 80 held-out falls, paired by condition (seconds to stand: `verified` where stood, the 30 s cap where not, a fall not made left out), the 95 % paired bootstrap interval of the mean difference does not lie wholly above zero. Otherwise a new commit puts the record, the profiles and the row's saved reading (`assets/evaluations/rising-target.json`) back as they were, and the report names that end. A kept record is then checked by a fresh agent ([the claim](../recipes/skill.md#the-claim)), given the claim, the row's lines (`npm run evaluate -- table --body crypt-skeleton --skill stand-up`) and the two held-out files and no account of the work; its words go in the report, and where they contradict the claim the report withdraws it.
8. Changed: the Skeleton's step-up record, its profiles and the row's saved reading; no other body, no Mind, no drill. Played here for the first time: the roll search on the Skeleton's own roll poses.
9. A defect met is fixed at its cause in a commit of its own, under the checks for code, and named in the report; any other need for code bodies share stops the run with a report.
10. Stops at a confirm that keeps the record, or once 7 decides; no second search and no next item. Nothing in `research/runs/` is committed. Each commit passes the checks [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#checks-and-landing) names for what it changes; committed on main, not pushed; this brief deleted in the run's last commit. One report: the end the search reached, its generations and hours, the confirm's two scores, the row before and after with its `replaced` lines, the held-out readings, the fresh agent's words where it ran, each defect fixed, and what is unchecked.
