# Brief: what the punch's steer keeps as one layer, apart from the legs

The first message of a run ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#the-rules)), as written.

1. Request: the owner: "the problem that I am trying to address is development velocity, especially around iterating on and adding new skills"; "i want to implement any framework/methodology changes, and then use the warrior punch as a testbed to validate the implementation"; and, offered readings taken in one run before anything is built (a control, three fits, the edits of the installed network and the time of a `--bar`): "go ahead, update the plans and write the brief". Why: a strike's steer is trained by PPO, one to two and a half days a run, and the punch's network reads the legs and asks the stance ([the notes](../plans/2026-11-14-many-skills.md#where-the-time-goes)). The drive's settings with no network land 20 of the Warrior's 240 held-out sparring starts, against 105 steered. Whether a steer small enough to search, asking nothing of the legs, keeps those landings is unread.
2. Question: on the Warrior's held-out sparring starts, how many of the installed steer's landings does the straight punch keep when its steer is one layer that reads the arm and the target and asks nothing of the legs, and what does the installed network lose with each of its ties to the legs cut?
3. Start on a clean working copy at `HEAD` on main, `.tmp/punch-readings/` made. Nothing else heavy runs; port 5180 is left alone. Every file the run writes but the tool is under `.tmp/punch-readings/` and is never committed.
4. Changed, none of it asked for by the owner beyond the words in 1: `research/skills/strikes/straight-punch/punch-sparring.mjs` alone. **(a)** `--network <form> --out <file>` writes a network file in the installed layout (`assets/skills/strikes/straight-punch/drive.layout.json`: 59 inputs, 12 outputs) from the Warrior's installed network (`dataOf("straight-punch/drive").man.network`), keeping its `normalization` and `actionLimit`. An input or output is found by its name in the layout, never by a number written in the tool. The forms: `installed`, the network unedited (the control); `no-stance-asks`, the last layer's `heading` and two `centre` rows at zero weights and bias; `walk-on` and `walk-off`, the last layer's `walk` row at zero weights, its bias 1 and -1; `blind-legs`, the first layer's columns at zero for every `angle` and `speed` input of a `hip.`, `knee.` or `ankle.` channel and for `near foot bearing`, `far foot bearing`, `stance shifting` and `stance swinging`; `fit-inputs`, the first layer's columns at zero for every input but the fit's 16; and the three fits. The fit's 16 inputs: `target from shoulder`, `fist from target`, `fist velocity` and `centre velocity`, each across, up and ahead, and the `angle` of `shoulder.right flexion`, `shoulder.right abduction`, `shoulder.right internal rotation` and `elbow.right flexion`. **A fit** is one layer (`recoveryNetwork`, `src/core/math/learning/recovery-network.ts`). The mode plays the twin's training half once (the `sparring-twin` starts `--on train` plays), steered by the installed network through `steeredTrial`, and keeps each decision's 59 inputs. It solves by least squares, on the inputs as `normalization` transforms them, for the installed network's eight `pushes` outputs before tanh, as `actionLimit` clamps them. Every other column is zero; its `heading` and `centre` rows are zero; its `walk` row is zero weights and bias -1. `fit` reads the 16; `fit-aged` reads them and `drive age`; `fit-spans` is a layer for each of two or three spans of `drive age`, the split the run's choice, each fitted on its span's decisions, in a file whose layer the tool chooses by `drive age` at each decision (`candidateIn`, `policyOf`). The solve's conditioning is the run's choice. **(b)** `--bar` prints, beside what it prints now: the starts only the candidate lands and the starts only the drive lands, the exact two-sided sign test's p on those two counts, and the wall-clock seconds each side took to play. Not changed: anything under `src/` or `assets/`; any record, network, layout, protocol or the bar (`RATE`, `LOW`). Nothing is installed, searched or trained.
5. Order: the tool's change first, under `npm run check` and `npm test`, with a test where [the test inventory](../../tests/README.md) asks for one, committed. Then the control: `node research/skills/strikes/straight-punch/punch-sparring.mjs --network installed --out .tmp/punch-readings/installed.json`, and its `--bar` as in 6. The control holds where its sign test's p is 0.05 or more and its damage ratio's interval holds 1. Where it does not, the tool does not steer a network file as the game steers its record's network: a defect of the tool, fixed at its cause in research code, in a commit of its own within the budget, and the control read again. With no control that holds by the budget, the run reads no other file and reports the control. With one, the other eight files are written (`--network <form> --out .tmp/punch-readings/<form>.json`) and read, in any order.
6. Measure: each file's starts landed of the Warrior's 240 held-out `sparring` starts beside the drive's on the same starts, with the starts only one of them lands and the sign test's p: `node research/skills/strikes/straight-punch/punch-sparring.mjs --bar .tmp/punch-readings/<form>.json --workers 30`, its output kept as `.tmp/punch-readings/<form>.txt`, once for each of `installed`, `fit`, `fit-aged`, `fit-spans`, `no-stance-asks`, `walk-on`, `walk-off`, `blind-legs` and `fit-inputs`. The drive's side of each is the measure before, the file's the measure after. Beside it, as `--bar` prints them: damage dealt a second against the drive's with its interval, life taken, falls and the table's value a trial. A `--bar` ends in PASS or FAIL against the bar of a better punch; that line is not this run's measure, and FAIL is expected.
7. Machine time: the fits' one play of the training half, 127 starts, and nine `--bar`s of 240 starts by two sides. A sparring trial plays at most 11 s at about 0.55 s of a core a simulated second, so a side is at most 24 core-minutes and the run about 7 core-hours, under half an hour on 30 workers, by arithmetic; it is priced again from the control's printed seconds. Budget: two hours of wall-clock time from the first play, a defect's fix and its replays among it. A `--bar` under way at the end finishes, nothing new starts, and the landing of 8 and the report follow.
8. Committed on main, not pushed. First the tool (5). Last, one prose commit: [the notes](../plans/2026-11-14-many-skills.md#the-readings-that-come-first), their "Where it stands" under "The readings that come first" brought to the readings, each with the command that reads it again, and "What a try's comparison costs" to the seconds of a `--bar`; the same seconds in [the played rows' notes](../plans/2026-11-14-saved-results.md#open), "What it saves a try"; a line of [the roadmap](../roadmap.md#parked-plans) only where a reading makes it untrue; and this brief deleted. It is prose: `node --test tests/documents.test.mjs`, `git diff --check`, the line-ending gate. Its message holds every file's figures as a table, the spans chosen and each fit's share of each output's variance on the training half.
9. Not read by this run: the Rogue and the Skeleton; the still and walk rungs; `fight` and `fights`, and whether sparring's commitments cover theirs; 480 Hz; profiles; any reel; any search or training from a fit. A defect met on the way (a trial faulted, a world refused; not a file that lands few, which is the measure) is fixed at its cause, in a commit of its own, under `npm run check`, `npm test` and `npm run build`. Where the fix is under `src/`: before it, `node scripts/fingerprint.mjs --json .tmp/punch-readings/fingerprint-before.json`; after it, `--compare` with that file, its lines read, not its exit code alone. No pose moved and no case new or gone: the run goes on. Otherwise the fix stays committed, nothing more is played, and the run reports.
10. Stops when each of the nine files has its reading, at a control that does not hold (5), or at the budget; no next item: no variation is built, nothing is searched, and no file's reading is acted on. One report covers: each file's landings beside the drive's, the starts only one lands and its p, its damage ratio and interval, falls, life taken and value; whether the control held and what was fixed for it; the spans chosen and how well each fit copies the network on the training half; the seconds a side of a `--bar` took; everything done beyond the question; what is unchecked. `.tmp/punch-readings/` is deleted once the report is written.
