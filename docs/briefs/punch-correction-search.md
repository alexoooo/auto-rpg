# Brief: a search of the punch's correction from its per-span fit

The first message of a run ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#the-rules)), as written; approved by the agent the owner set to drive [the day's notes in their order](../roadmap.md#next), and run as written.

1. Request: the owner: "please implement the entire sequencing of today's plans (profiles,saved,many-skill,fighting-base) as far as you're able", and "for any re-measure, let's do it at the end if possible". Why: a strike's steer is trained by PPO, one to two and a half days a run ([the notes](../plans/2026-11-14-many-skills.md#where-the-time-goes)). A layer for each of three spans of the Warrior's punch, over 16 inputs that hold no leg, fitted to the installed network with its asks of the stance, deals 0.9809 times the installed drive's damage a second on the judge's 2,400 held-out starts, 95 % 0.9154 to 1.0480 ([its readings](../plans/2026-11-14-many-skills.md#the-readings-that-come-first)). Whether a search from it passes the drive is unread, and no search of a correction has run ([the search](../plans/2026-11-14-many-skills.md#part-2-a-strike-made-by-search-apart-from-the-legs)).
2. Question: started from that fit, does the tool's search of the punch's 15 settings and the fit's 561 weights, in ten hours, make a punch that deals more damage a second than the installed drive on the judge's draw? Yes where the low end of its interval there is above 1; otherwise no, with the ratio and the interval.
3. Start: the run's working copy is a worktree of its own at this brief's commit, `.tmp/worktrees/punch-search/` of the repository, made by the driving session, and the run is started in it: every command below is run from the worktree's root, as written, with 24 workers. The driving session has put there the played rows (`.cache/evaluations/strike/`) and the fit the reading in 1 is of, `.tmp/punch-search/start.json`. The run changes no tracked file, commits nothing and runs no Git command that writes; it writes under `.tmp/punch-search/`, and (c) may add rows to `.cache/evaluations/strike/`. The repository's main working copy is another session's, at work meanwhile: nothing there is read, changed or reported. Port 5180 is left alone. Where `start.json` is absent the run stops and reports that.
4. Changed: nothing. The tool is `research/skills/strikes/straight-punch/punch-sparring.mjs` as the worktree has it; its header says what each mode does.
5. Order. **(b)** The search, started so that it outlives the shell call that starts it, from PowerShell: `Start-Process -FilePath $env:ComSpec -WorkingDirectory (Get-Location).Path -WindowStyle Hidden -PassThru -ArgumentList '/d','/c','node research/skills/strikes/straight-punch/punch-sparring.mjs --search --steer .tmp/punch-search/start.json --out .tmp/punch-search/search.json --lambda 48 --starts 24 --rank 4 --sigma 0.05 --step 0.03 --patience 12 --hours 10 --workers 24 >> .tmp/punch-search/search.txt 2>&1'`. It is waited on by reading `search.txt` between waits of at most 25 minutes a call, never by a call that holds a shell open for hours ([the traps](../traps.md#tools-on-windows), "A background wait ends on its own"). It prints the start's reading on the twin, then a line a generation, about five minutes each, every fourth with its mean's reading on the twin; it ends itself with a line beginning `ended after`, at the first generation's end past ten hours of its own clock or once twelve twin readings pass without a better one. Three branches: `search.txt` holds an error (a stack, a refusal): a defect (9). Its Node process is gone, `search.txt` holds no error and no `ended after` line, and `search.json` exists: the same command with ` --resume` after `--workers 24` goes on from it, the tool's hours counting on. The same with no `search.json`, or `search.txt` gaining no line in 40 minutes while its process lives: a defect, the process stopped with its tree (`taskkill /T /F /PID <pid>`). **(c)** The judge, once the search has ended, whichever way it ended: `node research/skills/strikes/straight-punch/punch-sparring.mjs --bar .tmp/punch-search/search.json --on judge --workers 24 > .tmp/punch-search/judge.txt 2>&1`, about ten minutes, the drive's side found among the played rows; it reads the mean the twin read best, the outer `best` of `search.json`.
6. Measure: damage dealt a second, times the installed drive's, with its 95 % interval, on the judge's draw, as (c) prints it (`damage ratio`). Before: the start's 0.9809, 0.9154 to 1.0480, read on this `start.json`. After: the search's best. Beside it, as (c) prints them: the starts landed, those only one side lands with the sign test's p, the falls, the life taken, the table's value a trial, how many of the drive's rows were found, and how many of those played again read as kept. A `--bar` ends in PASS or FAIL against the bar of a better punch, twice the drive with its low end above 1.5; that line is reported and is not this run's measure. Where the search's best is its start (the outer `best.g` is -1 in `search.json`), (c) is not played: the after is the before, and the report says the search found no mean the twin read better.
7. Machine time: ten hours of the search's own clock (`--hours 10`, read at each generation's end), and under half an hour for (c). A search that ends itself sooner by its patience is judged as one that ran its hours is.
8. Committed: nothing, and nothing is pushed. `.tmp/punch-search/` is left as it is for the driving session, which checks the report against its files, brings [the notes](../plans/2026-11-14-many-skills.md) to the reading, deletes this brief and removes the worktree.
9. Not read by this run: any other body, skill or variation; `sparring`'s 240 starts; `fight` and `fights`; 480 Hz; profiles; any reel; a search from another start or with other settings; nothing is installed. A defect met on the way (a trial faulted, a world refused, the tool throwing; not a search that finds nothing, which is the measure) stops the run: it is reported with its output and the command that met it, and is not fixed here, since the run changes no file.
10. Stops when (c) has printed, at a search whose best is its start, or at a defect; no next item. One report covers: the start's reading on the twin and each twin reading after it, as `search.txt` prints them, with the generation of the best; how the search ended, after how many generations and hours, and whether it was resumed; the damage ratio and its interval before and after, with what (c) prints beside it and its PASS or FAIL; the answer to 2; everything done beyond the question; what is unchecked.
