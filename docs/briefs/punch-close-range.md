# Brief: why the Warrior's punch is not begun from close

The first message of a run ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#the-rules)), as written; approved by the owner, and run as written.

1. Request: the owner's goal, "get skills into a good state, and then use them to have interesting fights in the Arena"; on whether to build "a dedicated close-range punch, or a punch that will work across both close and far ranges", the owner asked: "write a brief to diagnose the close-range punch losses". Why: which of the two is worth building depends on whose failure the close losses are, the punch's or the footwork's, and the saved results say only `outside`.
2. Question: why do the Warrior's still-rung punches from close never begin? The cell is `workshop-fighter`, `empty,empty`, `punch`, reading `strike/still option-drill/straight-punch`. Of its 96 trials, 42 at gaps 0.6 to 0.9 m are not begun and count 0: every trial at 0.6, 0.7 and 0.8 m and 6 of 12 at 0.9 m, 36 `outside` and 6 `unbegun` (the 2 not begun at 1.1 m are not close and not read). It is answered when each of the 42 has its cause read from its own trial, and each cause is named as one of two things. **The punch:** its window (`strikeWindow`, `src/skills/strikes/strike-window.ts`; the record's `reach`, `band`, `poised` in `assets/skills/strikes/straight-punch/straight-punch.json`) is placed where the body cannot stand or settle. **The footwork:** the drill's walk into that window (`src/minds/command/drill.ts`, the walk it orders, at up to 1 m/s, 8 s `patience`, 11 s a trial, `STILL` in `src/evaluation/strike/cases.ts`) does not bring the body there and hold it. A cause that is both, or neither, is named as what it is.
3. Start on a clean working copy at `HEAD` on main. Nothing else heavy runs; port 5180 is left alone. Before: `npm run evaluate -- table --body workshop-fighter --skill punch --loadout empty,empty --by gap`, kept in `.tmp/punch-close/before.txt`.
4. How: the 42 trials are played again at `HEAD`, one at a time, at 120 Hz, by the run's own script `.tmp/punch-close/diagnose.mjs`: it takes them from `research/drill-trials.mjs` (`cellOf`, `drillTrials(cell, "cell")`, the `still` reading's only) and builds each world as `playTrial` does, wrapping the reader it gives `playCase` (`playTrial` has no hook a step). Each step it keeps the punch's readiness and its window, where the striker's head and stance stand in that window, the stance's phase and speed, and what the drill orders. First it checks that each trial replays to its saved outcome (`assets/evaluations/strike.json`); a trial that does not is reported: one whose replay now begins is counted apart, not reproduced and its cause unresolved; one not begun another way is diagnosed on its replay. A cause is confirmed by a trial changed only where the cause says, in a run-local copy of the code or record under `.tmp/punch-close/`, loaded so that nothing else differs and the installed network is kept, never committed, and that trial then begins (its reading's `began`). A setting is never changed through `tuning`, which also drops the installed network (`src/skills/strikes/punch-part.ts`).
5. Changed: nothing under `src/`, `assets/` or `research/` but a defect's fix (9). The 42's cause is the question, not a defect: no skill, Mind, drill or evaluation is tuned or fixed for it; it is reported with its code and evidence, and its fix is the owner's next request. Committed on main, in one commit after any defect's, not pushed: the finding, in the punch's notes (`docs/plans/2026-11-10-warrior-punch.md`), its other open questions kept. The notes' "Where it stands" is brought to the row's saved readings in place of "not read" (`npm run evaluate -- table --body workshop-fighter --skill punch`, which plays nothing), and the close losses' cause is added under it. The commit also deletes this brief. Checks for prose: `node --test tests/documents.test.mjs`, `git diff --check`, the line-ending gate.
6. Measure: the 42 trials' causes, counted by gap, printed by `node .tmp/punch-close/diagnose.mjs`. Beside it, the before of 3 read again with the same command, which plays nothing and must be unchanged.
7. Machine time: a trial not begun ends 8.5 s in (the 0.5 s `settled` and 8 s `patience`), a few seconds of two bodies to play, so the 42 take minutes. Budget: one hour of the script's play, its confirmations among it, kept by the script itself, checked as it steps and across its restarts; a defect's checks and fingerprints are apart from it and reported. A trial cut by it is left without a cause. At the budget the run lands what it read, marked partial, as 5 and 10 say.
8. Not diagnosed by this run: the `empty,club` and `empty,sword` cells (the same gaps, 20 and 18 outside; their saved readings are read for 5), the Rogue's and the Skeleton's punch, the walk rung, 480 Hz, fights and Option Mind's footwork, any reel. Each cause names one trial and its Ratings link (the reading's line in 3) for the owner to watch.
9. A defect (a trial faulted, a world refused, a replay that errors) is fixed at its cause: first `node scripts/fingerprint.mjs --json .tmp/punch-close/fingerprint-before.json` on the commit before, then the fix in a commit of its own under the checks for code (`npm run check`, `npm test`, `npm run build`), named in the report, and `node scripts/fingerprint.mjs --compare .tmp/punch-close/fingerprint-before.json`. No pose moved and no case new or gone: the run goes on. Otherwise, or where either fingerprint fails, the saved outcomes are of the code before, so nothing more is played and the run reports.
10. Stops when 2 is answered or at the budget; no next item, nothing built or tuned. One report covers: the 42 trials' causes by gap; for each cause, one trial traced (when it was ordered, where the window was against the body, what the body did, when the trial ended); the change that made a trial begin; the code it lies in; whether it is the punch's or the footwork's; which of the owner's two punches, if either, it argues for; what is unchecked. `.tmp/punch-close/` is deleted once the report is written.
