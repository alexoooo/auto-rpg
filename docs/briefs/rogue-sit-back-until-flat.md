# Brief: the Rogue's sit-back, searched until it is stuck

The first message of a run ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#the-rules)), as written; a run starts on it once the owner approves it.

1. Request: "stand-up for all", and of the search: "i need it to keep running unless it gets stuck". This run: the Rogue (`workshop-rogue`), the table's `stand-up` row, by going on with the saved search of her sit-back until it is stuck, and nothing else.
2. Question: run to its plateau (thirty generations without a better best), does the search end better (its best beats the record she plays now, and her row is no worse for it) or flat (nothing beats that record)? The report names the one ([the skill recipe](../recipes/skill.md#3-a-native-controller-that-succeeds-on-some-cases)).
3. Search ([how](../reference/rising.md#one-technique-alone)): copy `research/runs/sit-back-search/workshop-rogue.json` (50 generations played; its best is the record she plays now) to `.tmp/skill-search/workshop-rogue.json`, then `node research/skills/skill-search.mjs sit-back workshop-rogue --out .tmp/skill-search/workshop-rogue.json --resume --patience 30`. It has no time limit and no count of generations: it runs for as long as it keeps improving, about 37 s a generation so far, and ends itself, its last line saying `"ended":"plateau"`. It is started in the background and waited for, however many hours, and never stopped for being long. What it moves, its trials and its score stay as they are; no hand-made variant is played or installed.
4. Cut before its plateau (a crash, a killed process), the same command goes on from its out file. A run that cannot go on copies the out file over `research/runs/sit-back-search/workshop-rogue.json`, corrects that folder's `README.md`, and reports the search unfinished, never flat.
5. Confirm: `node research/skills/skill-search.mjs sit-back workshop-rogue --confirm .tmp/skill-search/workshop-rogue.json` plays the record she plays now (`base`) and the search's best on fresh trials. Where it keeps her record (`"kept":"record"`) the search is flat: nothing is installed and the run ends there, its report giving the best score of each generation and how the record's and the best's fresh trials ended (`each`), by technique and loadout.
6. Where it keeps the best: the same command with `--write` puts it in her record (`assets/skills/recovery/staged/sit-back.json`); then `node research/skills/recovery/edge-profiles.mjs --model workshop-rogue --write`; then one commit on main holding the record and the rebuilt profiles together, before the row is read: a reading is kept only where the commit holds its code unchanged.
7. Measure: `npm run evaluate -- table --body workshop-rogue --skill stand-up --fresh`. Before, saved and not replayed: 12.75 s (empty hands), 18.41 (club), 15.82 (buckler and club), 15.05 (sword), 14.36 (buckler and sword). Under each cell its `replaced` line compares the new reading with the one it replaces, fall by fall. The record is kept, and the reading committed, unless a `replaced` line says worse; then a new commit puts the record, the profiles and the row's saved reading back as they were.
8. A kept record is checked before it is reported ([the claim](../recipes/skill.md#the-claim)): a fresh agent, in Node alone, reads the row's saved lines and outcomes and plays the held-out falls once (`npm run evaluate -- rising-target option --protocol fresh`), against their readings on the record before in `research/runs/sit-back-search/held-out-falls.json`. Nothing is opened or checked in a browser.
9. Changed: her sit-back record, her profiles and her row's saved reading. No other body, no Mind, no drill. Played here for the first time: a search with no count of generations and its `ended`, a confirm against the record she plays, and the table's `replaced` line (`research/skills/skill-search.mjs`, `scripts/table.mjs`); a defect in them is fixed at its cause and named in the report, and any other need for code bodies share stops the run with a report.
10. Stops at the row read or at a confirm that keeps her record; no second search and no next item. A search that reached its plateau is finished: its file in `research/runs/sit-back-search/` is deleted and the `README.md` corrected, and nothing in `research/runs/` is added to a commit. `npm run check`, `npm test` and `npm run build` passing; committed on main, not pushed. One report: the end the search reached, its generations and hours, the confirm's two scores, the row before and after with its `replaced` lines, the fresh agent's words, each defect fixed, anything done beyond the question, and what is unchecked.
