# Brief: the Rogue's roll, searched until it is stuck

The first message of a run ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#the-rules)), as written; a run starts on it once the owner approves it.

1. Request: "stand-up for all"; of the search, "i need it to keep running unless it gets stuck"; of a search of the Rogue's roll, "ok build it". This run: the Rogue (`workshop-rogue`), the table's `stand-up` row, by searching her roll onto her front (`staged-roll`) until the search is stuck, and nothing else. Why the roll: on her 80 held-out falls (`research/runs/sit-back-search/held-out-falls.json`) a fall she stood from spent 3.7 s rolling against the Warrior's 2.0 s, the largest part of her 3.3 s behind him; her roll's poses are the Warrior's.
2. Question: run to its plateau (thirty generations without a better best), does the search end better (its best beats the roll she plays now, and her row is no worse for it), flat (nothing beats it), or with its best confirmed and then rejected by her row (7)? The report names the one ([the skill recipe](../recipes/skill.md#3-a-native-controller-that-succeeds-on-some-cases)).
3. Search ([how](../reference/rising.md#one-technique-alone)): `node research/skills/skill-search.mjs roll workshop-rogue --out .tmp/skill-search/workshop-rogue-roll.json --patience 30`. It starts from her step-up record and moves the joint angles of the roll's four poses and each pose's time (`SEARCHES.roll`), 90 trials a candidate; a generation's time is not yet known. It has no time limit and no count of generations: it ends itself, its last line saying `"ended":"plateau"`. It is started in the background and waited for, however many hours, and never stopped for being long. What it moves, its trials and its score stay as they are, at 120 Hz, the one rate the technique world plays (480 Hz is reported unchecked); no hand-made variant is played or installed.
4. Cut before its plateau (a crash, a killed process), the same command with `--resume` goes on from its out file; cut before its first generation is written, it begins again, which is the same search and not a second. A run that cannot go on copies the out file to `research/runs/roll-search/workshop-rogue.json` with a `README.md` in that folder saying what it is and how to resume it, and reports the search unfinished, never flat.
5. Confirm: `node research/skills/skill-search.mjs roll workshop-rogue --confirm .tmp/skill-search/workshop-rogue-roll.json` plays her step-up record as it is (`base`) and the search's best on fresh trials (`placed` and `moving`, other seeds). Where it keeps her record (`"kept":"record"`) the search is flat: nothing is installed and the run ends there, its report giving the best score of each generation and how the record's and the best's fresh trials ended (`each`), by start and loadout.
6. Where it keeps the best: the same command with `--write` puts it in her step-up record (`assets/skills/recovery/staged/step-up.json`, its `roll` and `roll <pose>` times, the rest as it was); then `node research/skills/recovery/edge-profiles.mjs --model workshop-rogue --write`; then one commit on main holding the record and the rebuilt profiles together, before the row is read: a reading is kept only where the commit holds its code unchanged.
7. Measure: `npm run evaluate -- table --body workshop-rogue --skill stand-up --fresh`. It plays the stand-up reading whole, 240 falls, 80 on each body (about 3 minutes last time); the other bodies' settings are untouched, and only her five cells are judged. Before, saved and not replayed: 12.75 s (empty hands), 18.41 (club), 15.82 (buckler and club), 15.05 (sword), 14.36 (buckler and sword). Under each cell its `replaced` line compares the new reading with the one it replaces, fall by fall. The record is kept, and the reading committed, unless a `replaced` line says worse; then a new commit puts the record, the profiles and the row's saved reading back as they were, and the report names that end as it is: confirmed by the search's trials, rejected by her row, put back.
8. A kept record is checked before it is reported ([the claim](../recipes/skill.md#the-claim)): a fresh agent, in Node alone, reads the row's saved lines and plays the held-out falls once (`npm run evaluate -- rising-target option --protocol fresh --json .tmp/skill-search/held-out-after.json`, no `--write`), against their readings on the record before in `research/runs/sit-back-search/held-out-falls.json`: falls stood before and after, and the seconds a stood fall spends in `staged-roll` (the sum of its attempts whose `controller` is `staged-roll`), over the falls stood in each and over those stood in both. Where its words contradict the claim, the report withdraws the claim and says so; the record stays as 7 decided. Nothing is opened or checked in a browser.
9. Changed: her step-up record's roll, her profiles and her row's saved reading. No other body, no Mind, no drill. Played here for the first time: the search's roll (`SEARCHES.roll`), a candidate step-up record played in place of hers (`stepUpTuningWith`), and a row written into part of a record keeping how the rest was made (`research/skills/skill-search.mjs`); a defect in them is fixed at its cause and named in the report, and any other need for code bodies share stops the run with a report.
10. Stops at a confirm that keeps her record, or once her row is read and, where it keeps the roll, checked (8); no second search and no next item. Nothing in `research/runs/` is added to a commit. Each commit passes the checks [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#checks-and-landing) names for what it changes; committed on main, not pushed; this brief deleted in the run's last commit, whatever the end. One report: the end the search reached, its generations and hours, the confirm's two scores, the row before and after with its `replaced` lines, the fresh agent's words, each defect fixed, anything done beyond the question, and what is unchecked.
