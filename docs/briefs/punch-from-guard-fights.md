# Brief: the punch from its guard against its drive, in the Strike drill's fights

The first message of a run ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#the-rules)), as written, for the owner to approve.

1. Request, the owner's words: "now that we have the ability to have separate versions of a skill, we can test how well a change does", toward "interesting and high skill fights"; then "let evaluations name a variation" and "write the brief". Why this comparison: in the Strike drill's fights (`fights`) the Warrior's punch lands 13 of 42 reached trials in its saved reading at 86a217d4, and every fight commitment compared has an input outside the range the drill's commitments hold, the elbow's flexion in 19 of 20, which marks missing coverage rather than a cause ([the notes](../plans/2026-11-10-warrior-punch.md#what-a-fight-asks-that-the-drill-does-not)). The straight punch's `from-guard` variation (`src/skills/strikes/straight-punch/from-guard/`) commits only with the striking arm at its guard and still. The same rule once sat in the shared punch code, where it also changed the foe's punch and every body's guard, and was reverted (00d12b7a); a subject naming the variation (`+straight-punch/from-guard`, `variedSubjects`) changes the striker's punch alone.
2. Question: on the same `fights` cases, at the same commit, does the Warrior land more of its reached punch trials with `from-guard` than with `drive`?
3. Start on a clean working copy at `HEAD` on main, its hash kept as the start; make `.tmp/punch-variations/`. Nothing else heavy runs; port 5180 is left alone. No code, record, network, drill, protocol, judge or mind changes in this run; nothing is tuned.
4. Play, through PowerShell in the background (`npm.cmd` with the same arguments where `npm.ps1` is refused, [the traps](../traps.md)), waiting for each pass to exit, `<pass>` the pass's number from 1: `npm run evaluate -- strike option/straight-punch+straight-punch/from-guard --previous option/straight-punch --protocol fights --workers 30 --budget <minutes> --write --json .tmp/punch-variations/fights-<pass>.json`, `<minutes>` the budget's minutes left, at most 45. Both subjects play the same 144 conditions (Warrior, Rogue and Skeleton; each hand; gaps 3, 4 and 5 m; moments 1 to 8); the rung's other 432 conditions are dropped as not offered. The command is run again while the reading is not whole and the hour is not over (a pass ends with cases lost, or unplayed when its `--budget` ran out), each later pass going on from the rows kept; the reading is whole when every one of the 288 cases has a row. A faulted case keeps its row and is never played again: it is named in the report with its fault, and its condition is left out of the pairs in 5 and 6. The printed "condition by condition" table is the paired table reported, not used for the answer.
5. The one measure, read by `node .tmp/punch-variations/measure.mjs .tmp/punch-variations/fights-<last>.json`, a script the run writes there and never commits (a file under `scripts/`, `src/`, `research/` or `assets/` would be a build input, and no row would then be kept or `--write` accepted). From the `--json` file's `cases` rows (each `{ subjects, condition, reading, fault, ... }`: the subject `subjects[0]`, the body `condition.model`, and, where `fault` is null, the outcome `EVALUATIONS.strike.outcome(reading).label`, the one outcome source for both subjects and the saved reading), for each subject: the Warrior's (`man`) trials `landed` of those reached (every outcome but `unreached`), as a count and a share; the Warrior's conditions where the two subjects' outcomes differ, each with both outcomes; the Rogue's (`woman`) and the Skeleton's (`skeleton`) landed of reached, given beside, not judged. The script also reads drive's saved reading, the `fights` result of `option/straight-punch` in `git show <start>:assets/evaluations/strike.json` (13 of 42 for the Warrior): its `trials` follow its case list (`cases`, a key of the file's `cases`), each name a condition's `caseName` (`src/evaluation/results.ts`), each trial `[played, index into its outcomes]`, null where faulted. Each of drive's 48 Warrior trials now is matched to its saved one by `caseName(condition)`, its outcome against the saved outcome's `label`.
6. The answer, by share, the variation changing which moments a fight reaches: "yes" where `from-guard` lands a larger share of the Warrior's reached trials and an exact two-sided sign test over the Warrior's conditions reached by both, where exactly one subject landed, gives p below 0.05; "no" where its share is as large or smaller; "not shown" where it is larger but p is 0.05 or above. The run does not play more cases or other protocols to settle it.
7. Machine time: 288 trials on 30 workers, minutes; the budget is one hour of wall-clock time from the first play. No pass starts after the hour; a pass under way finishes. A reading not whole by then is reported as not whole, and `git checkout -- assets/evaluations/strike.json` puts back what `--write` changed: nothing is committed.
8. Where any of drive's 48 Warrior trials now differs from its saved one (another outcome, or a fault), committing would replace the reading the punch's notes and the roadmap describe: `git checkout -- assets/evaluations/strike.json`, nothing is committed, and the report names each differing trial and the commits since the saved reading that touch the punch, from their diffs and messages, playing nothing more. Otherwise, committed on main, not pushed: `assets/evaluations/strike.json` as `--write` left it, under `npm test`, `npm run build` and the line-ending gate; its message holds both subjects' Warrior counts and shares, the sign test, the differing conditions and the printed condition-by-condition table. Then one prose commit: a paragraph at the end of the notes' "Why the punch does not land in fights" ([the notes](../plans/2026-11-10-warrior-punch.md#why-the-punch-does-not-land-in-fights)) giving the two Warrior counts and the command that plays them again, item 4's with `--budget 45` and without `--write` and `--json`, its counts the `man` rows of the trial table it prints, with no commit hash or date; this brief deleted; `node --test tests/documents.test.mjs`, `git diff --check` and the line-ending gate.
9. Not read by this run: the table's cells, the `fight` protocol (its trials are the `fights` gap-4 Warrior trials), the other strikes, profiles, Option Mind's choice between variations, 480 Hz, any reel. A defect met on the way (a trial faulted, a world refused; not a punch that misses, which is the measure) is reported with its cause, not fixed.
10. Stops when 2 is answered or at the budget; no next item. `.tmp/punch-variations/` is deleted (the play's rows in `.tmp/evaluations/strike/` are evaluate's, left for `npm run clean`), then one report: the Warrior's landed of reached for each subject, count and share, and the answer by 6 with its p; the differing conditions; the Rogue's and the Skeleton's counts; drive's Warrior trials against their saved ones; each faulted case; the commits; what is unchecked.
