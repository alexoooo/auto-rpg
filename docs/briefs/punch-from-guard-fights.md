# Brief: the punch from its guard against its drive, in the Strike drill's fights

The first message of a run ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#the-rules)), as written, for the owner to approve.

1. Request, the owner's words: "now that we have the ability to have separate versions of a skill, we can test how well a change does", toward "interesting and high skill fights"; then "let evaluations name a variation" and "write the brief". Why this comparison: in the Strike drill's fights (`fights`) the Warrior's punch lands 13 of 42 reached trials in its saved reading at 86a217d4, and every fight commitment starts from a state the drill never holds, the punching arm away from its guard in 19 of 20 ([the notes](../plans/2026-11-10-warrior-punch.md#what-a-fight-asks-that-the-drill-does-not)). The straight punch's `from-guard` variation (`src/skills/strikes/straight-punch/from-guard/`) commits only with the striking arm at its guard and still. The same rule once sat in the shared punch code, where it also changed the foe's punch and every body's guard, and was reverted (00d12b7a); a subject naming the variation (`+straight-punch/from-guard`, `variedSubjects`) changes the striker's punch alone.
2. Question: on the same `fights` cases, at the same commit, does the Warrior land more of its reached punch trials with `from-guard` than with `drive`?
3. Start on a clean working copy at `HEAD` on main, its hash kept as the start; make `.tmp/punch-variations/`. Nothing else heavy runs; port 5180 is left alone. No code, record, network, drill, protocol, judge or mind changes in this run; nothing is tuned.
4. Play, repeated with the same command until the reading is whole (every case of both subjects present, none faulted), a pass going on from the rows kept: `npm run evaluate -- strike option/straight-punch+straight-punch/from-guard --previous option/straight-punch --protocol fights --workers 30 --budget 45 --write --json .tmp/punch-variations/fights-<pass>.json`. Both subjects play all 144 conditions (Warrior, Rogue and Skeleton; each hand; gaps 3, 4 and 5 m; moments 1 to 8); the printed "condition by condition" table is the paired comparison.
5. The one measure, read from the last pass's `--json` `cases` rows: for each subject, the Warrior's (`model` `man`) trials `landed` of those reached (every `outcome` but `unreached`), and the conditions where the two subjects' outcomes differ, each named with both outcomes. The Rogue's and the Skeleton's counts are given beside it, not judged. Drive's Warrior count is checked against the saved 13 of 42; a difference is reported and traced to the commit that made it, not fixed.
6. The answer is "yes" where `from-guard` lands more of the Warrior's reached trials and an exact two-sided sign test over the Warrior's conditions reached by both where exactly one subject landed gives p below 0.05; "no" where it lands as many or fewer; "not shown" where it lands more but p is 0.05 or above. The printed comparison's figures are over every body's trials and are reported, not used for the answer. The run does not play more cases or other protocols to settle it.
7. Machine time: 288 trials on 30 workers, minutes; the budget is one hour of wall-clock time from the first play. A pass under way at the end finishes, nothing new starts; a reading not whole by then is reported as not whole and is not committed.
8. Committed on main, not pushed, once the reading is whole: `assets/evaluations/strike.json` as `--write` left it, under `npm test` and `npm run build`; its message holds both subjects' Warrior counts, the differing conditions and the printed paired figures. Then, in one prose commit: the punch's notes ([the notes](../plans/2026-11-10-warrior-punch.md#where-it-stands)) get one paragraph under "Where it stands" with the two counts and the command that reads them; this brief is deleted; `node --test tests/documents.test.mjs`, `git diff --check` and the line-ending gate.
9. Not read by this run: the table's cells, the `fight` protocol (its trials are the `fights` gap-4 Warrior trials), the other strikes, profiles, Option Mind's choice between variations, 480 Hz, any reel. A defect met on the way (a trial faulted, a world refused; not a punch that misses, which is the measure) is reported with its cause, not fixed.
10. Stops when 2 is answered or at the budget; no next item. One report: the Warrior's landed of reached for each subject and the answer by 6; the differing conditions; the Rogue's and the Skeleton's counts; drive's count against its saved one; the commits; what is unchecked. `.tmp/punch-variations/` is deleted once the report is written.
