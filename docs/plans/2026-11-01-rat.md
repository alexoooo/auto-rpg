# The rat: Poly Haven's Street Rat as a crypt creature

## Why

The owner (2026-11-01) chose Poly Haven's Street Rat (<https://polyhaven.com/a/street_rat>, Sean
Buckley, CC0) as a new character. It is one realistic mesh of 14,286 triangles with its fur in
the texture (no hair cards, no rig), standing on all fours with its tail out and its mouth shut,
0.148 m from nose to tail tip as published.

The reptile's body is the rat's layout already: a trunk, a two-axis neck to a head, a hinged
lower jaw that bites, four legs of upper, lower and paw, two tail segments
(`assets/reptile/body.json`). What differs is proportion and the legs' direction: a rat's legs go
down under its body, the reptile's sprawl. The quadruped's controller settings are absolute
lengths tuned to the reptile (`REPTILE_CRAWL.stride` 0.035 m, `lift` 0.025 m,
`src/core/reptile/tuning.ts`), so the first rat is built at the reptile's scale, about 0.3 m
high: a terrier-sized crypt rat, the controller and Option Reptile carried over unchanged, Size
for other scales.

## The design

- **One quadruped family, two bodies.** `reptileSpec` reads its body from an argument, with the
  model's name; the reptile passes `assets/reptile/body.json` as now, the rat
  `assets/rat/body.json`, with the same segment and joint names, so the quadruped frame, the
  bite, recovery and Option Reptile read it without change. `BodyModel` gains `rat`; its source
  is its own `SOURCES` entry.
- **The body is fitted to the art**, as the skeleton's is (`docs/art/skeleton.md`): joint
  centres where the mesh's limbs bend, segment shapes on the mesh's parts, mass from the mesh's
  volume at the scale chosen. What the mesh does not give (joint ranges, torques, speeds) is the
  reptile's, sourced as an estimate as the reptile's are.
- **The art rides the segments in rigid pieces**, as the skeleton's: a Blender script takes the
  published glTF, scales it to the body, gives each triangle to one segment, cuts the lower jaw
  from the head, and exports one mesh per segment with its textures. The art collides with
  nothing and decides nothing. Pieces part at the joints when a limb swings; the skeleton shows
  this reads.
- **Not in this plan:** a rat-sized rat (the controller's lengths scaled with the body), a third
  tail segment, a mouth interior and teeth in the art, swarms.

## Steps

1. **The quadruped family takes a body file.** `reptileSpec(data, model)`; `FAMILIES`,
   `BODY_MODELS`, `MODEL_DISPLAY` and Option Reptile's preset (`models`) list `rat`, first on a
   copy of the reptile's body. Check: the gate; `node scripts/fingerprint.mjs` unchanged for the
   reptile.
2. **The art in the pipeline.** `scripts/rat/build-assets.py` fetches the pinned glTF (URL and
   SHA-256 in the script), writes `assets/art/environment/rat/rat.glb` and its texture maps (LFS),
   with Poly Haven's CC0 notice; `assets/art/catalog.json` lists them. Check: `npm run
   assets:check`, `npm run build`, the size budget.
3. **The rat's body fitted to the art.** `assets/rat/body.json` from the mesh's landmarks at the
   reptile's height (`scripts/rat/fit.mjs --write` reads the lossless GLB): trunk, head and jaw,
   legs under the body, the tail in two. Check: `bodyIntersections` clean; it stands, walks and
   bites in the Lab and the Arena (a look in a browser).
4. **The skin.** `src/render/rat-skin.ts` carries each piece on its segment from the segments'
   reference frames, its textures through the manifest's resolver; `src/render/dress.ts` lists
   it. Check: the Arena, rat against Warrior and rat against rat, looked at.
5. **The Crypt.** `rat` in `ENCOUNTER_MODELS` (`src/dungeon/encounters.ts`). Check: a Crypt run
   with rats in it, looked at.

## Done when

A rat drawn as the Street Rat stands, walks and bites in the Arena and the Crypt under Option
Reptile, and the owner has watched it.
