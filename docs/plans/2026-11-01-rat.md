# The rat: a fast quadruped drawn as Poly Haven's Street Rat

## Why

The owner chose Poly Haven's Street Rat (<https://polyhaven.com/a/street_rat>, Sean
Buckley, CC0) as a new character, and set the quadrupeds' travel at **1.0 m/s or more**: nothing
is to be slower. The owner watches each stage in the Arena or the Lab.

The rat is a second body of the quadruped family, built by the same spec builder and driven by the
same quadruped control, locomotion, bite and righting parts; its legs stand under the body where
the reptile's sprawl. What is built (the rat in the game and its art, the bite and lunge, fast
legs, the trot at speed, righting by recipe) is in [the reptile's reference](../reference/reptile.md).

## What is left

1. **The reptile at 1.0 m/s.** The rat trots at 1.08 m/s, upright at 8 headings and through half
   turns; the grass lizard reaches .6 m/s straight and falls in turns, its paws reaching .08 m
   fore and aft. One set of trot settings serves both bodies, with no per-body tuning in a game
   preset. Check: both bodies upright at 1.0 m/s at 8 headings and through half turns, 18 s each;
   reptile and rat bouts by `npm run match`; [the trot at
   speed](../reference/reptile.md#the-trot-at-speed) rewritten; the Arena, looked at, and the link
   to the owner.
2. **Righting on Option Mind's loop.** The rat rights itself on the Node stand, but under Option
   Mind its recipe's last pose, propped on folded legs, reads down, and driving the legs from it
   rolls the rat onto its back; knocked over in a fight it can be left crouched, its head on its
   front legs, until a retry. The reptile on a recipe of its own would retire the routes
   (`rightingByRoutes`). Check: the rat and the reptile, from side and back placements, right
   themselves and trot on under Option Mind; a fall in the Arena, looked at.
3. **The Crypt.** `rat` in `ENCOUNTER_MODELS` (`src/game/dungeon/encounters.ts`): every generated
   layout changes; [encounters](../reference/reptile.md#encounters), the README and the
   architecture's bodies list say so. Check: the fingerprint's moved Crypt cases named; a Crypt
   run with rats in it, looked at.

Not in this plan: a rat-sized rat, a spine that bends (a bound or gallop), a third tail segment, a
mouth interior and teeth in the art, swarms.

## Done when

The reptile and the rat trot at 1.0 m/s or more and right themselves under Option Mind; a rat
drawn as the Street Rat stands, trots, turns and bites in the Arena and the Crypt; and the owner
has watched it.
