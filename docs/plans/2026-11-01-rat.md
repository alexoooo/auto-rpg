# The rat: a fast quadruped drawn as Poly Haven's Street Rat

## Why

The owner chose Poly Haven's Street Rat (<https://polyhaven.com/a/street_rat>, Sean
Buckley, CC0) as a new character, and set the quadrupeds' travel at **1.0 m/s or more**: the
reptile's 0.3 m/s is too slow, and nothing is to be slower. The owner watches each stage in the
Arena or the Lab.

The rat is a second body of the quadruped family: the reptile's segment and joint names (trunk,
neck, head, hinged jaw, four legs of upper, lower and paw, two tail segments), its spec builder,
its physique and the quadruped control, locomotion, bite and righting parts, which fit a body by its shape, not its name. What differs
is the legs: under the body, knees and elbows bending front to back, where the reptile's sprawl.

What a rat-legged copy of the reptile's body showed on the Node stand (Rapier, 120 Hz, 8 kg, the
reptile's torques, no command delay; `d3a04559` and the runs before it):

- **It stands, and trots where the trot is slow and the legs are bent.** Built crouched (about
  1.5 rad at elbow and knee), it trots 0.15 m/s at 8 headings; built at about 1.0 rad, a leg
  nearly straightens at the end of its stance and its paw drags without lifting.
- **A dragged paw held the trot's step for up to 3 s** and tipped the body; the trot now lands a
  pair once its paws bear (`d3a04559`).
- **The legs' joint speed caps both bodies near 0.4 m/s**: the reptile's leg joints run to
  6 rad/s unloaded (`assets/reptile/body.json`). At 15 to 30 rad/s, both trot 0.5 m/s straight with
  every paw lifting.
- **At 1.0 m/s both tip within about a second**, paws lifting: the trot places each paw at its
  footprint plus the velocity times a lead, whatever the body's drift, and nothing catches a fall.
  The reptile at twice its strength, swing .12 s and lead .12 s held 0.88 m/s for 9 s.
- **Righting by routes (`rightingByRoutes`, `src/skills/recovery/righting/righting.ts`) is written for
  sprawled legs**: it drives the hip's axis 2 and the knee's axis 0 to angles from the sprawled
  reference pose, which the rat's ranges do not hold, so the rat is refused it.

## The design

- **One quadruped family, two bodies.** `reptileSpec(data, model, source)` builds either;
  `BodyModel` gains `rat`, its anatomy its own `SOURCES` entry.
- **The rat at the reptile's height, 0.325 m**: the art ×9.6, 1.42 m nose to tail, its body fitted
  to the art (`scripts/rat/fit.mjs`). **8 kg**, the
  reptile's mass (owner's decision, recorded), not the mesh's volume (about 27 kg at water's
  density), so one set of torques and controller settings serves both.
- **Fast legs for both bodies**: leg joint speed and strength set for the 1.0 m/s goal, sourced as
  the owner's decision, with their before/after table.
- **A trot that keeps its balance at speed**, in the toolkit, for both bodies: each landing placed
  to correct the body's drift (velocity error and tilt), a step cycle short enough for the legs'
  reach. One set of settings for both bodies, with no per-body tuning in a game preset.
- **Read at the game's command delay of one step** (`COMMAND_DELAY`): the trot's placements are
  decided a step late and carried out on the body as it is. The findings above were read with none.
- **Recovery for legs under the body**: righting by a recipe of poses found offline for each body
  (`rightingByRecipe`, `scripts/skills/recovery/righting/righting.mjs`), a body with none righting by the
  reptile's routes; the rat's hips reach ±1.6 rad to lever it off its side.
- **The art is one mesh skinned to the segments** ([docs/art/rat.md](../art/rat.md)), so the fur
  bends at the joints; the body is fitted to the art. The art collides with nothing and decides
  nothing.
- **Not in this plan:** a rat-sized rat, a spine that bends (a bound or gallop), a third tail
  segment, a mouth interior and teeth in the art, swarms.

## Steps

What is left of them follows [Option Mind general](2026-11-03-option-mind-general.md), which puts
the quadrupeds on Option Mind's one loop.

1. **The rat in the game, drawn as its shapes.** `reptileSpec(data, model, source)`; `rat` in
   `BODY_MODELS`, `FAMILIES`, `MODEL_DISPLAY`, `DEFAULT_MINDS`, `CRYPT_MODELS` and `ART`. Check:
   the gate; the fingerprint, no case moved; the Arena, looked at.
2. **The art, on a body fitted to it.** `scripts/rat/fit.mjs --write` writes `assets/rat/body.json`
   from the art's landmarks (`scripts/rat/landmarks.mjs`); `scripts/rat/build-assets.mjs` fetches
   the pinned files and writes `assets/art/environment/rat/` (the mesh simplified and skinned, the
   1k maps); `src/app/browser/render/skinned-art.ts` draws it; the catalogue lists it, the art budget raised for
   it; `docs/art/rat.md`. Check: `bodyIntersections` clean; the gate, `npm run build`; the fitted
   body standing and trotting at .15 m/s at 8 headings on the Node stand; rat against the Warrior
   in the Arena, looked at, and the link to the owner.
3. **It bites the Warrior.** The head carried raised so the open jaw clears the floor
   (`HEAD_RAISE`); the stance settled over the paws wherever the legs meet the trunk
   (`overPaws`); the bite's lean measured from where its chamber began; a crawl's paw landed once
   it bears. Check: the gate, `npm run build`; the rat's bouts against the Warrior with a sword,
   before and after ([the rat](../reference/reptile.md#the-rat)); the fingerprint's moved cases
   named; the Arena, looked at.
4. **Fast legs.** Leg joints of both bodies at 20 rad/s (the reptile's body file, the rat's
   fitted from it), sourced as the owner's decision; strength unchanged (twice it moved no
   reading); the trot's speed .5 m/s. Check: the gate, `npm run build`; the before/after table
   on the Node stand at 8 headings and the bouts against the Warrior
   ([fast legs](../reference/reptile.md#fast-legs)); the fingerprint's moved cases named.
5. **The lunge.** The bite aims its tooth's mid-stroke point at the target, launches on an open
   jaw with the mouth near its aim, and drives the mouth onto the aim through the stroke; the rat's
   neck keeps the reptile's margin over its longer head (`scripts/rat/fit.mjs`). Check: the gate;
   the close-range drill and the fights, before and after
   ([the lunge](../reference/reptile.md#the-lunge)); the fingerprint's moved cases named.
6. **A trot that keeps its balance at 1.0 m/s.** Drift-correcting placement and a shorter cycle in
   `src/skills/locomotion/quadruped/trot.ts`; the trot's speed setting 1.0 m/s or more; turns that keep up with
   it: paws placed for the heading the trunk will have, the trunk's heading never far ahead of
   its own (at .25 rad/s a half turn takes 12 s, and a faster one tips the rat). Check: both bodies upright at 1.0 m/s at 8 headings
   and through half turns, 18 s each; reptile and rat bouts by `npm run match`;
   `docs/reference/reptile.md`'s controller table and travel record rewritten; the Arena, looked
   at, and the link to the owner. Built: the rat at 1.08 m/s, upright at 8 headings and through
   half turns, on legs raised .08 m, twice as strong at 40 rad/s, its weight carried forward
   ([the trot at speed](../reference/reptile.md#the-trot-at-speed)). Left: the grass lizard, at
   .6 m/s straight and falling in turns, its paws reaching .08 m fore and aft; the Arena looked
   at.
7. **Recovery for legs under the body.** Check: the rat, laid on its side and on its back on the
   Node stand, rights itself and trots on; the reptile's recovery unchanged (fingerprint); a fall
   in the Arena, looked at. Built: 21 of 21 unseen starts on the stand; fighting, under half the
   time down or righting that the routes left it. Left: on Option Mind's one loop the recipe's
   last pose, propped on folded legs, reads down, and driving the legs from it rolls the rat onto
   its back; knocked over in a fight it can be left crouched, its head on its front legs, until a
   retry; the reptile on a recipe of its own, which would retire the routes.
8. **The Crypt.** `rat` in `ENCOUNTER_MODELS` (`src/game/dungeon/encounters.ts`): every generated
   layout changes; `docs/reference/reptile.md#encounters`, the README and the architecture's
   bodies list say so. Check: the fingerprint's moved Crypt cases named; a Crypt run with rats in
   it, looked at.

## Done when

The reptile and the rat trot at 1.0 m/s or more and right themselves; a rat drawn as the Street
Rat stands, trots, turns and bites in the Arena and the Crypt under Option Mind; and the owner
has watched it.
