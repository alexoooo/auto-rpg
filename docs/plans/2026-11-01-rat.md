# The rat: a fast quadruped drawn as Poly Haven's Street Rat

## Why

The owner (2026-11-01) chose Poly Haven's Street Rat (<https://polyhaven.com/a/street_rat>, Sean
Buckley, CC0) as a new character, and set the quadrupeds' travel at **1.0 m/s or more**: the
reptile's 0.3 m/s is too slow, and nothing is to be slower. The owner watches each stage in the
Arena or the Lab.

The rat is a second body of the quadruped family: the reptile's segment and joint names (trunk,
neck, head, hinged jaw, four legs of upper, lower and paw, two tail segments), its spec builder,
its physique and Option Reptile's parts, which fit a body by its shape, not its name. What differs
is the legs: under the body, knees and elbows bending front to back, where the reptile's sprawl.

What a rat-legged copy of the reptile's body showed on the Node stand (Rapier, 120 Hz, 8 kg, the
reptile's torques; `d3a04559` and the runs before it):

- **It stands, and trots where the trot is slow and the legs are bent.** Built crouched (about
  1.5 rad at elbow and knee), it trots 0.15 m/s at 8 headings; built at about 1.0 rad, a leg
  nearly straightens at the end of its stance and its paw drags without lifting.
- **A dragged paw held the trot's step for up to 3 s** and tipped the body; the trot now lands a
  pair once its paws bear (`d3a04559`).
- **The legs' joint speed caps both bodies near 0.4 m/s**: the reptile's leg joints run to
  6 rad/s unloaded (`assets/reptile/body.json`). At 15 to 30 rad/s, both trot 0.5 m/s straight with
  every paw lifting.
- **At 1.0 m/s both tip within about a second**, paws lifting: the trot places each paw at its
  footprint plus the velocity times a lead, whatever the body's drift, and nothing catches a fall.
  The reptile at twice its strength, swing .12 s and lead .12 s held 0.88 m/s for 9 s.
- **Recovery (`src/core/reptile/recover.ts`) is written for sprawled legs**: it drives the hip's
  axis 2 and the knee's axis 0 to angles from the sprawled reference pose.

The published glTF: one mesh, 57,216 triangles, 31,937 vertices, no skin, rig or animation;
0.148 m nose to tail, 0.034 m high, 0.032 m wide; open (6,504 boundary edges); textures at 1k
to 8k (`diff`, `nor_gl`, `arm`).

## The design

- **One quadruped family, two bodies.** `reptileSpec(data, model, source)` builds either;
  `BodyModel` gains `rat`, its anatomy its own `SOURCES` entry.
- **The rat at the reptile's height, 0.325 m**: the art ×9.6, 1.42 m nose to tail. **8 kg**, the
  reptile's mass (owner's decision, recorded), not the mesh's volume (about 27 kg at water's
  density), so one set of torques and controller settings serves both.
- **Fast legs for both bodies**: leg joint speed and strength set for the 1.0 m/s goal, sourced as
  the owner's decision, with their before/after table.
- **A trot that keeps its balance at speed**, in the toolkit, for both bodies: each landing placed
  to correct the body's drift (velocity error and tilt), a step cycle short enough for the legs'
  reach. One set of settings for both bodies, with no per-body tuning in a game preset.
- **Recovery for legs under the body**: righting reads the leg layout from the spec, or a second
  righting part fits bodies whose legs hang under them.
- **The art rides the segments in rigid pieces**, as the skeleton's (`docs/art/skeleton.md`): the
  body is fitted to the art first, then the mesh is cut, a triangle to a segment, the lower jaw
  from the head. The art collides with nothing and decides nothing.
- **Not in this plan:** a rat-sized rat, a spine that bends (a bound or gallop), a third tail
  segment, a mouth interior and teeth in the art, swarms.

## Steps

1. **The rat in the game, drawn as its shapes.** `reptileSpec(data, model, source)`;
   `assets/rat/body.json`, the hand-built crouched body (legs under the trunk, elbows and knees
   about 1.5 rad, knee axes along x); `rat` in `BODY_MODELS`, `FAMILIES`, `MODEL_DISPLAY`,
   `DEFAULT_MINDS` (`src/entrants/game.ts`), `CRYPT_MODELS` (`src/dungeon/actors.ts`, its footprint
   read from its own spec), `ART` (`src/render/dress.ts`, the reptile's shells in the rat's tint)
   and Option Reptile's preset `models`. Check: the gate; the fingerprint, no case moved; the rat
   against the Warrior and against the reptile in the Arena, looked at, and the link to the owner.
2. **Fast legs.** Leg joint speed and strength of both bodies set for the goal (start at 15 to
   30 rad/s), sourced as the owner's decision. Check: the gate; a before/after table on the Node
   stand at 8 headings at 0.3 and 0.5 m/s for both bodies, and reptile bouts by `npm run match`;
   the fingerprint's moved cases named.
3. **A trot that keeps its balance at 1.0 m/s.** Drift-correcting placement and a shorter cycle in
   `src/core/reptile/trot.ts`; the trot's speed setting 1.0 m/s or more. Check: both bodies upright
   at 1.0 m/s at 8 headings and through half turns, 18 s each; reptile and rat bouts by
   `npm run match`; `docs/reference/reptile.md`'s controller table and travel record rewritten;
   the Arena, looked at, and the link to the owner.
4. **Recovery for legs under the body.** Check: the rat, laid on its side and on its back on the
   Node stand, rights itself and trots on; the reptile's recovery unchanged (fingerprint); a fall
   in the Arena, looked at.
5. **The art in the pipeline.** `scripts/rat/build-assets.py` fetches the pinned 2k glTF (every
   file's URL and SHA-256 in the script), scales it ×9.6 to the body's frame and writes the whole
   mesh to `assets/art/environment/rat/rat.glb` with its textures (LFS), with Poly Haven's CC0
   notice; `assets/art/catalog.json` lists them. Check: `npm run assets:check`, `npm run build`, the
   size budget.
6. **The body fitted to the art.** `scripts/rat/fit.mjs --write` measures the scaled mesh: joint
   centres where its limbs bend, segment shapes on its parts, the tail in two; `assets/rat/body.json`
   rewritten from it. Where the art's legs are straighter than the trot needs, the body keeps the
   crouch and the pieces are posed to it. Check: `bodyIntersections` clean; steps 3 and 4's checks
   again on the fitted body.
7. **The skin.** `build-assets.py` cuts the mesh by the fitted body, one piece a segment;
   `src/render/rat-skin.ts` carries each piece on its segment from the segments' reference frames,
   its textures through the manifest's resolver; `ART` dresses the rat with it. `docs/art/rat.md`:
   source, licence, rebuild. Check: the gate, `npm run build`; rat against Warrior and rat against
   rat in the Arena, looked at.
8. **The Crypt.** `rat` in `ENCOUNTER_MODELS` (`src/dungeon/encounters.ts`): every generated
   layout changes; `docs/reference/reptile.md#encounters`, the README and the architecture's
   bodies list say so. Check: the fingerprint's moved Crypt cases named; a Crypt run with rats in
   it, looked at.

## Done when

The reptile and the rat trot at 1.0 m/s or more and right themselves; a rat drawn as the Street
Rat stands, trots, turns and bites in the Arena and the Crypt under Option Reptile; and the owner
has watched it.
