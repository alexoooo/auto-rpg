# Options: every skill in one shape

## Why

What tactics ask and what a skill does are tangled, so no skill can be measured alone and tactics
cannot know beforehand what a skill will do:

- the recipe strike brings the body to its range (`BlowAttack`, `src/core/mind/intent.ts`), though
  the action contract says an executor never closes distance;
- a blow's path family, `armExtension` and `lower` let tactics choose a technique no one has
  measured;
- locomotion takes a velocity and a facing, so spacing is written as a velocity;
- the rise drives the muscles through its own motor control (`step-up`,
  `src/core/mind/ground/step-up.ts`), beside the skill set: a second control stack.

Every skill takes one shape, so a drill, a profile and a tactic read each one the same way. The
[Drills](2026-10-24-drills.md) plan measures skills in this shape, and its profiles are what
tactics choose by.

## The shape of a skill

Every skill, of the legs, a hand or the trunk, has the same four parts:

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| Name and goal | what it does, at what: `jab(head)`, `keep-range(foe, 0.8 m)`, `cover(head)`, `walk(velocity, facing)` |
| Entry condition | where it can start, in sensed terms: pose class, a few motion features (centre-of-mass velocity, spin, which parts bear), range, bearing, target speed; a predicate its module exports |
| Resources | what it holds while it runs (the legs, a hand, the trunk), as the action contract's reservations hold them |
| Profile | how it does over its entry condition, measured on its drill ([Drills](2026-10-24-drills.md)) |

- **A skill does its own motion and nothing else.** Outside its entry condition it refuses and says
  why. It may shift its weight and place its feet as part of its motion; it never walks to its
  range. Reaching a range is a footwork skill.
- **A technique is a skill.** Jab, cross, hook and low jab are names, each with its own profile, not
  one skill given a path family, an arm extension and a crouch. A continuous goal parameter stays
  only where the profile covers it.
- **Tactics choose skills, never motion.** The intent names a skill and its goal for each resource,
  and what to interrupt: `legs: keep-range(foe, 0.8)`, `right: jab(head)`, `left: cover(head)`.
  A person's keys choose `walk` as an AI would. Tactics read entry conditions and profiles from
  their senses alone; when no skill fits, they choose one that brings another into its entry
  condition.

It is the options framework of reinforcement learning (Sutton, Precup and Singh, 1999): a skill's
entry condition, motion and ending are an option's initiation set, policy and termination, and its
profile is the option's measured model.

### Handing over: at rest or in motion

An entry condition covers motion as well as pose, so one skill hands the body to the next wherever
its ending lies inside the next one's entry condition, still or moving: the sequential composition
of funnels (Burridge, Rizzi and Koditschek, 1999).

| Handover | Where | Gains | Costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| At rest | a stable posture: hands and knees, kneeling, the guard | robust; easy to measure; any skill can start there | slow: the body brakes, then speeds up again |
| In motion | a moving state: rolling onto the side, the hips already turning | fast: momentum carries across | a narrow entry, its timing tight |

- **Split only where a describable, reachable state lies between.** A handover region that is tiny
  and timing-critical makes the two one skill: a punch (the hips and trunk start before the elbow),
  a kip-up, a sit-up tucking onto the feet.
- **A chain used together with tight timing is a skill of its own**, drilled and profiled as
  itself: a step-in jab, with a longer entry range. Its parts stay for when it cannot be used.
- **Stable postures are safe states**: the places any skill falls back to. The fast path is a
  moving skill with a narrow entry; failed or disturbed, the body ends in a safe state, and a
  slower skill goes on from there.
- **Momentum is in flight in the commitment.** The action contract's prepare, commit and release
  stay; an interrupt costs most in the commit, and a skill declares the region it hands over in
  at its release (its exit), so a chain is checked as one skill's exit inside the next one's entry.

## Where it starts

The action contract (`src/core/skills/action.ts`; [skills](../architecture.md#skills)): a request
by id and name, readiness, reservations, prepare, commit and release, a bounded ledger of endings
(`Ended`), interrupts. The intent (`Intent`) carries velocities, a facing, covers and action
requests; the skills report through `SkillReport`; action windows
(`src/core/mind/action-window.ts`) say where some actions may start. Locomotion and the guard are a continuous base, not actions.

## Steps

### 1. Strikes in the shape

The action contract gains the entry condition over pose and motion features, the refusal outside
it with its reason, and the exit declared at release. Each punch and kick is a named skill: jab,
cross, hook, front kick and the other variants the path family, the arm extension and `lower`
choose today. The recipe strike stops bringing the body to its range. The tactics parts that set
those fields choose names instead. Locomotion and the guard stay as they are until step 2.

Check: `npm run check`, `npm test`, `npm run build`; a toolkit change, read with the fingerprint
against the commit before, its message naming the moved cases; in the Lab's Attack scenario a
strike asked outside its entry condition is refused with its reason, and one inside moves as
before; the owner watches the Lab and an Arena bout.

### 2. Footwork, the guard and the intent

The legs' skills by name: `walk(velocity, facing)` and `go-to(place)` for what `move`,
`movement` and `face` give now, and `keep-range`, `step-in`, `step-out` and `circle` with goals
relative to a target; each hand's guard is `cover(region)`. The intent becomes one skill choice
per resource and its interrupts. Every tactics part, the orders a person gives (`orderedIntent`)
and the person's keys write that form; the closed orders vocabulary opens with it.

Check: as step 1; a person drives with the keys and orders in the Arena and the Crypt as before;
the owner watches.

### 3. The other fighting skills

Blade cuts and thrusts, held-item recipes, low support and combinations, each a named skill in
the shape; the step-in jab as the first chain that is a skill of its own.

Check: as step 1.

## Done when

The fighting skills (strikes, kicks, blade actions, held-item recipes, low support, combinations,
the legs' skills, the guard) have the shape; the intent is one skill choice per resource and its
interrupts; every tactics part, a person's keys and orders write it. The rise joins the shape with
its rework ([roadmap](../roadmap.md#order)).
