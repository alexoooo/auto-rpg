# On the ground: positions, techniques, and three sub-minds

The owner asked for this plan on 2026-10-13. A body that is down gets a new design, built beside the
two sub-minds that exist, "Rise, then steady" (`support-recovery`) and "Research rise"
(`research-rise`). It neither changes nor replaces them, and keeps nothing of theirs for
compatibility.

**The aims:**

- **A graph of positions and techniques.** Positions are where a body is (on its back, on all
  fours, kneeling, standing). Techniques are the ways from one position to another. Several
  techniques may join the same two positions, and several paths lead to standing, so the player
  meaningfully chooses which a fighter uses. The shape is the one in which characters later unlock
  techniques.
- **Three sub-minds, each with one job,** in the "When down" list the player already ranks
  (`FighterConfig.subs`):
  - **Fight on the ground** (`ground-fight`): while down with a foe in reach, keep the feet toward
    it, cover, and kick.
  - **Get away** (`get-away`): while lying with a foe near, roll away from it.
  - **Stand up** (`stand-up`): rise to the feet by the graph, and nothing else.
- **Rising works**: quickly, without fixed waits or blind retries, from however the body came to
  lie. The bar is the owner's gameplay target in `docs/reference/rising.md#dynamic-rise`:
  - a median verified recovery within 4 s;
  - at least 15 of 16 directional falls in each body and loadout group;
  - 61 of 64 within 6 s.
- **The Lab gets an "On the ground" scenario** that exercises all three, with a research bed and a
  search behind it.

**Out of scope:**

- `stand-up-acceptance-v1`, its criteria, cases and baseline: untouched (part I).
- The two existing sub-minds and the Lab's Get-up experiment: left as they are.
- The reptile's recovery.
- Character progression. Unlocking needs it, and it does not exist yet (part B).

## Where things stand

- **Rise, then steady** (`supportRecovery`, `src/core/mind/rise/support-recovery.ts`) plays the
  staged recipe (`RISE`, `src/core/mind/rise/stages.ts`; `stagedRise`, `staged.ts`).
  - It goes limp until still before every attempt and after every roll, with no cap.
  - Its pose stages advance on time, whatever pose was reached.
  - A failed bearing stage, or the trunk down again, sends it limp and back to the first stage.
  - From the back it always rolls over the right side, then settles limp and reads the lie again.
  - Stabilising for 6 s without readiness restarts the whole rise, even on a standing body.
  - The step and the lunge lead with the left foot only. Their postures carry one-sided channels
    such as `lumbar lateral flexion right`.
- **Its results** (`docs/roadmap.md`, Rising; `docs/reference/rising.md`, Staged):
  - The Warrior rises from 11 of 16 shoves empty-handed and 9 of 14 with the club.
  - The Rogue rises from 2 of 32.
  - Passing rises take 22 to 29 s.
- **The hard transition.** `docs/analysis/2026-10-11-stand-up-planning.md` records searches,
  references, learning and feedback that all fail at the same transition: from hands and knees to
  a loaded, flat foot under the centre of mass.
- **Locomotion's turnaround** came from four things:
  - phases ended by events, not timers;
  - honest scoring (`WENT`);
  - named gaits in a registry (`GAITS`, `src/core/skills/gaits.ts`), chosen by a rule list
    (`MoveConfig`, `src/core/mind/config.ts`; `move`, `src/core/skills/move.ts`), changed only at
    safe points;
  - CMA-ES on a seeded battery with held-out seeds (`research/locomotion-search.mjs`).
- **Sub-minds.**
  - `hosting` (`src/core/mind/sub-mind.ts`) asks each sub-mind `wants` every step. The first that
    wants the body has it at once, even from a lower-ranked sub-mind in the middle of what it does.
  - `hosting` captures each sub-mind's `state` once, when it is made.
- **`view.down`.** While a sub-mind has the body, its host asks no height, so `view.down` reads
  against full standing height (`commandMind`, `src/core/body.ts`; the human's `fallen` bar is
  0.25 m).
- **Fighting a body that is down** exists only from standing: `support-fold`, `groundCombat`
  (`src/core/mind/ground-combat.ts`, `docs/reference/ground-combat.md`). Nothing fights from the
  ground.
- **Who rises today:**
  - The Lab's Script and Guard presets lie still once down (`RECIPE_FIGHTER.subs` is `[lie]`), and
    so does `GAME_FIGHTER`, the Arena's default mind (`src/core/models.ts`).
  - Classic, Puncher, Scrapper and the behaviours presets use `support-recovery`.
  - In the Crypt a fall ends a humanoid's fight (`HUMANOID.fallEndsFight`,
    `src/dungeon/actors.ts`), and its body goes limp.

## What it draws on

- **Controllers with contracts and a supervisor.** Faloutsos, van de Panne and Terzopoulos,
  "Composable Controllers for Physics-Based Character Animation", SIGGRAPH 2001. Each controller
  declares where it can start, where it delivers, and a progress check that detects its own
  failure. A supervisor chooses again from the state as it is. Their 2D figure had several get-ups
  (prone, supine, kip) between shared postures: this plan's graph.
- **Sequential composition.** Burridge, Rizzi and Koditschek, IJRR 1999. A chain holds when each
  controller's outlet lies inside the next one's inlet. That is measurable by sampling
  (part G's outlet column).
- **Canonical positions first.** Kanehiro et al., ICRA 2007; He et al., "HumanUP", 2025; Tao et
  al., "Learning to Get Up", SIGGRAPH 2022.
- **Keyframes with feedback, hard windows and capped waits.** Reichenberg and Laue, "Stand Up,
  NAO!", 2025, which reaches 97 to 98 % in competition. Each phase also tracks a centre-of-mass
  reference recorded from good runs.
- **Phases ended by state, not time.** Kuniyoshi et al., IJHR 2004. The timing of energy input at
  a few moments separated successes from failures; overall tracking error did not.
- **Discover, then constrain.** Tao et al. find a strategy at full strength, then lower the limits
  stage by stage.
- **How people get up.**
  - VanSant, Phys Ther 1988: the commonest rise (supine, sitting, squatting, standing) is used by
    only a quarter of young adults. People differ, which is what a graph with several paths gives.
  - Schwickert et al., Sensors 2016: median 4.2 s for adults of 20 to 50.
- **Standing in base.** The martial artist's technical stand-up, `post-up` below: a hand and the
  opposite foot hold a tripod while the free leg swings back under the body.

## A. Positions

**A position is read once a step, for every sub-mind,** where `down` is read: in `commandMind`'s
`look` (`src/core/body.ts`), shown on `BodyView` as `position`. A sub-mind reads whether its body
is down from its host's view and never by a bar of its own, and the same holds for where it is.

- **When it is read.** Only while the body is down or a sub-mind has it, since reading contacts
  allocates (`contactsOf`, `src/core/engine/rapier.ts`). Otherwise `position` is `null`.
- **Knowing a sub-mind has the body.** `commandMind` keeps a flag in its `state` (so a fork keeps
  it), set in `release` and cleared in `resume`.
  - `hosting` calls `look` before it hands over, so on a takeover step the flag is still clear.
  - The reading starts that step anyway, because a takeover follows `down`, which `look` has just
    read.
- **The first reading.** When reading starts, the reader's settle memory clears. Until the
  hysteresis has a position, `position` is the raw reading.
- **The module.** The reader is `src/core/mind/ground/position.ts`.

```ts
/** Where a body is: lying four ways, on the way up, standing, or none of these. */
export type Position =
  | "back" | "front" | "left" | "right"
  | "sitting" | "fours" | "kneeling" | "half-kneel" | "crouch" | "standing"
  | "between";

/** Where a body is this step: plain data, written in place. */
export interface PositionReading {
  position: Position;
  /** How the pelvis lies (`lieOf`, `src/core/mind/rise/staged.ts`). */
  lie: Lie;
  /** The centre of mass over the lowest point, as a part of standing height. */
  height: number;
  /** The centre of mass's speed, m/s. */
  speed: number;
  /** Which segments bear on the ground this step, by `BodySpec.segments` order: 1 bears. */
  readonly bears: Uint8Array;
  /** For a half-kneel, the side whose foot is planted; for a lie on a side, the side down. */
  side: Side | null;
}
```

**What bears.** A segment bears when one pair of one of its contacts with a fixed collider has all
of these:

- the step pushed on it (impulse above 0);
- its normal's upward part (`-normal[1]`, the normal pointing from the body into the ground, as
  `groundContact` reads it, `src/core/control/support.ts`) is at least `GROUND_UP`. That is a
  cosine of 0.7, about 46° from vertical, so the Arena's walls are not ground;
- the pair's own shape (`ContactPair.mine`) is not one of a held item's shapes.

It is tested pair by pair, not on the whole `Contact`, whose impulse and normal sum every pair, a
held item's included. A held item is shapes of the hand's body, filtered as
`src/core/control/effector-feedback.ts` filters them, so a club's head on the floor is not the
hand bearing.

**The positions,** tested in this order; the first that holds wins. "Trunk" is `lowerTrunk`,
`middleTrunk` and `upperTrunk`; "hand" is a hand or forearm.

| Position | Must bear | May bear | Must not bear | Also |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| standing | both feet | | everything else | height at least `STANDING`, 0.85 |
| crouch | both feet | hands | shanks, trunk | |
| half-kneel | one shank, the other side's foot | that shank's foot, hands | the other shank, trunk | |
| kneeling | both shanks | feet | hands, trunk | trunk upright within 0.6 rad |
| fours | both shanks, a hand on each side | feet | trunk | |
| sitting | `lowerTrunk` | feet, hands | `middleTrunk`, `upperTrunk` | `upperTrunk` over `lowerTrunk` by 0.15 m |
| back, front, left, right | a trunk segment | anything | | by `lieOf` |
| between | anything else | | | |

- **Hysteresis.** A position changes only after it has read the same for `SETTLE_STEPS`, 6 steps
  (0.05 s).
- **Tuning.** The thresholds are the module's own constants, tuned freely: it is a mind's module,
  not the body's.
- **The core's rules apply.** The reader's work arrays are made once, it does no trig of its own
  (`src/core/math/real.ts`, `turn.ts`), and its memory is plain data in one `state`.

**Tests:** `tests/ground-position.test.mjs`, fast. Every reading is taken after the world has
stepped, since contacts are the last solver step's.

- Each body (`HUMANOID_MODELS`) is built flat on its back and on its front: guard posture, the
  root turned, its lowest point 0.02 m over the ground. After 0.25 s it reads that lie.
- Each is built on each side, and read after 0.1 s, before it can roll.
- The Warrior is built and held in `fours`, `half-kneel` and `squat`
  (`assets/research/posture-holds.json`, as `createPostureHoldProbe` builds them,
  `src/core/tasks/posture-hold.ts`). After 0.25 s it reads fours, half-kneel (left foot planted)
  and crouch.
- Each body stands in its guard under the stance for 1 s, and the reader, called directly (the
  view reads nothing while the body is up), reads standing. It still does with the club's head
  resting on the floor.
- A body lying against a wall reads its lie, not `between`, and the wall bears nothing.

## B. Techniques: the edges of the graph

A **technique** is one way from some positions to others, with a contract. The contract and the
registry are in `src/core/mind/ground/techniques.ts`, and each technique is in a file of its own
beside it. ("Move" is already Locomotion's rule list, so these are techniques.)

```ts
/** How a technique ended. */
export type TechniqueEnd = "done" | "stalled" | "failed" | "timed-out" | "interrupted";

/** A technique as it runs: made once, with the sub-mind; its memory is plain data in `state`. */
export interface TechniqueRun {
  readonly state: object;
  /** The phase it is in, by name, for the readout. */
  readonly phase: string;
  /** How far it has come, 0 to 1, by its own measure. */
  readonly progress: number;
  /** Start from the body as it is. `side` is a roll's side over, or the leg that leads. */
  begin(side: Side): void;
  /** Drive the body this step; null while it runs, how it ended once it has. */
  step(reading: PositionReading, senses: Senses, dt: number): TechniqueEnd | null;
  /** The body is taken from it: it ends `interrupted`. */
  end(): void;
}

export interface TechniqueSpec {
  readonly label: string;
  readonly stage: "game" | "experimental";
  /** The sides it is made from: a technique written for one leg is offered on that side alone. */
  readonly sides: readonly Side[];
  /** Where it delivers the body from `from`, over or leading with `side`; null where it does not start from `from`. */
  to(from: Position, side: Side): Position | null;
  /** Whether a body of `spec` can make it. */
  fits(spec: BodySpec): boolean;
  make(own: OwnBody, view: BodyView, world: World): TechniqueRun;
}

export const TECHNIQUES: Readonly<Record<TechniqueName, TechniqueSpec>>;
```

**Every sub-mind makes its runs once, when it is made,** one per technique it may use.

- Its `state` holds every run's state, `state.runs`, by name, and the name of the running one.
- `hosting` captures a sub-mind's `state` once, and a run made later would not exist in a fresh
  bout that loads a save (`loadState`, `src/core/state.ts`). Either way a fork would not play the
  same.

**What every technique does:**

- **Phases end on events, never on time alone.** Examples: a contact gained or lost, an angle
  passed, the centre of mass over the support, a height reached. Each phase has a cap; past it the
  technique ends `timed-out`.
- **Windows.** Each phase declares an admissible window: the trunk's tilt within a band and the
  height over a floor. Leaving it ends the technique `failed` at once.
- **Progress.** If `progress` gains less than `STALL.gain`, 0.05, in `STALL.seconds`, 0.75 s, the
  technique ends `stalled`.
- **No limp settle anywhere.** A body still tumbling is the sub-mind's (part D).
- **Pose phases** use the staged recipe's velocity rule:
  - each freedom asked `(goal - angle) / within`, no faster than `top`;
  - activation 1;
  - `within` 0.2 s and `top` 3 rad/s unless the phase says.

  It is written as the exported `poseDrive` in `techniques.ts`. Goals are the posture less each
  freedom's bind angle, as `stagedRise` converts them (`DofSpec.bind`). So chunk 03's first reading
  changes the supervisor and nothing else.
- **Bearing phases** use the bearing solve (`makeBearing`, `bearLimbs`, `carryRoot`,
  `src/core/control/bearing.ts`) through `riseLimbs` (`src/core/mind/rise/limbs.ts`), which reads
  only `recipe.limbs`. Its parameter narrows to `Pick<Recipe, "limbs">`, and `RiseLimbs` is
  exported (see "What it changes of existing code").
- **Mirroring.** `mirrorPosture` and `mirrorLimbs` (`techniques.ts`):
  - `mirrorPosture` swaps `.left` and `.right` and negates the one-sided channels (`lateral flexion
    right`, `rotation right`).
  - `mirrorLimbs` swaps the bearing phases' limbs' segments and channels (`foot.left` to
    `foot.right`).
  - They land with a test: mirrored twice is the original, and the half-kneel's mirror built on the
    Warrior has its centre of mass mirrored across the body's plane within 1 mm.
  - A technique offers a side only once its mirror passes the bed.
- **No assist** unless the body's balance gives one, as everywhere.

**The techniques,** by the chunk that builds them:

| Technique | From, side: to | Chunk |
|---|---|---|
| `roll` | back: front; front: back; left or right: front or back by `side` | 03 (back, sides), 04 (front) |
| `to-fours` | front: fours | 03 |
| `kneel-up` | fours: kneeling | 03 |
| `step-up` | kneeling: half-kneel | 03 (left lead) |
| `lunge-up` | half-kneel: standing | 03 (left lead) |
| `sit-up` | back: sitting | 06 |
| `sit-to-squat` | sitting: crouch | 06 |
| `post-up` | sitting: standing | 06 |
| `walk-back` | fours: crouch | 06 |
| `crouch-up` | crouch: standing | 06 |

- **The 03 techniques** are the staged recipe's whole sequence, cut into transitions with event
  exits:
  - `roll`: wind, swing, over and flat. The swing ends once the pelvis has turned past 90° toward
    the side, and flat once `lieOf` reads the target.
  - `to-fours`: fold, tuck and prop end once both shanks and a hand on each side bear, then
    `fours`' bearing phase ends once no trunk segment bears.
  - `kneel-up`: sit, kneel, the arms and tall, ending once no hand bears and the trunk is upright.
  - `step-up`: shift, lift, swing and reach, then the bearing phases `half kneel` and `onto the
    toes`, ending once the lead foot bears flat.
  - `lunge-up`: the lunge's four rungs, each a bearing phase ended by its height, then `even`.
- **The 06 techniques** are human strategies the old recipe lacks:
  - `post-up`: the technical stand-up.
  - `walk-back`: the hands walk back toward feet planted flat, the hips over the feet.

  Both keep three supports through the transfer every past attempt failed at.
- **More than one technique per edge is the point.** A second `roll` (a log roll beside the curl
  roll), or a faster `lunge-up`, is a new entry with the same `to`. The search (part G) and the
  game's progression add them.

**Unlocking.** A technique's `stage` says whether the game offers it. When character progression
exists, a character's unlocked techniques are a list of names that filters what its configs may
name (`Part.fits`). Nothing reads such a list until then, so none is written.

## C. The graph, and what each technique is measured to do

- **The graph.** Positions are its nodes, and `(technique, side)` pairs are its edges, by `to`.
- **The graph is Stand up's.** The other two sub-minds share its positions, and Get away its
  roll, but walk no path.
- **`pathsTo`** (`src/core/mind/ground/graph.ts`, pure and exported) finds paths from a position
  to standing over the edges Stand up's config allows, and chooses the next edge from the record.
- **The record.** `assets/core/ground-techniques.json` holds, for each technique, side, model and
  hold, what the bed (part G) measured:
  - done of begun;
  - mean time;
  - outlet: the share of its done ends from which the next edge on some path succeeded.

  It is read as `simbiconEnvelope` reads its envelope (`assets/core/simbicon-envelope.json`).
  Without a record an edge counts once, so the shortest path wins.
- **The cost of an edge** is its expected time to success: mean time over done share. A path's
  cost is the sum of its edges'.

**Tests:** `tests/ground-graph.test.mjs`, fast, on `pathsTo` alone:

- From each position, with the 03 techniques, the path is the staged order.
- Sitting with no `sit-*` technique has no path.
- With the record, a path through a faster, surer technique beats a shorter one.

## D. Stand up

`src/core/mind/ground/stand-up.ts`: `standUp(own, view, world, config): SubMind`. It is a kind of
its own, `"stand-up"` (`SubMindConfig`, `SUB_MIND_PARTS`, `subMind`,
`src/core/mind/sub-minds.ts`; `CONFIG_KEYS`). It only rises.

**`wants`:** `state.active || view.down`. `active` is set at `begin` and cleared at hand-back.

**Each step:**

1. If a technique runs, step it. If it ends, log `{technique, side, end, seconds}` in
   `state.log`, a ring of fixed length, and begin nothing this step.
2. If none runs, choose one by `pathsTo` toward standing and begin it. A technique's end and the
   next one's beginning are always a step apart, and between them the sub-mind does not hold the
   body (part E), so a higher-ranked sub-mind can take it there.
3. **Brace.** On `between`, or while the centre of mass is faster than `TUMBLING`, 0.5 m/s, the
   joints are held at the angles read on entry at moderate activation: protective, not limp.
   Bracing ends at a position, or after `BRACE_LIMIT`, 1 s, after which the edge is chosen from
   the nearest lie (`reading.lie`).
4. **Hand-back.** Once `standReady` (exported from `position.ts`) has held for 0.1 s, `active`
   clears and the host resumes. `standReady` holds when all of these do:
   - the body is standing and `view.down` is false;
   - both feet bear;
   - the centre of mass is over their polygon (`withinSupport`, `src/core/control/support.ts`);
   - the centre of mass is slower than 0.3 m/s.

**Why the hand-back asks so little.** It asks less than `recoveryReady` because the stance takes
the body from there (`Locomotion.resume` raises the asked height over `RISING_SECONDS`). A crouch
is never handed back: `view.down` would read it down against full height and take it back the
next step. So `crouch-up` is a technique.

**Failures** are counted per `(technique, side)` over one time down.

- An edge that has failed `RETRIES`, 2, times is dropped from `pathsTo`'s edges while any path
  remains without it.
- With none left, the counts clear and it tries again.
- Every choice is from the position read now; nothing restarts from a fixed first stage.

**What the player configures:**

```ts
/** **Stand up** (`standUp`): rise by the techniques it may use, choosing among them by `by`. */
interface StandUpConfig {
  readonly kind: "stand-up";
  /** The techniques it may use, in the player's order of preference. */
  readonly techniques: readonly TechniqueName[];
  /** How it chooses a path: the cheapest by the record, or the first technique in its order that has one. */
  readonly by: "fastest" | "order";
}
```

- **The editor** shows `techniques` as a ranked list of the offered ones, each switchable. That is
  where a character's unlocks will show.
- **"By order"** lets a player say "from the back, post up" by putting `post-up` and `sit-up` ahead
  of `roll`.
- **Faults:**
  - a technique the body does not fit;
  - a list with no path to standing from the back or from the front (`pathsTo`).
- **The preset** is `STAND_UP`: the 03 techniques by `fastest`, and every offered technique once
  06 lands.

**Its name** for the readout is `stand up: <technique>.<phase>` or `stand up: brace`.

## E. Get away, and Fight on the ground

Both are kinds of their own in `src/core/mind/ground/`. They read the same position as Stand up
(part A), but neither walks the graph: that is Stand up's alone. Get away uses one technique,
`roll`, the same one Stand up turns over with, so a roll is written and searched once. Fight on
the ground uses no technique; its pivot, cover and kick are its own.

### Get away

`getAway`, kind `"get-away"`. It wants the body while it lies (back, front, left, right) and the
nearest foe is within `metres`.

- **The foe** is `nearestFoe(senses, centre, "standing")` (`src/core/mind/targets.ts`), as Move
  reads it.
- **The dead band** is Move's `LEAVING`, 0.5 m, exported from `src/core/skills/move.ts` and reused.
- **What it does:**
  - It rolls over the side away from the foe, one `roll` after another. As with Stand up, a roll's
    end and the next roll are a step apart, where it reads the foe again.
  - A roll from the back or the front goes to the other lie, so rolls alternate and the body
    travels sideways, about a body's width a roll.
  - It keeps the body until the running roll ends, then lets it go once the foe is beyond `metres`
    plus the band.

```ts
interface GetAwayConfig {
  readonly kind: "get-away";
  /** It rolls away while the nearest foe is within this, m: 2 as it starts. */
  readonly metres: number;
  /** The rolls it may use, in order: today `roll`. */
  readonly techniques: readonly TechniqueName[];
}
```

### Fight on the ground

`groundFight`, kind `"ground-fight"`. It wants the body while it is on its back or sitting and the
nearest foe is within `reach`.

- **What it does:**
  - It turns the feet toward the foe: a hip pivot on the back, the legs swinging round.
  - The forearms cover the head.
  - It kicks a foot at the nearest of the foe's segments within the leg's reach, by an effector
    goal for the foot (`motorControl`'s `EffectorGoal`, `BodySpec.effectors`), chambered then
    extended.
- **Blows need no new rule.** A foot that meets a segment closing is a blow, as any two segments
  are (`energyShares`, `src/core/rules/share.ts`).

```ts
interface GroundFightConfig {
  readonly kind: "ground-fight";
  /** It fights while the nearest foe is within this, m: 1.2 as it starts. */
  readonly reach: number;
  /** Which feet kick: either, the left, the right, or none (cover only). */
  readonly kicks: "either" | "left" | "right" | "none";
}
```

When the discrete actions protocol lands (`docs/plans/2026-10-13-discrete-combat-actions.md`), its
kick becomes an action whose executor runs from these positions. Until then it is the sub-mind's
own.

### The three together

The player ranks them in "When down", e.g. Fight on the ground, then Get away, then Stand up. With
that ranking:

- a foe in reach is fought;
- a foe near but out of reach is rolled away from;
- otherwise the body rises.

**Holding the body to a boundary.** `hosting` hands the body to a higher sub-mind at once. So
`SubMind` gains an optional `holds?(): boolean`. While the sub-mind that has the body holds it, no
other sub-mind takes it.

- **What holds means.** True while a technique is under way. False on the step after one ends,
  since the next begins a step later (part D). A higher-ranked sub-mind that wants the body gets it
  there, as gaits change only at a landing.
- **When it is read.** Only while a sub-mind has the body (`state.has >= 0`). The host is never
  held back from a body that stands.
- **Existing sub-minds.** `lying`, `supportRecovery`, `researchRise` and the reptile's `recover`
  do not implement it and behave as now.
- **The test.** `hosting` changes by one condition, held by a new fast test,
  `tests/sub-mind-holds.test.mjs`, of a mock host and mock sub-minds. A lower sub-mind running a
  chain of techniques keeps the body through each, and loses it to a higher one that wants it at
  the step between two of them.

The conditions also exclude each other by position. Get away wants only a lying body, and Fight on
the ground only a body on its back or sitting. A body kneeling or lunging is Stand up's, whatever
the foe does.

**Wiring:** each kind has its `Part` in `SUB_MIND_PARTS`, its keys in `CONFIG_KEYS`, and its
fields in the editor (`src/ui/mind-editor.ts`). Links carry them (`src/ui/mind-link.ts`).

## F. The Lab's "On the ground" scenario

`ScenarioId` gains `"ground"`, and `SCENARIOS` gains `{ id: "ground", name: "On the ground" }`
(`src/lab/scenarios.ts`). The Get-up experiment stays. The files are
`src/lab/ground-scenario.ts`, `ground-mode.ts` and `ground-meter.ts`.

**The body's start.** The shell builds every scenario's body standing in its guard at the origin
(the load in `src/lab/main.ts`). `LabScenario` gains an optional placement hook, which the load
uses in its place:

```ts
placement?(spec: BodySpec, address: LabAddress): Parameters<typeof buildBody>[2];
```

**Starts**, each drawn from the address's `seed` (`mulberry32`, `src/rng.ts`):

- **Shoved.** Standing, then one shove at 1 s to the middle trunk, of a drawn direction (0 to 360°)
  and impulse (40 to 90 N s). The shove is lifted out of `startStance`'s session
  (`src/lab/stance-mode.ts`) into an exported function that both scenarios call.
- **Dropped.** The body is built in its guard posture with each joint's angles drawn within 0.3 of
  its range about the guard's. The root is turned to a drawn orientation, its lowest point 0.3 m
  over the ground.
  - Its own mind has it from the first step, so the fall is part of the run, and the clock starts
    at the first `down`. A drop that is not down within 3 s ends the run as "landed standing", and
    is counted apart.
  - Self-overlap: `contactsOf` reports pairs of the body's own segments other than joint partners.
    So if the first step pushes on such a pair, the scenario restarts on the draw of `(seed,
    attempt + 1)`, at most 5 times. A placement hook cannot see a step, so the check is the run's.
- **Placed.** The body is built in one position:
  - lying four ways, as part A's test builds it;
  - or fours, half-kneel or squat (the Warrior's `posture-holds.json`; the other bodies offer the
    lying ones until theirs are added);
  - each joint perturbed by a drawn 0.1 of its range.

  It exercises one technique from many starts.

**The foe.** None, or a mindless standing dummy at 1, 2 or 4 m, which is enough to show Get away
and Fight on the ground.

- The scenario makes its own senses hub and adds the body and the dummy to it, as
  `src/lab/attack-mode.ts` does (`createSenses`, `hub.add`, `actor.drive(undefined, { senses })`).
- The dummy is a `hangDummy` (`src/lab/targets.ts`): one sphere hung at its centre's height, 0.5 m,
  low enough for a kick from the ground.
- It is added with `out: () => false`. Attack's targets go out once hit, which would drop the dummy
  from `nearestFoe`.
- Nothing pulls a struck dummy back, so a kick moves it.
- A walking foe with a mind comes only if the eye gate asks for one. It needs a second minded body
  in the Lab, which the Lab does not have.

**The panel:**

- **Scenario panel** (restarts on change):
  - Start; Seed; direction and impulse for Shoved; position for Placed.
  - **Driven by:** "As the mind does", or one technique alone from where the body is, reporting how
    it ended.
  - The foe; Gusts (`src/lab/wind.ts`).
- **Controls:** Restart, Next seed, Shove again.
- **The mind.** `LabScenario.mind(chosen)` gives a chosen mind whose `subs` is `[lie]` the ground
  sub-minds instead: `[support-recovery]` in chunk 02, and the three new kinds once they exist. Any
  other choice keeps its own, so Classic shows Rise, then steady on the same start.

**The readout** (`ground-meter.ts`):

- time down;
- time to hand-back;
- the trail: each position read, when it was entered, and which sub-mind had the body;
- the technique and phase under way;
- each technique's attempts and how they ended;
- after Shove again, a running table: falls, stood, median time and worst.

**Tests:** `tests/lab-ground.test.mjs`, fast:

- The address round-trips the scenario's fields.
- A Placed fours start on the Warrior reads fours after 0.25 s.
- Dropped starts on two seeds build.

## G. The bed and the search

The names avoid the acceptance era's `research/stand-up-*.mjs`.

**`research/rising-bed.mjs`**, with `research/rising-worker.mjs`: worker threads, one sequential
loop in each, as `research/core-rise-trials.mjs` and `core-rise-worker.mjs` do.

- **Flags:**
  - `--starts shoved,dropped,placed`;
  - `--models`;
  - `--held empty,club`;
  - `--seeds 1-6`;
  - `--sub stand-up|support-recovery`, so the old sub-mind reads on the same bed;
  - `--techniques <names>`, or `--technique <name>` alone;
  - `--gusts`.
- **Episodes** are capped at 30 s.
- **Each run records** whether it stood, its time to hand-back, its trail, and each technique's
  ends and times.
- **The report,** by start × model × held:
  - stood of all;
  - within 6 s and within 12 s;
  - the median with failures counted at the cap;
  - for each technique and side: done of begun, mean time, and outlet.
- **`--target`** runs the owner's target battery itself, as `docs/reference/rising.md#dynamic-rise`
  defines it:
  - the Warrior and the Rogue standing under Puncher for 1 s, then the mass-scaled shove in 16
    directions 22.5° apart, empty-handed and with a right-hand club;
  - the clock from the sub-mind's takeover;
  - completion: 0.5 s of verified quiet standing (`recoveryReady`), then 2 s standing and a 1 s
    commanded walk.

  It reports the target's gates, passed or not. Only this mode judges the target. The everyday bed
  is quicker and wider (three bodies, drops), and is not that battery.
- **`--record`** writes `assets/core/ground-techniques.json`.
- **The bed:**
  - Shoved in 8 directions, plus Dropped.
  - The Warrior, the Rogue and the skeleton, empty-handed and with the club.
  - Calm, seeds 1 to 6, validated on 7 to 12.
  - Its first reading, in chunk 02, is Rise, then steady's, as the reference.

**`research/rising-search.mjs`**: CMA-ES (`research/cma.mjs`) over one technique's settings in the
unit cube, as `research/locomotion-search.mjs` does.

- **What it searches:** each phase's posture offsets, its event thresholds and its bearing time
  constants.
- **Its starts:** Placed starts of the technique's `from` positions.
- **Its score:** done share first, then time, with a failure costing the cap. A technique's
  searched numbers go into its spec as code constants. The player never sees them.

## H. Making it rise: the order of attack

1. **The staged sequence, event-gated** (03). The recipe's poses cut into techniques with event
   exits and windows, driven exactly as the recipe drives them, and read on the bed against Rise,
   then steady. The first measurement is how much of the 22 to 29 s was the supervisor.
2. **Searched techniques, with a centre-of-mass reference** (05):
   - Each technique is searched on its own starts.
   - Each bearing phase holds the centre of mass to a reference relative to its support, recorded
     from the bed's done runs (Reichenberg and Laue's balancer).
   - Where the pose drive falls short, a phase may push freedoms by `MusclePush` speed servos at a
     share of strength, as SIMBICON does (`src/core/skills/simbicon.ts`).
   - The mirrored side lands here too.
3. **The other paths** (06): `sit-up`, `sit-to-squat` and `crouch-up` (VanSant's commonest rise);
   `post-up`; and `walk-back` from fours. Each is searched as in 2, and the record says which path
   is fastest from where.
4. **Discover, then constrain** (07), for any technique still failing on the bed.
   - The research search starts with the muscles' ceilings raised by an override passed in
     ("Tuning is immutable"), then steps them down by 0.95 a stage to the body's own, searching
     again at each.
   - Nothing raised ships. A technique that does not complete at the body's own strength is not
     offered.

## The owner's choices

**Decided:**

- The design is built beside "Rise, then steady" and "Research rise". It keeps nothing of theirs
  for compatibility, and the Get-up experiment stays as it is.
- Fighting on the ground and getting away are sub-minds of their own, so Stand up only rises.
- Several techniques per edge and several paths to standing, so the player configures, and later
  unlocks, how a fighter gets up.

**Open, for chunk 09:**

- **The gate for the switch.** Either the owner's target on `--target`, or Stand up beating Rise,
  then steady on the same bed. The target is the bar for "rising works". Waiting for it may hold
  the presets on the old sub-mind for a long time.
- **Which presets switch.** `docs/reference/rising.md#dynamic-rise` records "Presets retain their
  existing recovery". Part I gives the ground sub-minds to Classic, Puncher, Scrapper, the
  behaviours presets and `GAME_FIGHTER`, the Arena's default, which lies still today.
- **The Crypt.** Whether a fall still ends a humanoid's fight there. If not,
  `HUMANOID.fallEndsFight` becomes false, and downed enemies and party members get up and fight on,
  as the reptile already does.
