# The back step

A Warrior standing in a foe's reach has two choices, to punch or to wait. `back-step` is a third:
a step out of a punch's reach that leaves the body on its feet and near enough to answer. Its
drill and baselines are made (`research/skills/locomotion/back-step-drill.mjs`, `fd8ec9d56`);
this plan is the worker's: the step itself, to the drill's number, then its place in the Mind.

## The goal, in the owner's words

> now that we have a good Warrior punch, what's the next step for interesting and dynamic fights
> (we can stay with empty handed Warrior for now)? would it be a new skill, an improvement of an
> existing skill, or something else?

> after the price-repair, you said the next thing would be a new skill?

> I want you to do 1 and 2 and write the plan for 3.

There, 1 was the drill and its baselines, 2 what they say the skill is, and 3 a worker under a
goal adding the skill and iterating on it against the drill's number.

Said before, and standing: "i just don't want to have to repeatedly re-train footwork as part of
every skill"; "We shouldn't have any rules that prevent a Mind from doing what it wants, the mind
alone should decide the behaviors of the body"; "a gentle accidental graze to me is not a 'landed
punch'".

**The owner has set no bar for this plan.** The bar below is the agent's, shown to the owner on
2026-11-16, who asked for this plan without changing it; the owner overrules it.

- **The punch reaches to 1.0 m.** Of 128 punches whose defender was 1.0 m or more from the
  attacker (centres) 0.225 s after the fire, when a punch that lands does, one took 0.5 hp; of the

- **A body walking in takes the punch double**: coming at 0.3 m/s or more at the fire, 72 of 108
  taken and a mean 1.13 hp against 0.59 standing. A walk back that has turned to come in again

- **A step begun on seeing the punch is too late.** From a punch's fire to its blow is 0.225 s

  on the foe's range and approach, before the punch.
- **The legs' walk takes about a second from a stand before the body travels away**: half

  a walk away is a help only where it was ordered about a second before the fire, and it is not

- **A body that left at once, at 0.5 to 1 m/s, is not hit, and the stance catches it**: given

  second on, 21 of 21 on its feet; at 1.5 m/s it falls in 4 trials of 21. Given it again each
  0.75 s the attacker is near, under the Mind, 1 m/s falls in 3 or 4 trials of 24 and 0.5 m/s in
  none.
  It reads what a step is worth, not that the body can make one; and standing where the step
  left it, it is back for under half the punches.
- **Not known**: whether the Warrior's legs can put its centre at 0.5 m/s away within 0.15 s of
  the order (some 40 N s from the ground through soles that start under the body); what the Mind
  would give for the step, which is its profile's to say once it exists.

## What done is

The agent's bar, until the owner gives one:

1. **One number until it is met, on the drill**: of the punches thrown at a defender that may
   only step, the share that take 0.5 hp, at most half of the standing defender's on each
   attacker (drilled 28 %, the Mind 19 %; the body given 0.5 m/s reads 0 and 16 %), the defender
   on its feet in every trial.
2. **It gets back**: of the punches the defender was farther than 0.95 m from the attacker
   after, in two of three it is within it again 1.5 s from the fire, at a median of a second or
   less (the drill's `back`; the body given 0.5 m/s and left standing reads 65 of 156).
3. **Bouts, read on life**, once the Mind chooses it: a Warrior with `back-step` against one
   without, 24 start gaps that chose nothing, each on each side: it loses less life, deals no
   less, wins most. Blows avoided are not counted
   ([the approaches](../approaches.md#guarding): more blocks are not more wins).
4. **The mirror stays a fight**: both Warriors with it, no more silent than without it on the
   same gaps, as many ended by knockout.
5. **The owner watches a Warrior mirror in the Arena** and says it looks like a fighter making a
   punch miss, not one running away.

No bouts and no Mind work until 1 and 2 are met.

## The rules this plan is followed by

- **It is run as the punch was** ([the skill recipe](../recipes/skill.md#how-the-work-is-run)): a
  worker under a goal, a reviewing session as `AGENTS.md` has it.
- **The step is the skill's own.** The stance's and the walk's code and every record of theirs
  stay as they are, so no other skill's footwork is trained again: what `back-step` asks of the
  legs it asks as the moving punch asks the walk into its blow (`e29d81e7d`). A change the
  stance itself needs is a finding, written down and brought to the owner, not made here.
- **A step is asked, never forced**: the drill's defender is ordered the step as any body is
  ordered a gait (`Orders.gait`, or what the worker finds fits); in a fight the Mind alone chooses
  it, by its price.
- **Each lever is read on the drill**, both attackers, the same gaps before and after, counts
  before shares; a reading that moves under 4 punches of 40 is the drill's own spread until a
  second set of gaps says otherwise.
- **The price repair has landed** (`54dc338cc`, [the approaches](../approaches.md#minds)):
  steps 1 to 3 touch nothing under `src/minds/option/`; step 4 prices the step as the Mind now
  prices a foe and a hold.
- **A run over half an hour is announced** with its price and what will differ when it ends.

## The steps

### 1. What the body can do

From the stand the drill's defender holds, the legs' pushes searched flat out
([the skill recipe](../recipes/skill.md#2-what-the-body-can-do)), scored on the centre's speed
away from the attacker 0.15 s after the start with the body on its feet 1.5 s later.

**Gate**: the speed, beside the scale the drill gives (0.5 m/s clears most punches, 1 m/s nearly
all, 1.5 m/s falls). Under 0.3 m/s from the stand as it is, the starts tried are the ones a
fight gives (a foot behind, the walk's own strides) before the body is called unable; a body
that cannot is the owner's to change.

**Met from a foot behind, not from the square stand**, read on what a punch hits: the head, the
upper and the middle trunk, the nearest of them to the attacker (probes outside the repository,
the defender's skill set's command wrapped, the attacker standing 1.4 m off).

- **From the square stand the legs' push moves the centre, not the targets.** With the stance let
  go for a push and given back to catch, the centre is at 0.45 to 0.51 m/s away at 0.15 s
  (knees extended at a quarter, ankles plantarflexed at a quarter to three quarters, 0.06 to
  0.08 s; flat out it hops at 0.89 m/s and falls), but the hips go back and the trunk turns
  forward over them: over 1,728 pushes of the hips, knees, ankles and the trunk's own flexion,
  the nearest target is at most 0.025 m back at 0.225 s and 0.09 m at 0.4 s. The pushes with the
  hips flexed at a half bring the head 0.16 m *toward* the attacker.
- **Asked through the stance alone from the square stand** (a swing of either foot behind, its
  capture point sent back), under 0.1 m/s at 0.15 s: the pendulum's pivot is no further ahead of
  the centre than the bearing sole's toe, about 0.13 m.
- **From a foot behind, the stance sways the whole body.** The defender's walk in a carriage of
  the left foot 0.4 m ahead and 0.25 m wide (0.445 m apart as it stands), its stance's centre sent
  0.1 m behind the rear sole: the targets 0.06 m back at 0.225 s, 0.14 m at 0.4 s, 0.22 m at
  0.6 s, 0.31 m at 1 s, with the centre; no step and no fall in six moments of six. A stagger of
  0.3 m gives 0.05, 0.11, 0.17 m; of 0.2 m, 0.04, 0.09, 0.14 m. A push of the lead leg on top
  changes nothing with the stance kept (it holds those freedoms), and less with it let go.
- A probe's readings of the centre take it from the segments: the stance's reading stands still
  while the stance is let go.

### 2. A step that clears the drilled punch

The simplest controller that makes the motion
([the skill recipe](../recipes/skill.md#3-a-native-controller-that-succeeds-on-some-cases)), its
mechanism named before it is searched: a push that sends the capture point out behind the soles,
so the stance's own catch step lands it, is the first to try, since the stance lifts a foot at
once where the capture point has left the sole and caught 1 m/s in the drill.

- The drill gains a policy that orders the step where `away` orders the walk, at a range.
- Read: the drilled attacker's 21 gaps, the range the step is ordered at swept; `gone`, falls,
  taken.

**Gate**: the drilled attacker's number (28 %) with no fall.

**Met** by the sway (`back-step`, `src/skills/locomotion/back-step/index.ts`: a gait that asks the
stance's centre behind the rear sole while it is ordered, from both feet down), the drill's
`step:1.0` with `--carriage '{"width":0.25,"stagger":0.4}'`: 3 of 25 and 5 of 23 punches took
0.5 hp on the two sets of 21 gaps (2 to 3 m and 2.025 to 3.025 m), no fall in 42 trials; the same
defender standing in that carriage, 27 of 41 and 25 of 42. Ordered at 1.1 m, 5 of 41 and 7 of 41,
one fall; at 1.2 m and 1.4 m the sway is over before the punch and the attacker follows (47 to
87 %).

- **The order is held until the body is still**: the drill orders the step at least 0.6 s and
  until the defender stands on both feet slower than 0.15 m/s; let go sooner, Move changes gait
  between catch steps and the walk asked back in felled the body (7 trials of 21, the push).
- **The pushes, read in the drill**, square: the push with the hips flexed at a half took 1 of 22
  on each set at 1.4 m, no fall once it began only from both feet down, by drawing the drilled
  punch early (fired from 1.22 m, a standing defender's from 0.93 m); with the hips at none,
  44 to 53 % taken, and with the trunk leaning back, 42 to 81 %.
- **The Warrior fights square** (`CARRIAGES` has only the square stance): a body that sways back
  stands with a foot behind first. In the drill the defender is given the carriage; in a fight it
  would be the Warrior's stance, which every other skill of it was made in. That is the owner's to
  choose before step 4, and is brought to the owner with this reading.

### 3. The Mind's punches, and back

The same step under the Mind attacker, where punches fire from 0.85 m and come again: the range
and the approach it is begun on, and the step in that brings it back (the walk's start is a
second; a step in of the same kind, or a step out short enough to need none).

**First reading**, the sway under the Mind attacker, both in the carriage, two sets of 24 gaps
(2.6 to 4.9 m and 2.65 to 4.95 m): standing, 103 of 296 and 98 of 293 taken (35 and 33 %);
ordered at 0.95 to 1.2 m, 23 to 33 %, falls in 1 to 4 trials of 24. The drill's policy walks the
defender back to 0.8 to 0.95 m after each step, inside the Mind's punching range, for 1.5 s.

**Gate**: the bar's 1 and 2 on both attackers. A learned actor over the step
([the skill recipe](../recipes/skill.md#4-a-learned-actor-over-it)) is opened only if the native
step meets some of the gaps and not the number, and is announced with its price first.

### 4. The Mind chooses it

The price repair has landed: `back-step` an Option with its profile measured (what it
takes and loses against a foe that punches, its falls), no rule sending a body out. Then the
bar's 3 and 4 on 24 to 48 bouts, and the owner's look.
