# A testbed: what an action brings, behind a contract

The Option Mind prices an Option from profiles: one table of trials a skill, a limb, a body and a
body struck, read by its nearest trials, with the foe's pressure worked out from them by a rule.
This plan puts what a Mind is told behind contracts, each with more than one implementation, fed
from one log and judged by one scorecard. **It is taken up after the back step's plan lands**, by
a worker under a goal; nothing here is begun.

## The goal, in the owner's words

> if we will be adding many more skills and many more loadouts and character bodies, and fights
> will happen in many more environments (randomly generated levels, multiple units fighting each
> other, etc.) -- how do we handle this all in a clean/systematic/general/simple way?
> what we have now is just a small fraction of where i want to get, if we want to have a scalable
> system (boht in terms of scope, and also interms of throughput/latency), then is there a
> different way that you would apporach the problem?
> i am open to different ideas, including making the "profiles" a pluggable contract that can have
> multiple implementations (e.g. hard-coded esimator, vs coarse lookup table, vs decision forest,
> vs any other approach).
> what i want is a general ai testbed, that we can experiment on without being locked in to one
> way of doing things (unless we have a good reason to believe that one way is optimal or
> dominant).

> write it up as its own plan after the back step

Of the chooser, the same day, and to its place here "yes, add the chooser work to the testbed
plan":

> right now it does 100 per step, and discards everything every step.
> what if we wanted it to be incrementally performing some number of search iterations per step,
> and then not throwing away the work each time.
> is there some principled way to do that?
> idreally i'd like to experiment with many different approaches to this, so it should be
> pluggable (and it might already be there with the Chooser).

Said the same day, and standing:

- "I want the design (profiles, etc.) to be scalable".
- "is our use of profiles circular? is there a more principled way of doing this?"
- Of a fixed setting or a rule deciding for a Mind: "So you are proposing a hard-coded ad-hoc

## What is known

Each read on 2026-11-17, Warrior on Warrior, empty hands, aggression 2, the 24 gaps 2.65 to
5.41 m; [the approaches](../approaches.md) hold the Mind's and the back step's own.

- **The tables multiply.** 51 strike profiles over 27 keys (striker, skill, what it holds, the
  body struck), a limb each: three bodies are nine tables a skill a limb. Only the punches fought
  by have over a thousand trials a key; the rest 4 to 77. The asset is 9.7 MB
  (`assets/skills/strikes/strike-profiles/strike-profiles.json`), 6.2 MB before one re-measure
  (`5e6cfac66`). The two hands read alike from the square stand (dealt 0.50 and 0.48, landed
  94 and 93 %).
- **A start leaves out state that decides the outcome.** Back steps the Mind chose went down in
  4.4 % of 914, ordered ones in 1.5 %, and the difference holds at like range, phase, speed and
  feet (stagger under 0.2 m: 3.8 % of 765 against 1.4 % of 997). Built from half the chosen sways
  and read on the other half, a profile of chosen sways priced falls at 5.1 % against 4.5 % given
  and life at 1.03 of given; pooled with the ordered ones, 2.7 % and 1.46 (`dac468d58`). What the
  chosen moments hold that the start does not record is not known.
- **The foe's behaviour is a rule, and wrong at both ends.** The pressure is the foe's best
  strike's dealt over its cycle, times the seconds (`pressureRateAt`,
  `src/minds/option/percepts.ts`): the foe throwing back to back from wherever it stands. What a
  hold is priced to take (that rate with its grace and the blow coming, as the Mind offers it),
  against the life lost in the next 0.2 s in bouts, is 17 to 20 times over where the foe throws
  nothing and 0.79 to 0.83 where its punch is committed. Of a punch's own priced terms, the blow
  already coming as it begins is priced at nothing and takes 1.33 % of life, and the pressure
  while it runs is priced at 4.35 % and takes 1.87 %.
- **Measured from bout records with the foe's punch state in the start, the near future travels
  and the far does not.** Nearest 40 moments of like foe state, by range and seconds, held out by
  bout: priced over given 0.94 to 1.10 at 0.2 s, 0.99 to 1.01 at 0.9 s and at 1.65 s. Built on the
  mirror without the back step and read where a side steps back and throws a third as often: 0.97
  to 1.07 at 0.2 s, 1.21 to 1.37 at 0.9 s, 1.40 to 1.69 at 1.65 s. So the next fifth of a second
  is the bodies', and beyond it is the foe's habits.
- **A read's cost grows with its trials.** The same 5 s of fight took 2.4 s before trials held
  stances, 10.1 s after (`5e6cfac66`), 5.0 s with the nearest trials found by k-d trees and each
  body read in its nearest measured carriage (`08f31c70e`, `e00486838`): 472 look-ups a decision
  became 966.
- **The tree chooser keeps nothing between decisions.** It decides every 0.2 s (`DECIDING`,
  `src/minds/option/value.ts`) or at a cue, by 100 runs (`RUNS`, `src/minds/option/chooser.ts`) of
  a tree made anew: its searches are dropped as the clock moves (`searching`), and its state holds
  its stream of draws alone. Its runs are a number of its config, not a time. Of a decision's
  time, the profile look-ups were 68 % before the k-d trees and about half after. A chooser is a
  part with a state of its own and one `decide` (`Selection`, `src/minds/choice.ts`), so one that
  keeps its work is another kind; a mind's memory is its saved data, and a bout saved and loaded
  plays on as it would have ([the architecture](../architecture.md#bouts-tapes-and-replay)).
- **Seams that exist.** A Mind's predictions are a part with two kinds, `measured` and `prior`
  (`src/minds/option/predictions-part.ts`, `src/minds/option/profile-contract.ts`); its chooser is
  one of `CHOOSERS` (tree, valued, ranked); every question of how well is an evaluation
  (`src/evaluation/`); a bout's saved decisions are read as priced against given by
  `research/skills/strikes/bout-readings.mjs`; a stale profile is read and says so (`ee5b0836d`).
  Not read for this plan: how levels and several units a side are built
  ([the architecture](../architecture.md#command)).

## The design

1. **A situation is numbers.** A body by what it is (reach, height, mass, what it holds); two
   bodies by range, bearing, velocity, and what each is doing and how far into it; how each stands
   (`src/skills/perception/stance-numbers.ts`); the ground near (room behind, slope, the nearest
   wall). No name is a key. A new body, loadout or level is other numbers; several units are
   several pairs.
2. **Four contracts**, each a part with kinds, as predictions are now:

   | Contract | It answers | Kinds to begin with |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | the outcome model | from here, doing this: what is dealt and taken, how long, where it leaves the body, whether it falls, and on how many trials it says so | by hand; nearest trials (today's); a coarse table; a forest; the world itself played forward, as the truth the rest are read against |
   | the others' model | what each other body does next | throws back to back (today's); its habits as measured; the Mind's own choosing |
   | the chooser | which Option, within the time it is given | tree, valued, ranked (today's); the kinds that keep their work (below); a trained policy |
   | the reader | the numbers of now | the senses as they are |

   A model with no trials near a situation says so and the next kind answers: stale or coarse
   before by hand.
3. **One log.** A drill's trial and a bout's decision write the same row: the situation, the
   action, what followed over the seconds asked. Every model is built from rows; a drill is how
   rare situations are reached, and drawn against chosen is a weight on rows.
4. **One scorecard**, one command, any kind against any other on the same cases: priced over
   given, held out by bout and by slice (the foe's state, range, body), counts first; the bouts
   (wins, life, silence, falls, faults); the milliseconds a decision.
5. **A chooser that keeps its work.** What a search found is evidence of a situation that has
   since moved: it is used again by how like the new situation is and how old it is. The kinds to
   read against each other, the cheapest first:

   | Kind | What it keeps | What may go wrong |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | the models' answers kept | what each Option brings at a situation, by its numbers rounded | a rounding too fine never hits |
   | a warm start | each Option's worth at the last decision, as the first estimate at this one | an old worth misleads the first runs: it fades with age |
   | the branch kept | the tree under the Option taken and the way it ended, as the new root | the body is never quite where the model left it: a test of near enough, else a tree anew |
   | the search spread over steps | the tree for the next decision, a few runs a step while the Option runs | it needs where the body will be then |
   | a table by situation | each rounded situation's worths, across decisions and bouts | a rounding too coarse joins unlike situations |
   | a prior fitted from past searches | each Option's worth, as a model | its fitting, again as skills change |

   The Mind hands a chooser its budget a step, in runs or in time. What a chooser keeps is in its
   saved state, or is a cache a loaded bout makes again and the bout plays the same without.

## The steps

Each lands alone; 1 to 3 change no bout (the fingerprint).

1. **The scorecard**, on today's Mind: `bout-readings.mjs --prices` and the held-out readings of
   2026-11-17 made one evaluation, with a decision's milliseconds beside them.
2. **The outcome model's contract**, today's profile reading its first kind and the prior its
   second, the Options asking it and nothing else.
3. **The others' model's contract**, today's rate its first kind.
4. **The log**: the row, written by the Strike drill's sparring and by a bout; one profile built
   from rows and read against the one built today.
5. **A second kind of each**, read on the scorecard: the others' model from bout records with the
   foe's punch state (the reading above); an outcome model whose read does not grow with its
   trials. Whichever the scorecard prefers is the game's, the other kept.
6. **Bodies and the ground as numbers** in the situation; one model a skill across bodies, read
   against the tables a body it replaces.
7. **Several units**: whether what two foes cost is the sum of what each costs, read two on one.
8. **The chooser's budget and its kept work**, at any time after step 1: the budget handed in,
   the tree chooser at a budget of 100 runs a decision playing as it does now (the fingerprint);
   then the kinds of the design's 5, the models' answers kept and the search spread over steps
   first. Each is read against the tree chooser at the same budget: the bouts between them
   (`npm run match`), and a step's worst milliseconds, not its mean.

## What done is

The agent's bar, until the owner gives one:

1. Any kind of each contract is named in a Mind's config and played against any other by one
   command, with no source changed.
2. The scorecard gives the three readings for each, on the same cases.
3. The game's Mind after steps 1 to 4 plays the bouts it played before them (the fingerprint).
4. A body or a skill added after step 6 is fought with before anything is measured for it, its
   prices said to be coarse, and measuring it only adds rows.
5. A decision's milliseconds are no more than before step 1, on the same bouts.
6. A chooser's budget is its config's or the Mind's to give, a chooser that keeps its work is
   named like any other, and a bout saved and loaded under one plays on the same.

## Left open

- **Search or a trained policy at scale.** Not decided here: both are choosers, and the scorecard
  is how they are compared. The back step's learned actor found no gain in 2 M decisions of a
  20 M run.
- **What the chosen moments hold that a start does not.** To look at first: the foe's action and
  its progress, the seconds since the body's own last action ended and since it last decided.
- **Whether a long price is made of short ones**: the next 0.2 s by the foe's state, and the
  chance the foe begins a strike, the second re-read when a Mind changes.
