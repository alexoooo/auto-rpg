# The punch: a person's level, in fights, on Kinetic

## Why

The owner wants punches that land strong, at a person's level, in real fights, not only on one
drill's grid. The punch is the first skill trained on [Kinetic](2026-10-27-kinetic.md) once it is
generalized (its steps 1 to 14): its reward, inputs and profile are the ones Kinetic sets, it is
trained by the general trainer, and each step is paired against the baseline evaluation. A
punch is learned offline as a timed whole-body motion steered as it runs, and plays cheap in the
browser. The body allows it with the arms and trunk at 2.5 times the curves read
([the punch's body](../reference/punch-body.md)); a planner that looks ahead every step is not the
route, since the one-step solve already costs 2.2 to 2.8 ms a body a step.

- **What it does today**: retrained with its leg outputs as asks of the stance and walking starts
  (`research/runs/kinetic-punch`, 50.7 M decisions, toed, 120 Hz), on the Strike drill's still rung
  30 of 32 and walking 20 of 32, against the learned punch's 24 and 0; from a walk it halts and
  lands 0.19 s from ready against 1.66 s settled. A walking punch's failures are fast and light:
  the fist meets under 1.2 kg. On the fight rung the learned punch lands 3 of 56 moments: the
  fighters stand guard to guard at about 0.6 m, nearer than its window (0.89 to 1.01 m), 9 moments
  never come into it, 16 meet the foe's arms, and 20 of the 23 blows past the arms are weak
  ([learned punch](../reference/learned-punch.md)).

## The design

### Robustness

- **Train where it will be used.** Each trial draws its start: the range and bearing, the target's
  height and motion, the body's own motion (standing, mid-step, closing), the body. Trained on two
  bodies and fight moments at once, it landed none of the fights and almost none of the Rogue's
  still places ([tried and not kept](../reference/learned-punch.md#tried-and-not-kept)), so one
  ground joins at a time.
- **Judge on what training never saw.** A claim is read on the held-out set at 120 and 480 Hz. A
  punch that lands only through 120 Hz's integration error fails at 480.
- **It reacts while it runs.** The punch reads the body and the target as it goes and corrects, or
  ends when the target leaves its reach or its support fails.
- **Failing safely is part of the score.** A punch that meets a moved target, is covered or is
  shoved mid-swing does not fall; falls count against it.
- **No gain by narrowing.** A punch's success is read with its window's coverage beside its landing
  rate: a punch that lands more by refusing more has not improved.
- **One skill for every body and start.** One punch, its settings and window per body; no
  controller per loadout, hand or starting state.
- **Fights last.** The final check is fights against minds it never trained with, under the
  owner's verdict, and the owner watching.

### The bar

Human level is the untrained adult man's, from the sources the project holds
([human strike reference](../reference/human-strike-reference.md#1-punches-straight-jabcross-hook)):
the mean less one SD (`BAR`, `src/evaluation/strike/read.ts`). A punch lands when all of these hold:

| Reading | Rear hand (right) | Lead hand (left) |
|---|---|---|
| Fist speed over its last 0.1 m before contact | 6.8 m/s (8.0 ± 1.2, Adamec) | 5.3 m/s (6.2 ± 0.9, Adamec) |
| Effective mass the fist meets on its own side (`TouchWatch` masses) | 2.12 kg (2.93 ± 0.81) | 2.34 kg (3.27 ± 0.93) |
| Straight: its last 0.1 m within 20° of the surface's normal | yes | yes |
| A punch: its last 0.1 m within 45° of the line from the shoulder to the fist; the elbow within 30° of straight and the chest within 30° of upright at contact | yes | yes |
| On the mark asked, first contact within 0.5 s of the action's start | yes | yes |
| The striker up and its action ended | yes | yes |

Impulse is reported, not gated: the contact model's impulse depends on the rate
([punch](../reference/competencies.md#punch)).

A punch is good enough when 90 % of those thrown land: a blow must pass every reading at once, and
a person's single readings already sit at the mean less one SD, so 95 % asks more than an
untrained adult gives. In a fight the 90 % is of the blows that meet the foe past its arms: a fist
met by the guard (`guarded`) or by nothing as the foe steps off (`missed`) is the foe's defence,
reported beside it; when to throw is judged in matches.

The bar is the Warrior's. Another body's is that bar scaled by its mass, size and strength, so
that any humanoid's follows from its own numbers (step 2c).

## Steps

Each in order, after Kinetic step 14 and [Body layer](2026-10-28-body-layer.md) step 9 (the one
learned punch). A training run is priced on the pool first, from its first decisions' rate.

### 1. The punch, walking or not

The one learned punch on the toed Warrior, trained on the general trainer, its asks in the legs
goal: `heading` for the hip turn, `centre` for the weight behind the fist, and the walk its own to
keep or stop (`walk` left out, or `halt`), learned, not two skills. Its reward counts unmet asks;
it reads the gait's phase. Trained on to 90 % at 480 Hz as at 120, the rates' share of episodes
and the score's margins the levers.

Check: the Strike drill's still and walk rungs paired with Kinetic's baseline at 120 and 480 Hz;
walking within 10 points of still, its median time from ready under its settled start's; the
held-out reading (`research/punch-policy-check.mjs`, 320 trials, a fresh seed) at least 90 % for
each hand at both rates, none fallen; the still and walk reels watched.

### 2. The punch at its bar everywhere

Each its own ground, joined one at a time, the rungs before it holding:

- **2a. Fight moments and the close range**: from guard-to-guard range (about 0.6 m), against the
  foe's guard, and where the fight never brings it into its window. The punch widens its entry over
  the states a fight and footwork leave (mid-step, drifting, closing, nearer than its window) and
  drives its own step where it needs one. Its aim finds the trunk past the guard. Check: the fight
  rung's held-out moments at the bar (of those that meet the foe past its arms), `outside` and
  `guarded` beside it, and the land rate by starting state; the owner watches a reel of moments.
- **2b. Moving targets**: the Strike drill's rungs 1 to 5 as [Drills](2026-10-24-drills.md) builds
  them (its step 2), and Stand's shove mid-strike. A punch that winds up 0.3 s aims where the
  target will be. Check: each rung's held-out reading against the bar; the owner watches each rung.
- **2c. The bar scaled to the body, then the Rogue**: `BAR` a function of the striker's mass,
  dimensions and strength, the Warrior's values its fixed point; then the Rogue's punch at its
  scaled bar, its own schedule and window, the same network trained on with its starts. The Rogue
  weighs 0.73 of the Warrior and its arm 0.65; at the Warrior's bar the most any of its schedules
  meets is 2.1 kg. Check: the Warrior's readings unchanged by the scaling; the Rogue's held-out
  reading at its bar at both rates; its still rung watched.
- **2d. The high mark**: the guard covers the face, and the open lane over it is the top of the
  head, which a straight punch cannot meet square; a punch at the head goes around or over the
  guard, or waits for it to open, and the fist's threshold against a head is read again first
  ([what a blow is worth](../reference/learned-punch.md#what-a-blow-is-worth)). Check: the high
  mark's held-out reading at the bar on the still rung; the owner watches.

### 3. The one-two

Two punches chained at the first's stroke's end ([Kinetic](2026-10-27-kinetic.md) step 9), the
other hand's punch admitting from there and the chooser choosing it there.

Check: on the Strike drill, the second punch of a chain lands as often as a lone one from where the
first ends; `npm run match` of Kinetic choosing chains against it without.

### 4. Fighting, and iterating

The punch in the game's fighting minds as their strike action, under the owner's verdict; Kinetic
against the Duellist and Vanguard on the toed Warrior, both sides; fight moments taken again from
these bouts, since a better punch changes the moments a fight makes. Then an iteration is one
question on the punch, one change, paired by the baseline evaluation.

Check: `npm run match` of each fighter with the punch against the same fighter without, and against
minds it never trained with: blows landed at the bar, damage dealt, falls; the owner watches bouts.

## Done when

At 120 and 480 Hz the Warrior's and the Rogue's punches (the Rogue's at the scaled bar) land 90 %
of those thrown on held-out trials of the still rung, the walk rung (no slower than settled), the
fight rung (of the blows past the arms) and rungs 1 to 5, none falling and their coverage at least
today's; the high mark lands at the bar on the still rung; a one-two is two punches chained; and
Kinetic with the punch has fought the Duellist and Vanguard in matches the owner has watched a bout
of and accepted.
