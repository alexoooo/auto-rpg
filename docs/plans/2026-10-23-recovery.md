# Recovery: a graph of posture skills

## Why

The owner's target ([rising](../reference/rising.md#the-owners-target)): of 64 falls, at least 15
of 16 standing within 6 s in each body and loadout group, 61 of 64 overall, every cardinal way, a
failure-inclusive median of at most 4 s, unassisted. Young adults rise from the floor in a mean
1.4 to 2.6 s at a comfortable rate.

The game's Stand up (`step-up`, `src/core/mind/ground/step-up.ts`) stands 56 and 53 of 64 at
`cbdf389a` (two runs, each beside a placebo), a median of 15 to 16 s, and no group stands one fall
within 6 s. The time is the route's: step-up rolls onto its front, comes onto hands and knees,
kneels up, steps a foot through and lunges, each stage waiting on balance
([where the rise stops](../reference/rising.md#where-the-rise-stops),
[the budget](../reference/rising.md#the-budget)).

So the rise is a graph of posture skills: the safe postures its nodes, skills its edges, the
fastest route whose first edge admits the body taken, a failed edge ending in a safe posture from
which the graph routes again. It joins the skill set in the
[Kinetic](2026-10-27-kinetic.md#the-shape-of-a-skill) shape: a name, an entry condition, the resources it holds (the whole body), its exit and its
profile. The rise as a chain of skills, and its fast edge from hands and knees on toes, are built
on Kinetic ([Kinetic](2026-10-27-kinetic.md) step 8); this plan brings the game's bodies to it.

Defaults chosen here and open to the owner: a shove the body holds is given again (`SHOVED`,
`src/lab/ground-mode.ts`), and the skeleton is read beside the target, not held to it.

## Where it starts

- **The graph exists and is priced.** Step-up's route is five staged edges (`staged-roll`,
  `staged-down`, `staged-kneel-up`, `staged-step-through`, `staged-lunge`;
  [the staged route](../reference/rising.md#the-staged-route)) that hand over at their postures and
  in motion. Stand up (`standUp`, `src/core/mind/ground/stand-up.ts`) takes the route of least
  expected time from each edge's profile on the Rise drill
  ([routes priced](../reference/rising.md#routes-priced),
  [the Rise drill](../reference/rising.md#the-rise-drill); `assets/core/edge-profiles.json`,
  `src/core/mind/ground/profiles.ts`). The drill starts placed from each posture, kneeling
  included, and in motion after the edges before.
- **The hand-back**: Stand up's steady climbs from a crouch quiet on both flat feet in a median of
  1.41 s (the Warrior) and 1.56 s (the Rogue)
  ([a planned climb](../reference/rising.md#a-planned-climb)). A median fall has about 3.4 s from
  rest, so a fast edge ending quiet on flat feet within about 1.9 s of rest is within the target.
- **The graph against step-up** at `cbdf389a`: the staged route stands 52 and 53 of 64, step-up 56
  and 53; every group is within a fall or two but the Rogue with the club, 9 and 10 of 16 against
  step-up's 11 and 12. The fresh falls: the staged route 54 and 55, step-up 57 and 55. The rows the
  next step pairs with are `assets/evaluations/rising-target/2026-10-27-4d8f12c1-67b77fb2.jsonl`
  (the target) and `assets/evaluations/rising-target/2026-10-27-4d8f12c1-6b9dfbc6.jsonl` (the fresh
  falls).
- **The Rogue with the club**: down and the kneel-up are each done about half the times begun, the
  kneel-up 0 of 6 from its placed start; the roll from its left side is capped 10 of 24
  ([the pose stages](../reference/rising.md#the-pose-stages)).
- **No fast edge**: from hands and knees the bearing solve holds a pike on the toes with the hands
  bearing, and does not balance on the feet alone
  ([tried and not kept](../reference/rising.md#tried-and-not-kept)). Toes that bend are in the
  core ([toes](../reference/toes.md)); with them and the ankle's dorsiflexion stop at 0.8 rad, a
  stand-up from hands and knees stands 30 of 32 in a median of 3.98 s, against 0 of 32 without toes.
  The Warrior and the Rogue take them with step 2: on them step-up stands none of the target's 64
  falls ([on the game bodies](../reference/toes.md#on-the-game-bodies)).

`npm run evaluate -- rising-target` plays the 64 falls (a riser: `step-up`, `stand-up` with every
technique, or one technique), paired with the step before on the same falls. Any change reshuffles
which falls stand, so a small paired difference is read beside a placebo's (a change that should
move nothing).

## Steps

### 1. The Rogue with the club

The graph stands the Rogue with the club as step-up does: its down and kneel-up done from their
own postures, the roll from its left side not capped. Its edges are read for its own body, with no
strength raised; the arms that do not raise its chest are the first lead
([where the rise stops](../reference/rising.md#where-the-rise-stops)).

Check: on the Rise drill, the Rogue with the club's down and kneel-up done placed; on the target
and the fresh falls, its group at least step-up's (11 of 16), every other group within the
placebo's spread of the rows above.

### 2. The game's Stand up

Kinetic's rise ([Kinetic](2026-10-27-kinetic.md) step 8) replaces step-up in the Arena and the
Crypt; step-up is removed once no route falls back to it. With it the game's Warrior and Rogue take
the toed foot ([Kinetic](2026-10-27-kinetic.md) step 1). What toes move is answered in the same
change ([on the game bodies](../reference/toes.md#on-the-game-bodies)): the Vanguard's support fold asked
for the 0.30 m it reaches now (`COMBAT_FOLD.lower`); the swap test's takeover window short of the
toed ankle's sag; the learned punch on the toed body ([Kinetic](2026-10-27-kinetic.md) step 6);
shoves, shoved walks and the standard match read before and after.

Check: the target's every gate met; the fresh falls read against the same gates, a shortfall beyond
a placebo's spread reported as one; the skeleton's figures beside it; rising in fights
([checks](#checks)) no worse than step-up's; the owner looks in the Lab's Stand-up mode.

## Checks

- Every step: `npm run check` and `npm test`; a toolkit change, its message naming the cases the
  fingerprint moves.
- Unassisted: no `Assist` and no strength raised; a failure is reported as a failure.
- A run is priced before it runs.
- **The fresh falls**: the target's bodies and loadouts shoved at the sixteen directions halfway
  between the target's (11.25° off them), a case set of `rising-target` beside `standard`. No
  edge, timing or route is tuned on them; they show that a gain on the target is not fitted to its
  64 falls.
- **Rising in fights**: the standard match (`npm run match`) of the default mind against itself,
  once with step-up and once with the graph as its Stand up. Each time a fighter goes down: whether
  it stood, the time to standing, and whether it was struck or shoved while rising. Read at step 2
  for what the falls cannot show: a rise interrupted by a foe.

## Done when

`npm run evaluate -- rising-target` meets every gate with the game's Stand up, unassisted; the
fresh falls hold within a placebo's spread of it; rising in fights is no worse than step-up's; and
the owner accepts the look. Each edge is a skill in Kinetic's shape: a name, an entry predicate
its module exports, the resources it holds (the whole body), its exit (the posture it reaches) and
its profile from its drill.
