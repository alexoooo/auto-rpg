# Recovery: a graph of posture skills

## Why

The owner's target ([rising](../reference/rising.md#the-owners-target)): of 64 falls, at least 15
of 16 standing within 6 s in each body and loadout group, 61 of 64 overall, every cardinal way, a
failure-inclusive median of at most 4 s, unassisted. Young adults rise from the floor in a mean
1.4 to 2.6 s at a comfortable rate ([against people](../reference/rising.md#the-owners-target)).
The game's Stand up (`step-up`, `src/core/mind/ground/step-up.ts`) stands 51 of 64, none within
6 s, a median of 15.4 s, at `8e3df885` ([the budget](../reference/rising.md#the-budget)).

The time is the route's. Step-up is one technique from every position to standing (`TO_STANDING`)
that plays the whole staged recipe (`RISE`, `src/core/mind/rise/stages.ts`): it rolls onto its
front, comes onto hands and knees, kneels up, steps a foot through and lunges up, then the stance
steadies it. Of 45 stood falls at `0792d310`, the median seconds were 1.1 bracing and settling,
4.0 rolling, 1.6 coming onto hands and knees, 3.2 kneeling up, 0.9 stepping through, 2.2 lunging
and 2.7 steadied and verified ([where the rise stops](../reference/rising.md#where-the-rise-stops)).
Ending stages sooner, shorter routes from hands and knees and twice the strength did not shorten
it: the stages wait on balance, and none makes a movement that carries momentum
([tried and not kept](../reference/rising.md#tried-and-not-kept)).

- **The budget** ([the budget](../reference/rising.md#the-budget)). The target's clock holds the
  landing and the steadying as well as the rise. The body is first slow 0.6 s after takeover, so
  a median fall has about 3.4 s from there to verified standing. Step-up's hand-back from the end
  of its lunge takes 1.7 s, the standing check's 0.5 s of quiet included, leaving the rise about
  1.7 s, a young adult's comfortable rise. The stance alone from a squat takes 2.1 to 3.4 s, most
  of it the trunk settling after the climb: handed over in a squat, a rise has at most 1.3 s.
- **The roll is on the median's route.** Of the target's 64 falls, 27 lie on the back, 26 on a
  side and 11 on the front, and step-up rolls the back and side falls onto the front, 4.0 s. A
  fast route from hands and knees alone does not meet the median; the front falls, 5 of 11 stood,
  hold the per-group gate.
- **Whether a fast route exists is open.** The statics close every slow way from hands and knees
  to the feet but the bear, whose last move, the hands let go of the squat, holds at no height
  ([the verdict, by route](../reference/postures.md#the-verdict-by-route)); they cannot speak to
  momentum. Searched stand-ups have stood single falls in 5.8 to 7 s and failed fresh starts
  ([tried and not kept](../reference/rising.md#tried-and-not-kept)). So the body is asked for one
  witness, a trajectory within the budget, before the graph is built.

So the rise becomes a graph of posture skills. Its nodes are the safe postures (on the back, on
the side, on the front, hands and knees, kneeling, half kneeling, sitting, standing); its edges are
skills, slow ones that hand over at rest and fast ones that carry momentum
([handing over](2026-10-24-options.md#handing-over-at-rest-or-in-motion)). The fastest route whose
first edge's entry condition holds is taken, and a failed edge ends in a safe posture, from which
the graph routes again. The rise joins the skill set in the [Options](2026-10-24-options.md) shape:
a name, an entry condition, the resources it holds and a profile measured on its drill.

Defaults chosen here and open to the owner: a shove the body holds is given again (`SHOVED`,
`src/lab/ground-mode.ts`), and the skeleton is read beside the target, not held to it.

## Where it starts

- **Stand up already routes through a graph** (`standUp`, `src/core/mind/ground/stand-up.ts`): the
  body's position is read from its contacts (`Position`, `groundReading`,
  `src/core/mind/ground/reading.ts`); a technique (`TechniquePart`,
  `src/core/mind/ground/technique.ts`) declares its edges, whether it can begin now (`admits`, a
  method of its live run) and whether it has arrived (`reached`); failures are counted and retried,
  and a roll repositions a body no route stands up. The route is the first that admits in the
  config's order (`choose`, `src/core/mind/ground/graph.ts`), unpriced, on the settled position.
- **Between edges the supervisor brakes**: a body moving faster than 0.5 m/s or spinning faster
  than 1 rad/s is braced for up to a second (`BRACE_LIMIT`) before the next edge begins, so no
  edge yet hands over in motion.
- **The techniques** (`TECHNIQUES`, `src/core/mind/ground/techniques.ts`): `step-up`, the game's,
  from every position; experimental, none with a target reading in the records: `learned-rise`
  (every position), `plant-up` (front, hands and knees, half kneel, crouch to standing), `sit-up`
  (back to sitting), `post-up` (sitting to standing), `roll` (back or right side to front),
  `roll-back` (front to back).
- **Step-up has cut points**: it joins its recipe part-way where the body is read on hands and
  knees or half kneeling and its supports bear (`JOINS`); an attempt after a failed one rolls over
  the other side (worth 53 stood against 52).
- **A reading carries motion**: the centre of mass's velocity and the trunk's spin (`GroundReading`);
  the position's label settles over six readings (`SETTLE_STEPS`), its raw class at once.
- **One technique can be played alone** from a placed start, after another or not, with an 8 s cap
  and a standing check (`techniqueTrial`, `src/evaluation/rising.ts`). The placed starts: on the
  back, front and either side, and the Warrior's stored postures, hands and knees, half kneeling
  and squatting (`GROUND_POSTURES`, `src/lab/ground-starts.ts`;
  `assets/research/posture-holds.json`); none kneeling or sitting.
- **A search to reuse**: diagonal CMA-ES in the unit cube on worker threads (`research/cma.mjs`);
  `research/rising-step-search.mjs` searches step-up's stage timings with it on target falls, a
  fall scored 2 and the share of the cap left once verified standing, under 1 by how far it got.
- **What the body allows**, held still: hands and toes, hands and feet, and a squat with the hands
  down at 0.70 m; with the hands let go the squat is held at no height, the centre of mass 3 to
  17 cm ahead of the soles. Played as bearing stages after hands and knees, the knees never leave
  the ground, whatever the ankle allows: a shin's task holds its knee down
  ([tried and not kept](../reference/rising.md#tried-and-not-kept)). Without a toe joint the knees
  cannot leave the ground with the toes tucked, and sitting back onto the heels passes the knee's
  stop ([the verdict, by route](../reference/postures.md#the-verdict-by-route)). A toe joint fails
  on the solver ([passive toes](../reference/man-passive-toes.md)).
- The staged route's own steps (stage ends, a shorter route, the Rogue's roll) are read; their
  findings are in [rising](../reference/rising.md).

`npm run evaluate -- rising-target` plays the 64 falls (a riser: `step-up`, `stand-up` with every
technique, or one technique), so every step reads the whole target, paired with the step before
(the same falls). Any change reshuffles which falls stand, so a small paired difference is read
beside a placebo's (a change that should move nothing).

## Steps

### 1. The budget

Step-up on the target at the commit, its rows kept as the baseline every later step pairs with;
for each fall, the lie it lands in, the time from takeover to the body's first slow reading (under
0.5 m/s and 1 rad/s) and from the rise's end to verified standing. Then the stance alone from the
placed squat to verified standing, the Warrior and the Rogue. The allowance is 4 s less the median
time to slow: what a median fall has from rest to verified standing.

Check: a table by group of the falls landing on the back, front and side, the median time to slow,
the hand-back and the allowance ([the budget](../reference/rising.md#the-budget),
`research/rising-budget.mjs`).

### 2. Can a fast route exist

The question that decides the rest: does one trajectory of the body as built go from where falls
lie to verified standing within the allowance? One found is a witness, and the answer yes; a
search of a stated budget that finds none is the answer no.

- **The moves the statics leave to momentum**: the *push-off*, hands and knees to the stance (the
  hands drive the centre of mass back over the feet and the knees leave the ground with the hips);
  the *rock-up*, the back to the stance through a tuck and a swing.
- **The search** (`research/rise-witness.mjs`, [a fast route](../reference/rising.md#a-fast-route)):
  four phases, each open-loop (the pose drive) or closed-loop (plant-up's bearing solve on limbs
  given weight shares, the centre of mass driven at a place and a velocity), then Stand up's
  steady; one placed start, the Warrior empty-handed, calm; 100 generations of `research/cma.mjs`,
  priced from the first. A control from the squat, where the stance alone stands the body.
- **The answer**: a witness from the back within the allowance (about 3.4 s from rest), and the
  postures it passes through are the nodes steps 7 and 8 build edges between; none, and the owner
  gets each search's best trial and the body's options (a toe joint on the solver, the ankle's
  weight-bearing range).
- **Width is recorded, not gated**: each witness replayed from the next three seeds the placement
  accepts. An edge's feedback gives it width (step 8).

Check: a table by move of the best time to verified standing or how far the best trial got, its
width, and where its time goes; each witness kept as a record that replays.

### 3. The hand-back from a crouch

The gate on every fast route, landed. Stand up's steady climbs on one planned path from a crouch
quiet on both flat feet (`CLIMB`, `quietOnFeet`): from the combat fold it stands the Warrior in a
median of 1.41 s and the Rogue in 1.56 s, against 2.2 to 2.9 s for the stance's steady
([a planned climb](../reference/rising.md#a-planned-climb)), near the body's floor (0.5 s of
quiet after a climb of at least 0.5 to 0.6 s). The target is unchanged fall by fall: no rise hands
over in such a crouch yet (`assets/evaluations/rising-target/2026-10-27-0abb11b0-b727caf1.jsonl`).
From anywhere else the stance's steady catches the body, 2 s at best, and no climb on the stance
does better ([a climb on the stance](../reference/rising.md#a-climb-on-the-stance)).

What it asks of the fast edges (step 8): a median fall has 3.4 s from rest, so a rise that ends
quiet on both flat feet in a crouch within about 1.9 s of rest is within the allowance. Neither
witness does: the rock-up hands over on its toes at 2.7 s, the push-off with its feet off the
ground at 1.5 s.

### 4. The step-up cut at its safe postures

The slow edges the fast ones fail into, landed. Step-up's route is five techniques, one edge each,
with its stages and each body's timings (`STEP_UP`): `staged-roll`, `staged-down`,
`staged-kneel-up`, `staged-step-through` and `staged-lunge`
([the staged route](../reference/rising.md#the-staged-route)). The cuts are where the postures are
read (`CUTS`): hands and knees after `sit`, kneeling after `tall`, half kneeling after
`onto the toes`, read on the ball of a foot ahead of the knee. A part plays the route on from its
first stage and hands over at the first cut it reads, so a posture passed unread costs nothing.
Stand up lists them, then `step-up` (`STAGED_ROUTE`, the riser `staged`); the game keeps step-up
alone until step 10.

On the target, four runs each with placebo nudges, the staged route stands 49 to 54 of 64 and
step-up 50 to 54, every group within the placebo's spread, the Warrior empty-handed 0.25 s later
at the median: the rows step 5 pairs with are
`assets/evaluations/rising-target/2026-10-27-2c946c86-9b067999.jsonl`.

### 5. Edges that hand over in motion

Landed. A technique may declare that its entry reads the raw position and the body's motion
(`TechniquePart.moving`); Stand up begins one on a tumbling body where it admits it and braces only
a body none takes, waits for the position to be read before its first choice, and holds a body
still landing or too fast for a listed edge, no longer than a brace. The staged parts' entry is
`stagedEntry`: the rise's parts hand over under 1 m/s and 2 rad/s, the roll and down begin at the
body's step-up stillness ([the staged route](../reference/rising.md#the-staged-route)).

On the target, four runs with placebo nudges: 49 to 54 of 64, each group within step 4's spread but
the Warrior empty-handed at its edge (13 to 16 of 16, the median 0.2 s later); placed starts chain
through the parts, the Warrior 12 of 12 and the Rogue 6 of 8. The rows step 7 pairs with are
`assets/evaluations/rising-target/2026-10-27-d54daa41-2a1a71dd.jsonl`.

### 6. A drill for each edge

Landed. The Rise drill (`npm run evaluate -- rise`, [the Rise drill](../reference/rising.md#the-rise-drill))
plays each edge alone on techniques in turn: rung `placed` from each posture it is begun from
(kneeling added; no edge is begun sitting), rung `moving` after the route's edges before it. Its
summary is each edge's profile: begun, done and time, by body and by the speed it was begun at.
The kneel-up begins at `sit` on a body not yet sat back (`SAT_BACK`), so the Warrior kneels from
any hands and knees; the target is within the placebo's spread of step 5.

Every edge is done from its own posture placed but the Rogue's kneel-up, 0 of 6 (it rides the
sit's swing back): step 9's. The reel cannot be watched yet, as the Ratings reel plays bout cases
only; the owner's watching waits on a page that plays a technique case. The records are
`assets/evaluations/rise/2026-10-27-5ad20295-e43109c9.jsonl` (placed) and
`assets/evaluations/rise/2026-10-27-5ad20295-4beb3093.jsonl` (moving).

### 7. Routes priced

Landed. `choose` takes the route of least expected time to standing from the edges' profiles
([routes priced](../reference/rising.md#routes-priced)): an edge's trial time, then the route on
from its posture where done and from where its failures come to rest where not; the order decides
only where no profiled edge admits the body. The drill reads a failure where the body comes to
rest. The profiles are the Rise drill's at `07d287b0`.

With one staged edge from each position, the target is the same fall by fall as step 6, whose
placebo family is within step 5's spread; the fresh falls (`--protocol fresh`, now a case set)
stand 50 of 64. The rows step 8 pairs with are
`assets/evaluations/rising-target/2026-10-27-e3415f5b-2936d039.jsonl` (the target) and
`assets/evaluations/rising-target/2026-10-27-e3415f5b-636e13ea.jsonl` (the fresh falls).

### 8. The fast edges

Each witness of step 2 becomes edges at the postures it passes: its phases closed with feedback
where they are open-loop, an exported entry predicate, a drill entered placed and in motion (at the
end of down, mid-roll), handing back through step 3's steady. A rise's last edge ends in a crouch
quiet on both flat feet (`quietOnFeet`), where the steady's climb begins, within about 1.9 s of
rest. An edge stays where the target gains with it and the fresh falls lose nothing beyond a
placebo's spread; one the target never takes is removed.

Check: the target and the fresh falls paired against step 7; each edge's width on its drill.

Searched; no edge found ([tried and not kept](../reference/rising.md#tried-and-not-kept)): neither
witness, nor a search of the push-off from four starts a seed, ends quiet on flat feet. The bearing
solve holds a pike on the toes with the hands bearing, and does not balance on the feet alone. A
fast edge waits on a controller that brings the weight from the hands onto flat feet and balances
there; the target keeps step 7's route.

### 9. The Rogue

The Rogue with the club stands 9 of 16 ([step-up on the
target](../reference/rising.md#step-up-on-the-owners-target)); its arms do not raise its chest
([where the rise stops](../reference/rising.md#where-the-rise-stops)). Its edges are read for its
own body, with no strength raised.

Check: each Rogue group at 15 of 16.

Landed short of its check. Down tipped the Rogue onto a side in `tuck`; the tuck's arms out to the
sides ([the pose stages](../reference/rising.md#the-pose-stages)) stand the Rogue empty-handed 13 of
16 on the target and the fresh falls (11 and 11 before), the club 9 of 16 on both (10 and 13
before), the Warrior 15 to 16; step-up, the game's, 56 of 64 against 51. Not kept: the Rogue's
kneel-up timings searched on its drill (done 22 of 24 after down against 8, but its falls stood 11
fewer of 64), and the club arm less far out. What is left for the Rogue with the club: down and
the kneel-up each done about half the times begun, the roll from its left side capped 10 of 24, and
step-up from between positions 12 of 51. The rows step 10 pairs with are
`assets/evaluations/rising-target/2026-10-27-4d8f12c1-67b77fb2.jsonl` (the target) and
`assets/evaluations/rising-target/2026-10-27-4d8f12c1-6b9dfbc6.jsonl` (the fresh falls).

### 10. The game's Stand up

The graph replaces step-up in the Arena and the Crypt; step-up is removed once no route falls back
to it.

Check: the target's every gate met; the fresh falls read against the same gates, a shortfall
beyond a placebo's spread reported as one; the skeleton's figures beside it; rising in fights
([checks](#checks)) no worse than step-up's; the owner looks in the Lab's Stand-up mode.

## Checks

- Every step: `npm run check` and `npm test`; a toolkit change, its message naming the cases the
  fingerprint moves.
- Unassisted: no `Assist` and no strength raised; a failure is reported as a failure.
- A run is priced before it runs.
- **The fresh falls**: the target's bodies and loadouts shoved at the sixteen directions halfway
  between the target's (11.25° off them), a case set of `rising-target` beside `standard`. No
  edge, timing or route is tuned on them; they are read at steps 7, 8 and 10 to show that a gain
  on the target is not fitted to its 64 falls.
- **Rising in fights**: the standard match (`npm run match`) of the default mind against itself,
  once with step-up and once with the graph as its Stand up. Each time a fighter goes down: whether
  it stood, the time to standing, and whether it was struck or shoved while rising. Read at step 10
  for what the falls cannot show: a rise interrupted by a foe.

## Done when

`npm run evaluate -- rising-target` meets every gate with the game's Stand up, unassisted; the
fresh falls hold within a placebo's spread of it; rising in fights is no worse than step-up's; and
the owner accepts the look. Each edge is a skill in the Options shape: a name, an entry predicate its
module exports, the resources it holds (the whole body) and its profile from its drill.

## Relations

- [Options](2026-10-24-options.md): a posture skill is a skill in its shape; an edge's `reached` is
  its exit, which the [skill chaining](../analysis/2026-10-26-skill-chaining.md) analysis
  proposes for every skill, and routes priced (step 7) are its ground edges priced. The Options
  plan names the rise's pose drive a second control stack beside the skills' motor control; the
  edges keep it until a step here reads one on the other.
- [Stand-up planning](../analysis/2026-10-11-stand-up-planning.md): its next component, a
  transition under hand support, is step 2's push-off; whether Research rise stays selectable is
  decided at step 10.
