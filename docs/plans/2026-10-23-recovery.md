# Recovery: a graph of posture skills

## Why

The owner's target ([rising](../reference/rising.md#the-owners-target)): of 64 falls, at least 15
of 16 standing within 6 s in each body and loadout group, 61 of 64 overall, every cardinal way, a
failure-inclusive median of at most 4 s, unassisted. Young adults rise from the floor in a mean
1.4 to 2.6 s at a comfortable rate ([against people](../reference/rising.md#the-owners-target)).
The game's Stand up (`step-up`, `src/core/mind/ground/step-up.ts`) stands 51 of 64, none within
6 s, a median of 15.4 s, at `8e3df885` ([the budget](../reference/rising.md#the-budget)).

The time is the route's. Step-up is one technique from every position to standing (`TO_STANDING`)
that plays the whole staged recipe (`RISE`, `src/core/mind/rise/stages.ts`): it rolls onto its
front, comes onto hands and knees, kneels up, steps a foot through and lunges up, then the stance
steadies it. Of 45 stood falls at `0792d310`, the median seconds were 1.1 bracing and settling,
4.0 rolling, 1.6 coming onto hands and knees, 3.2 kneeling up, 0.9 stepping through, 2.2 lunging
and 2.7 steadied and verified ([where the rise stops](../reference/rising.md#where-the-rise-stops)).
Ending stages sooner, shorter routes from hands and knees and twice the strength did not shorten
it: the stages wait on balance, and none makes a movement that carries momentum
([tried and not kept](../reference/rising.md#tried-and-not-kept)).

- **The budget** ([the budget](../reference/rising.md#the-budget)). The target's clock holds the
  landing and the steadying as well as the rise. The body is first slow 0.6 s after takeover, so
  a median fall has about 3.4 s from there to verified standing. Step-up's hand-back from the end
  of its lunge takes 1.7 s, the standing check's 0.5 s of quiet included, leaving the rise about
  1.7 s, a young adult's comfortable rise. The stance alone from a squat takes 2.1 to 3.4 s, most
  of it the trunk settling after the climb: handed over in a squat, a rise has at most 1.3 s.
- **The roll is on the median's route.** Of the target's 64 falls, 27 lie on the back, 26 on a
  side and 11 on the front, and step-up rolls the back and side falls onto the front, 4.0 s. A
  fast route from hands and knees alone does not meet the median; the front falls, 5 of 11 stood,
  hold the per-group gate.
- **Whether a fast route exists is open.** The statics close every slow way from hands and knees
  to the feet but the bear, whose last move, the hands let go of the squat, holds at no height
  ([the verdict, by route](../reference/postures.md#the-verdict-by-route)); they cannot speak to
  momentum. Searched stand-ups have stood single falls in 5.8 to 7 s and failed fresh starts
  ([tried and not kept](../reference/rising.md#tried-and-not-kept)). So the body is asked for one
  witness, a trajectory within the budget, before the graph is built.

So the rise becomes a graph of posture skills. Its nodes are the safe postures (on the back, on
the side, on the front, hands and knees, kneeling, half kneeling, sitting, standing); its edges are
skills, slow ones that hand over at rest and fast ones that carry momentum
([handing over](2026-10-24-options.md#handing-over-at-rest-or-in-motion)). The fastest route whose
first edge's entry condition holds is taken, and a failed edge ends in a safe posture, from which
the graph routes again. The rise joins the skill set in the [Options](2026-10-24-options.md) shape:
a name, an entry condition, the resources it holds and a profile measured on its drill.

Defaults chosen here and open to the owner: a shove the body holds is given again (`SHOVED`,
`src/lab/ground-mode.ts`), and the skeleton is read beside the target, not held to it.

## Where it starts

- **Stand up already routes through a graph** (`standUp`, `src/core/mind/ground/stand-up.ts`): the
  body's position is read from its contacts (`Position`, `groundReading`,
  `src/core/mind/ground/reading.ts`); a technique (`TechniquePart`,
  `src/core/mind/ground/technique.ts`) declares its edges, whether it can begin now (`admits`, a
  method of its live run) and whether it has arrived (`reached`); failures are counted and retried,
  and a roll repositions a body no route stands up. The route is the first that admits in the
  config's order (`choose`, `src/core/mind/ground/graph.ts`), unpriced, on the settled position.
- **Between edges the supervisor brakes**: a body moving faster than 0.5 m/s or spinning faster
  than 1 rad/s is braced for up to a second (`BRACE_LIMIT`) before the next edge begins, so no
  edge yet hands over in motion.
- **The techniques** (`TECHNIQUES`, `src/core/mind/ground/techniques.ts`): `step-up`, the game's,
  from every position; experimental, none with a target reading in the records: `learned-rise`
  (every position), `plant-up` (front, hands and knees, half kneel, crouch to standing), `sit-up`
  (back to sitting), `post-up` (sitting to standing), `roll` (back or right side to front),
  `roll-back` (front to back).
- **Step-up has cut points**: it joins its recipe part-way where the body is read on hands and
  knees or half kneeling and its supports bear (`JOINS`); an attempt after a failed one rolls over
  the other side (worth 53 stood against 52).
- **A reading carries motion**: the centre of mass's velocity and the trunk's spin (`GroundReading`);
  the position's label settles over six readings (`SETTLE_STEPS`), its raw class at once.
- **One technique can be played alone** from a placed start, after another or not, with an 8 s cap
  and a standing check (`techniqueTrial`, `src/evaluation/rising.ts`). The placed starts: on the
  back, front and either side, and the Warrior's stored postures, hands and knees, half kneeling
  and squatting (`GROUND_POSTURES`, `src/lab/ground-starts.ts`;
  `assets/research/posture-holds.json`); none kneeling or sitting.
- **A search to reuse**: diagonal CMA-ES in the unit cube on worker threads (`research/cma.mjs`);
  `research/rising-step-search.mjs` searches step-up's stage timings with it on target falls, a
  fall scored 2 and the share of the cap left once verified standing, under 1 by how far it got.
- **What the body allows**, held still: hands and toes, hands and feet, and a squat with the hands
  down at 0.70 m; with the hands let go the squat is held at no height, the centre of mass 3 to
  17 cm ahead of the soles. Played as bearing stages after hands and knees, the knees never leave
  the ground, whatever the ankle allows: a shin's task holds its knee down
  ([tried and not kept](../reference/rising.md#tried-and-not-kept)). Without a toe joint the knees
  cannot leave the ground with the toes tucked, and sitting back onto the heels passes the knee's
  stop ([the verdict, by route](../reference/postures.md#the-verdict-by-route)). A toe joint fails
  on the solver ([passive toes](../reference/man-passive-toes.md)).
- The staged route's own steps (stage ends, a shorter route, the Rogue's roll) are read; their
  findings are in [rising](../reference/rising.md).

`npm run evaluate -- rising-target` plays the 64 falls (a riser: `step-up`, `stand-up` with every
technique, or one technique), so every step reads the whole target, paired with the step before
(the same falls). Any change reshuffles which falls stand, so a small paired difference is read
beside a placebo's (a change that should move nothing).

## Steps

### 1. The budget

Step-up on the target at the commit, its rows kept as the baseline every later step pairs with;
for each fall, the lie it lands in, the time from takeover to the body's first slow reading (under
0.5 m/s and 1 rad/s) and from the rise's end to verified standing. Then the stance alone from the
placed squat to verified standing, the Warrior and the Rogue. The allowance is 4 s less the median
time to slow: what a median fall has from rest to verified standing.

Check: a table by group of the falls landing on the back, front and side, the median time to slow,
the hand-back and the allowance ([the budget](../reference/rising.md#the-budget),
`research/rising-budget.mjs`).

### 2. Can a fast route exist

The question that decides the rest: does one trajectory of the body as built go from where falls
lie to verified standing within the allowance? One found is a witness, and the answer yes; a
search of a stated budget that finds none is the answer no.

- **The moves the statics leave to momentum**, each searched alone:
  - *push-off*, hands and knees to the stance: the hands drive the centre of mass back over the
    feet, the knees leave the ground with the hips, and the feet come under flat: a hop, since the
    knees cannot leave the ground with the toes tucked;
  - *rock-up*, the back to the stance through sitting: the legs swing down and the trunk forward,
    so momentum carries the centre of mass over the soles;
  - *roll and push-off*, the back or either side to the stance through hands and knees: a roll
    that ends in motion inside the push-off, seeded with the push-off's witness.
- **The hand-back is searched with the move**: the last keyframe may be at standing height, the
  stance taking only the quiet, since the stance from a squat leaves a rise at most 1.3 s. A
  witness's time is split at the hand-back, so a route that fails the allowance only on the
  stance's settling is read as such.
- **The search** (`research/rise-witness.mjs`, [a fast route](../reference/rising.md#a-fast-route)):
  open-loop keyframes of the pose drive, each a seed posture with the trunk and the limbs' main
  freedoms searched, a duration that may be none, a rate and an activation; then Stand up's
  steady. One placed start, the Warrior empty-handed, calm; 100 generations of
  `research/cma.mjs`, priced from the first. A control from the squat, where the stance alone
  stands the body, checks that the search finds what exists.
- **Read**, in order: the push-off first (front and side falls need it, and the roll and push-off
  starts from its witness), then the rock-up, then the roll and push-off if the push-off has a
  witness. A route's time is its witness's, from the start at rest to verified standing,
  beside the allowance (about 3.4 s).
- **The answer**:
  - a witness from the back and one from a side within the allowance: a fast route exists, and the postures its
    keyframes pass through are the nodes steps 6 and 7 build edges between;
  - witnesses, none from the back or none from a side within the allowance: the median is out of reach on this body;
    the 6 s gate's routes are still built;
  - none: the plan stops. The owner gets each search's best trial, where it failed (the contact
    or joint stop that ended it) and the body's options (a toe joint on the solver, the ankle's
    weight-bearing range).
- **Width is recorded, not gated**: each witness replayed from three neighbouring placed starts
  (the next three seeds the placement accepts). An open-loop trajectory need not have width; an edge's feedback gives it
  (step 7).
- **The Rogue** is searched only where the Warrior has a witness, seeded with it.

Check: a table by body and move of the evaluations played, the best time to verified standing or
how far the best trial got, its width, and each route's time beside the allowance; each witness
kept as a record that replays.

### 3. The step-up cut at its safe postures

The slow edges the fast ones fail into. Step-up's recipe becomes five techniques, one edge each,
keeping its stages and each body's timings (`STEP_UP`): `staged-roll` (on the back or either side
to the front, the retry over the other side kept), `staged-down` (the front to hands and knees),
`staged-kneel-up` (hands and knees to kneeling), `staged-step-through` (kneeling to half kneeling)
and `staged-lunge` (half kneeling to standing, with the stance's climb and steadying). Each admits
where its supports bear, as the cut points do now; a cut whose posture is not read where its
stages end (the tall kneel as `kneeling`, the end of `reach` as `half-kneel`) moves to where it is.
Stand up lists them in route order, then `step-up` as the fallback from what they do not cover
(between, sitting, crouching), until step 9.

Check: on the target, each group's stood falls and median paired against step 1's rows and a
placebo; no group worse beyond the placebo's spread.

### 4. Edges that hand over in motion

The supervisor stops bracing within a route: an edge's entry reads the raw position and the body's
motion, so the next edge can begin before the body is still, and only a body tumbling outside every
edge's entry is braced. Each edge's entry condition is a predicate its module exports, as the
[Options](2026-10-24-options.md) shape asks.

Check: the target paired against step 3; a placed start whose edges chain at rest still stands.

### 5. A drill for each edge

The Rise family of the [Drills](2026-10-24-drills.md) plan (its step 8), on `techniqueTrial`: each
edge from its posture placed (kneeling and sitting starts added) and in motion, entered from the
edge before (`after`) at the speeds that edge leaves it; a trial ends done or in the posture it
fell to, with its time. Its record summarised is the edge's profile: success and time by the
entry's motion.

Check: each edge done from its own posture placed; the owner watches the reel, the failures first.

### 6. Routes priced

`choose` takes the route of least expected time to standing from the edges' profiles: an edge's
time over its success, with the route from where its failures end. Order in the config stops
deciding.

Check: the target paired against step 4; no group worse beyond a placebo's spread.

### 7. The fast edges, and the hand-back

Each witness of step 2 becomes edges at the postures it passes: its keyframes closed with feedback
(the bearing solve on its supports, the stance at the hand-back), an exported entry predicate, a
drill entered placed and in motion (at the end of down, mid-roll). The sit-up and post-up compete
beside them. An edge stays where the target gains with it; one the target never takes is removed.
The last edge hands the body to the stance at height and quiet, so the steadying and verification
shrink toward the hand-back's floor.

Check: the target paired against step 6; the time from the last edge's end to verified standing.

### 8. The Rogue

The Rogue with the club stands 9 of 16 ([step-up on the
target](../reference/rising.md#step-up-on-the-owners-target)); its arms do not raise its chest
([where the rise stops](../reference/rising.md#where-the-rise-stops)). Its edges are read for its
own body, with no strength raised.

Check: each Rogue group at 15 of 16.

### 9. The game's Stand up

The graph replaces step-up in the Arena and the Crypt; step-up is removed once no route falls back
to it.

Check: the target's every gate met; the skeleton's figures beside it; the owner looks in the Lab's
Stand-up mode.

## Checks

- Every step: `npm run check` and `npm test`; a toolkit change, its message naming the cases the
  fingerprint moves.
- Unassisted: no `Assist` and no strength raised; a failure is reported as a failure.
- A run is priced before it runs.

## Done when

`npm run evaluate -- rising-target` meets every gate with the game's Stand up, unassisted, and the
owner accepts the look. Each edge is a skill in the Options shape: a name, an entry predicate its
module exports, the resources it holds (the whole body) and its profile from its drill.

## Relations

- [Options](2026-10-24-options.md): a posture skill is a skill in its shape; an edge's `reached` is
  its exit, which the [skill chaining](../analysis/2026-10-26-skill-chaining.md) analysis
  proposes for every skill, and routes priced (step 6) are its ground edges priced. The Options
  plan names the rise's pose drive a second control stack beside the skills' motor control; the
  edges keep it until a step here reads one on the other.
- [Stand-up planning](../analysis/2026-10-11-stand-up-planning.md): its next component, a
  transition under hand support, is step 2's push-off; whether Research rise stays selectable is
  decided at step 9.
