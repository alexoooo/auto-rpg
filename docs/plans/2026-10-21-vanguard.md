# Vanguard

## Why

Build a separate fighter that wins more than 80% of its bouts against each existing opponent,
first empty-handed, then with equipment. The win requirement is per opponent and loadout; an
aggregate rating does not satisfy it. Actual injury wins and health leads at the bell are reported
separately.

## Steps with checks

1. Develop the unarmed fighter on one Warrior against the strongest unarmed opponents. Keep
   anatomy, attributes, assists, physics and wound rules fixed. Each candidate answers one question;
   retain its cases and compare against the preceding implementation or the Counter-puncher.
   Its punch is an executor part of its own (`vanguard-punch`,
   `src/core/entrants/vanguard/punch-part.ts`) that follows one sensed body point through the
   stroke instead of choosing a new surface every step; its `predict` setting leads that point
   with its currently sensed rigid motion. Its layers are its planner part (`vanguard-plan`,
   `plan.ts`), among them the contact-driven re-evaluation that compares attack and slow evasion
   between strokes, periodically retrying a policy whose early trial was quiet. The Skeleton's
   `vanguard-exchange` child planner learns per-hand contact scores per attempted second. Each part is read
   by replacing it alone (`--ablate`). Select the explicit `vanguard-warrior`, `vanguard-rogue` or
   `vanguard-skeleton` calibration; the selected settings run unchanged. The Lab drives the same
   kit, and each planner layer reports its decision through the shared inspection details. The
   Warrior's `vanguard-navigation` gives its approach extra obstacle room; it replaces or ablates
   through the navigation slot, without changing physical clearance or collision geometry.
2. Exercise the successful candidate against every existing mind, with mirrored sides, on the
   Warrior, Rogue and Skeleton and both Arena levels. Include the Reptile on its own body. Vary
   starting distances, retain misses and falls, and use fresh conditions after development.
3. Use equipment through the same mind and physical control path: held-item recipes through
   `vanguard-weapon`, and shared blade cuts and thrusts. Screen each representative
   loadout, then the remaining catalog assignments that both contestants support. Compare against
   the strongest existing mind on each kit and widen only where needed.
4. Freeze the candidate and read held-out cases by opponent, loadout, body and level. Report actual
   wins, losses, draws, endings and uncertainty. Repeated deterministic cases count once.
5. Land each useful change with `npm run check`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, the entrant scope
   check and the line-ending gate. Briefly inspect visible changes in the browser. Keep the
   fighter experimental until its results meet the target.

Runs use the Node Arena Duel, vendored Rapier, 120 Hz, default character attributes and Balance
0/0. Humanoid mind comparisons use matched bodies and equipment; the Reptile comparison names
each body. The primary rule continues after falls to the Arena's ordinary two-minute cap.
Other fall rules and equal assists are separate checks, with their conditions stated. A run's
first cases price its remaining work; cut or ask above about ten minutes, and ask before any
single run exceeds an hour. Rows are retained under `research/runs/` and reused by case key.

## Done when

- The frozen fighter wins more than 80% of held-out bouts against every existing opponent, first
  empty-handed and then for each supported equipment assignment, with both levels and the named
  body variations represented.
- The report states each opponent's results and uncertainty, coverage and failures; a draw is
  not a win and an invalid case is not a loss or silently discarded.
- Physical attacks account for its wins, its missed blows recover under control, and it uses
  only its granted senses and bodily actuation.
- Required checks pass, the owner can select the fighter in Arena, and temporary servers and work
  are cleaned up. This plan is deleted when the work lands.
