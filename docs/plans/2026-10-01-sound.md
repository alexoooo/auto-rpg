# Sound from touches and air

## Context

The game voices one thing: a blow one fighter lands on another (`watchBlows` -> `blowCue` ->
`GameAudio.cue`). The Lab has one body, so it is silent. The owner wants the Lab to sound --
landings, footfalls, falls, the air of a swing, the shove -- and wants it general: no per-sound
trigger scattered through the scenarios. Scope chosen: the Lab first, then the arena and the crypt
on the same path, and the blow-only path removed.

So sound becomes two physical rules, the same on every screen:

1. **A sound is a touch.** When a segment of a body meets another body, or something fixed, that
   it was not in contact with, and is closing on it, that is a sound: as loud as the energy the
   touch carries (`impactEnergy`, which damage already uses) and in the voice of the softer of
   the two surfaces. A footfall is a foot meeting the ground; a fall is the trunk, head and limbs
   meeting it; a blow and a clash are what they were. A body walking into a wall sounds, unasked.
2. **Air.** Each body has one voice that follows the speed of its fastest extremity: a leaf of
   its own tree, with what it holds. No list of hands.

Nothing is triggered from a stance phase, a fallen flag or a strike phase.

## What was measured before this was written

Node stand, Rapier, 120 Hz, balance 0 % (assist off), one run each; ground contacts read from the
raw Rapier world as chunk 1 will report them. Chunk 2 reads it again through the real interface and
that table is the record.

- **Standing is silent**: 10 s, all three models, no new closing touch at all.
- **"The solver pushed" flickers; "in contact" does not.** A walking foot is pushed on only 64 to
  72 % of its steps down, so "new since the solver last pushed" gives 33 to 57 touches for 26
  strides. "New since the two were last in contact" gives 25 for 26 strides walking and 74 to 79
  for 79 in the Run, on all three models, with no tuned number. No energy floor or speed threshold
  separates the extras. So a touch lasts while the two are in contact.
- **Energies**: a human footfall 0.20 to 0.33 J; a skeleton's 0.020 to 0.041 J (under today's
  floor, 0.0735 J: it walks silent); the first footfall setting off 0.019 to 0.027 J; blows in
  three bouts 0.004 to 54 J, medians 0.4 to 9; a fall's trunk landing 5 to 57 J (a skeleton's to
  120 J); a club on the floor 3.5 to 9.7 J.
- **The mass a contact meets** is at most 0.39 of the body's, so skipping by a whole-body bound
  saves almost nothing; a touch is simply priced. `contactMass.update()` is 0.2 to 1.4 ms: once a
  body a step, not once a touch.
- **Far-point speeds**: walking and the Run stay under 3.5 m/s (the feet); a striking fist's far
  point 12.8 to 16.8 m/s; a club's end 19 to 21 m/s; a club's return to guard to 9.4 m/s.
- **A fallen body driven on by its stance thrashes**: 50 or more touches in 4 s, hands and feet
  at 10 to 19 m/s, single touches to 100 J (`docs/reference/rising.md#driven`). That was read
  before a fallen body lay still (`docs/reference/rising.md#lying`); chunk 2's table reads the
  fall again.

## What stays special, and why

- The **shove** is the page's hand on the world (an impulse, no body), and the **Blow scenario's
  head** is a mark the club passes through (`watchClubBlow`). Neither is a contact; each reports
  through the same cue function, one call.
- Only the Lab replays, so only the Lab keeps a **log** of what was heard, against the mind's time.
- **A blow keeps its own rule for how long a touch lasts** (while the solver pushes), because the
  bouts were fought under it and changing it changes them. Sound uses contact. It is one named
  option of the one reader, and an item on the roadmap.

## Decisions the owner may want to reverse (most are judged by ear, once it plays)

- **A fallen body is heard doing what it does.** Whatever it does on the ground is touches and
  air, and nothing here mutes it.
- **The arena still goes silent at its verdict**, which today comes as a body starts down, so a
  bout-ending fall is not heard there; the crypt stops its world when a run ends. Falls are heard
  in the Lab, and in a fight once a fall no longer ends it (Rising 05).
- **The softer surface decides the voice** (flesh, bone, wood, stone, in that order). Today the
  struck body decides and any clash is the wood knock. What changes: fist on fist, and a club on a
  bare hand, become the struck hand's voice (were wood); a flesh fist on a skeleton becomes a
  thump (was bone); two skeletons' fists become bone (was wood). Club on club is still wood.
- **A body and an item say what they are made of in their specs** (`BodySpec.surface`,
  `ItemSpec.surface`), not in a table of names in the audio module.
- **A hand that holds something sounds as what it holds**, whichever of the two met: they are one
  rigid body.
- **Everything fixed is stone.**
- **No new impact sound.** A footfall is a quiet thump or bone tick. A footstep of its own later
  is one more voice, not a new mechanism.
- **`CUE.floor` goes from .035 to .017** (0.0173 J), under the quietest footfall measured, so a
  skeleton's walk is heard. `CUE.joules` stays 60: the hardest blow measured is 54 J, and a hard
  fall saturates with it.
- **The mixer's line is unchanged**: a touch plays at `MIX.impact.floor + perStrength x strength`,
  so a footfall (strength .06 to .07) plays at about half a middling blow's gain. If footfalls are
  too loud, `MIX.impact.floor` is the one number.
- **A body's touch with itself is not heard** (9 to 17 a second in a bout).
- The volume control sits at the end of the Lab's transport bar; it is the game's one setting.
- The `swish` loop's formula and `MIX.swish` are written unheard.

```ts
/** **A body's contact with another body or with a fixed collider**, as the last step left it:
 *  one for each thing it is in contact with, whatever colliders met. */
export interface Contact {
  /** The body; null when it is a fixed collider. */
  readonly other: SegmentBody | null;
  /** The fixed collider, when `other` is null: a number that is that collider's for as long as it exists. */
  readonly fixed: number | null;
  readonly point: Vec3;      // pushed: as now. Not pushed: the solver contact points' mean.
  readonly normal: Vec3;     // pushed: as now. Not pushed: the manifolds' normals' mean, unit.
  /** N s; 0 when the two are in contact and the solver pushed nothing. */
  readonly impulse: number;
}
```

`contactsOf` in `rapier.ts`:
- **In contact** is a collider pair with a solver contact point (`numSolverContacts() > 0`: touching,
  or within the solver's prediction distance). **Pushed** is what it is now: a positive impulse.
- The pushed sums keep their arithmetic and their order: the same accumulation, the same `Map`,
  now keyed by the body or by the fixed collider's handle. A second map gathers what was seen.
- Out: the pushed bodies and fixed colliders in the order they were first pushed, as now; then
  those in contact and not pushed, with impulse 0. Each fixed collider is its own contact, so a
  floor's normal is never blended with a wall's.

So a blow reads the contacts it read, in the order it read them; the fingerprint proves it.

### `src/core/touches.ts`

The reading lifted from `watchBlows`, its arithmetic and order unchanged:

```ts
/** A segment of a body that is watched. */
export interface Part<B> { readonly body: B; readonly segment: BuiltSegment }
/** **A new touch**: a segment met another watched body's, or something fixed (`on` null), and was closing on it. */
export interface Touch<B> {
  readonly time: number;                 // the world's clock when it landed, s
  readonly of: Part<B>;
  readonly on: Part<B> | null;
  readonly point: Vec3;                  // world, m
  readonly normal: Vec3;                 // `of` into `on`
  readonly closing: number;              // m/s, from the velocities as the step before left them
}
export interface TouchWatch<B> {
  readonly state: object;                // { touching: Set<string>, last: [...] }, as `BlowState` had them
  /** The masses `touch` meets (`contactMass`; `Infinity` for something fixed) and its energy, J.
   *  Read from the bodies as they are: good only while the touch is being heard. */
  priced(touch: Touch<B>): { readonly ofKg: number; readonly onKg: number; readonly energy: number };
  dispose(): void;
}
export function watchTouches<B extends { readonly built: BuiltBody }>(world: World, bodies: readonly B[], options: {
  /** The segments whose contacts are read, in this order; all of a body's, in its own order, if not given. */
  readonly segments?: readonly string[];
  /** How long a touch lasts: `pushed`, while the solver pushes on it (one let go of for a step
   *  lands again: a blow's rule); `contact`, while the two are in contact (it lands again only
   *  once they have parted). */
  readonly lasts: "pushed" | "contact";
  /** Whether a contact is one to read at all, asked before it is remembered. */
  counts(of: Part<B>, on: Part<B> | null): boolean;
}, heard: (touch: Touch<B>) => void): TouchWatch<B>
```

The `afterStep` hook, for each body, each read segment, each of `contactsOf`:
1. `on`: the part whose body it is, or null for a fixed collider; a body that is not watched is skipped.
2. `counts(of, on)`, or skip.
3. The key: the two parts' places, or the part's place and `f<fixed>`.
4. If it is pushed, or `lasts` is `contact`: the key into `now`.
5. Not pushed, or the key in `touching`: skip.
6. `closing` from `last` (something fixed does not move); not over 0: skip.
7. `heard(touch)`, inline: what it does (a wound) changes later `counts` in the same step.

Then `touching = now` and `remember()`. With `lasts: "pushed"` this is `watchBlows`'s loop.
`priced` updates each body's `contactMass` once a step (the pose does not change between touches
of one step, so the numbers are the ones a fresh `update()` gives), then `along`, `along`,
`impactEnergy`, in that order. `EXACT_MATH` holds here (`tests/core-boundary.test.mjs`).

`watchBlows` passes `segments: STRIKERS`, `lasts: "pushed"`, and `counts`: `of`'s fighter standing
with the striker attached, `on` another side's standing fighter. A fallen fighter's hands are read
and count nothing, which is what skipping them did. Its `heard` prices, wounds, and freezes a
`LandedBlow` that copies `point`, `normal` and `closing` out of the touch: the touch names the
fighters, and `deepFreeze` would walk into them.

### `src/audio/body-sounds.ts`

```ts
/** A body that is heard: its key in a cue, and what it was built as. */
export interface Heard { readonly id: string; readonly built: BuiltBody }

 *  fixed, once a pair, as its cue. */
export function hearTouches(world: World, bodies: readonly Heard[], heard: (cue: SoundCue, touch: Touch<Heard>) => void): { dispose(): void }
```

- `airOf`: the leaves are the segments that are no joint's parent. A leaf's points are `lowsOf` of

  time it is asked: no end is chosen ahead (the end of a club farthest from the hand's centre of
