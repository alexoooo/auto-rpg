# Sound from touches and air

## Context

The game voices one thing: a blow one fighter lands on another (`watchBlows` -> `blowCue` ->
`GameAudio.cue`). The Lab has one body, so it is silent. The owner wants the Lab to sound --
landings, footfalls, falls, the air of a swing, the shove -- and wants it general: no per-sound
trigger scattered through the scenarios. Scope chosen: the Lab first, then the arena and the crypt
on the same path, and the blow-only path removed.

So sound becomes two physical rules, the same on every screen:

1. **A sound is a touch.** When a segment of a body meets another body, or something fixed, that
   it was not in contact with, and is closing on it, that is a sound: as loud as the energy the
   touch carries (`impactEnergy`, which damage already uses) and in the voice of the softer of
   the two surfaces. A footfall is a foot meeting the ground; a fall is the trunk, head and limbs
   meeting it; a blow and a clash are what they were. A body walking into a wall sounds, unasked.
2. **Air.** Each body has one voice that follows the speed of its fastest extremity: a leaf of
   its own tree, with what it holds. No list of hands.

Nothing is triggered from a stance phase, a fallen flag or a strike phase.

## What is in, and what was measured

The engine names every contact, one reader reads touches (`src/core/touches.ts`), blows are built
on it, the audio module hears bodies (`src/audio/body-sounds.ts`, `src/audio/cues.ts`,
`GameAudio.swish`), and the Lab plays them from its log (`src/lab/sound-log.ts`). The record is `docs/reference/look.md#sound`, with its
table (`research/touches.mjs`): standing is silent, a walk is a touch a footfall, a fall is a
second or three of landing and then stillness, and a body's fastest point walks under 5 m/s and
swings a club's end at 20.

What is left is the arena and the crypt (chunk 5).

## What stays special, and why

- The **shove** is the page's hand on the world (an impulse, no body), and the **Blow scenario's
  head** is a mark the club passes through (`watchClubBlow`). Neither is a contact; each reports
  through the same cue function, one call.
- Only the Lab replays, so only the Lab keeps a **log** of what was heard, against the mind's time.
- **A blow keeps its own rule for how long a touch lasts** (while the solver pushes), because the
  bouts were fought under it and changing it changes them. Sound uses contact. It is one named
  option of the one reader, and an item on the roadmap.

## Decisions the owner may want to reverse (most are judged by ear, once it plays)

- **A fallen body is heard doing what it does.** Whatever it does on the ground is touches and
  air, and nothing here mutes it.
- **The arena still goes silent at its verdict**, which today comes as a body starts down, so a
  bout-ending fall is not heard there; the crypt stops its world when a run ends. Falls are heard
  in the Lab, and in a fight once a fall no longer ends it (Rising 05).
- **The softer surface decides the voice** (flesh, bone, wood, stone, in that order). Today the
  struck body decides and any clash is the wood knock. What changes: fist on fist, and a club on a
  bare hand, become the struck hand's voice (were wood); a flesh fist on a skeleton becomes a
  thump (was bone); two skeletons' fists become bone (was wood). Club on club is still wood.
- **A body and an item say what they are made of in their specs** (`BodySpec.surface`,
  `ItemSpec.surface`), not in a table of names in the audio module.
- **A hand that holds something sounds as what it holds**, whichever of the two met: they are one
  rigid body.
- **Everything fixed is stone.**
- **No new impact sound.** A footfall is a quiet thump or bone tick. A footstep of its own later
  is one more voice, not a new mechanism.
- **`CUE.floor` is .0125** (0.0094 J), between a sole laid down and the quietest footfall
  measured, so a skeleton's walk is heard. `CUE.joules` stays 60: the hardest blow measured is
  54 J, and a hard fall saturates with it.
- **The mixer's line is unchanged**: a touch plays at `MIX.impact.floor + perStrength x strength`,
  so a footfall (strength .02 to .07) plays at about a third of a middling blow's gain. If footfalls are
  too loud, `MIX.impact.floor` is the one number.
- **A body's touch with itself is not heard** (9 to 17 a second in a bout).
- The volume control sits at the end of the Lab's transport bar; it is the game's one setting.
- The `swish` loop's formula and `MIX.swish` are written unheard.
- **A low catching step is not heard**: one that stays within the engine's 2 cm contact margin is
  no new touch (24 recoveries from shoves, 14 footfalls). It is on the roadmap with what would
  hear it.

## What must not move

- **No bout changes by a bit**: `node scripts/fingerprint.mjs` and `node research/bout-trace.mjs`
  print the same lines before a chunk and after it.
- A listener only reads. A headless bout has none and pays nothing.
- `watchClubBlow`'s reading (`tests/lab-blow.test.mjs`).
- **The Rising plans in flight** (`docs/plans/2026-10-01-rising-0[3-5]-*`) edit files this plan

Then the owner's checklist, in play, with what to notice:

- The page is silent until it has had a click or a key (the browser's rule): click once, then
  Restart, or the Blow's landing at 2.4 s is missed.
- Stance: a footfall a stride, a skeleton's as a bone tick; a shove louder with its impulse; the
  catching steps, some of which are silent (a low step is no new touch).
- Shoved over: the landing, as its parts land, and whatever the body does on the ground after.
- Routine and Blow: the air rises through a swing and is highest as it lands; a club louder than
  a fist; the club on a head is a thump, on a skeleton's a bone crack. The club's return to guard
  is heard too, more quietly. At 1/4 and 1/10 speed the air lasts as long as the swing is shown.
- Pause, scrub, seek: silent. Replay from a scrubbed frame: the same sounds again.
- Arena and crypt (chunk 5): footfalls under a fight; bodies bumping; a club on the floor; a body
  into a wall.
- Anything late against what is seen (the inbox waits 60 ms, `INBOX.coalesce`); whether footfalls
  are too loud or too quiet beside a blow (`MIX.impact.floor`).
