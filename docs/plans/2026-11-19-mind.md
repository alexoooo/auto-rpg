## Where a decision's time goes

Three Warrior mirror bouts, empty-handed, the game's Mind both sides (gaps 2.65, 3.5 and 4.5 m;
121 s of play, 83.5 s of processor), sampled every 0.2 ms with `node --cpu-prof`:

- **The Mind's deciding** is 38.7% of the bout; the tree search 31.1%; Rapier's step 15%.
- **The leaf is 97% of the search** (30.3 of 31.1 points): almost all of a decision is the tail.
- Of the search's time: **predictions 49%** (an Option's model asked, 33%, and an open Option's
  model asked again for each time left to the horizon, unkept, 16%); **making the states** a model
  leaves (`child`, `place`) 17%; **the Objective's price** 14%; finding what is on offer and the
  tail's sums 13%; **the tree's own bookkeeping** (choosing, drawing, backing up) 1.5%.

So PUCT or any change to how the tree chooses touches 1.5% of the time: it is judged on strength,
not speed. Speed is in the leaf and the predictions; the world moves little in the 1/60 s between
two decisions, each made from nothing.

## The first candidates

**A value function of few hand-chosen terms, fitted to played outcomes** (the leaf). In place of
the tail, a state's worth read straight from a few features of it: each side's life, the range
over each one's reach, each one's posture (standing, down, rising) and how far into its action,
whether a strike is on offer to each, the closing speed, the time left. Weights fitted by least
squares to what the bout gave from that state on: life dealt and taken over the next seconds,
priced by the Objective. How it differs: the written form predicts one Option's outcome from its
start (range, velocity) and is fitted to drill trials; this predicts a whole state's worth to the
horizon and is fitted to bouts' moments. The Objective's few terms price a path once it is known;
this stands in for the path not searched. Judged by bars 1 to 4; its time against the tail (97% of
the search) is where "faster" is read.

*The approaches' warning, answered before fitting.* The leaf read from bouts (`e4225d62a`) priced
the life each side lost in the seconds left to the 20 s horizon. It went flat over range past 3 s:
the bouts' future from any range already held the closing their Minds did. And a line that spent
seconds safely had fewer left in which to lose life, so a back step out of reach was the cheapest
way to spend them: the mirror stood off (72 to 79%).

What is fitted here differs in its target. The target is the life each side loses from the state
to the bout's end (a knockout or the cap), the two numbers the Objective prices (dealt and taken).
The leaf adds them to the line's own tally. Seconds spent then buy nothing: the bout still ends.

Flat over range is then expected in a mirror at even life, since either side can still win. It
is harmless only if the lines' own terms decide: a trade at aggression 2 is worth taking, and the
end state's lives, postures and actions move the value. The value is today's Mind's own, fitted to
its bouts. A search that changes the policy reads it off-policy, as the approaches warn; that
risk stands.

Flatness is caught by a reading before any bout. On bouts held out:
- the fitted value against what followed, its error by bout;
- at recorded decisions, the spread of the leaf's worth over the root Options' end states, the
  fitted value's against the tail's;
- its spread over the played states 1, 3 and 10 s after a decision, by range, against the tail's.

If its spread at the root is under a fifth of the tail's, the line's own terms decide alone. It
is then read on the mirror at 24 gaps before any bar, not after.

Its features are chosen against the mended reading (above), not the window's: the range over each
side's reach, where the exchange and the danger's edge are; what each side is into (striking,
clear, down); the closing speed; each side's life.

*A first fit, before the strike's profiles are measured again.* Least squares on those features
(each life, their product, range and its square, two bumps at 0.7 and 1.15 m, the closing speed,
each one down, a blow coming, lateness), to the life each side loses from a search root to the
bout's end, fitted on `warrior`'s rows (14,528 roots of 72 bouts) and read on `warrior-drawn`'s
(27,831 of 216): R² 0.62 held out for each side (0.63 fitted), the error 0.19 of a life. It is
the lives and lateness: every feature of range or posture weighs under 0.05 of a life. Over range
the held-out mean is 0.42 to 0.46 from 0.25 to 1.75 m and rises only past 2 m, as what followed
does. So over the bout's end the value is flat where the exchange is, as the plan expects; the
spread readings at the root decide whether the line's own terms are enough.

**The Warrior's own way up: the staged-down cell's baseline is stale.** Its move (man e/e 1.64 to
2.15 s an attempt) is one commit, `5baf9e257`, which let every reposition compete with the ways
up. The drill's chooser is ranked, with staged-down before the repositions, so no price enters
its choice. On the same eight drill cases the man now lands on his front where he landed on his
side or back, and stands sooner: 7.0 to 10.8 s (mean 8.25) against 7.4 to 12.2 s (about 9.6) at
its parent. The cell measures staged-down from other starts, not a slower way up.

**The search**, two pieces, each its own kind:

- **The branch kept**: the subtree under what the body did and how it ended becomes the next
  decision's root when the state it reaches is near enough the one modelled, else a tree anew; the
  models asked at a state kept with it. Unlike the warm start, it keeps the states below, not
  the root's worths, so a worth stays tied to the situation it was found at. Judged by bars 1 to 3;
  read first at the same runs (strength), then at fewer runs (speed).
- **PUCT with priors from the Mind's own prices**: each Option's prior its one-step price at the
  state (the valued chooser's), so the runs go first to the Options the Mind already rates. Judged
  on strength at the same runs and at fewer.
