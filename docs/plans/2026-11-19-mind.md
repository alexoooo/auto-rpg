## Where a decision's time goes

Three Warrior mirror bouts, empty-handed, the game's Mind both sides (gaps 2.65, 3.5 and 4.5 m;
121 s of play, 83.5 s of processor), sampled every 0.2 ms with `node --cpu-prof`:

- **The Mind's deciding** is 38.7% of the bout; the tree search 31.1%; Rapier's step 15%.
- **The leaf is 97% of the search** (30.3 of 31.1 points): almost all of a decision is the tail.
- Of the search's time: **predictions 49%** (an Option's model asked, 33%, and an open Option's
  model asked again for each time left to the horizon, unkept, 16%); **making the states** a model
  leaves (`child`, `place`) 17%; **the Objective's price** 14%; finding what is on offer and the
  tail's sums 13%; **the tree's own bookkeeping** (choosing, drawing, backing up) 1.5%.

So PUCT or any change to how the tree chooses touches 1.5% of the time: it is judged on strength,
not speed. Speed is in the leaf and the predictions; the world moves little in the 1/60 s between
two decisions, each made from nothing.

## The first candidates

**A value function of few hand-chosen terms, fitted to played outcomes** (the leaf). In place of
the tail, a state's worth read straight from a few features of it: each side's life, the range
over each one's reach, each one's posture (standing, down, rising) and how far into its action,
whether a strike is on offer to each, the closing speed, the time left. Weights fitted by least
squares to what the bout gave from that state on: life dealt and taken over the next seconds,
priced by the Objective. How it differs: the written form predicts one Option's outcome from its
start (range, velocity) and is fitted to drill trials; this predicts a whole state's worth to the
horizon and is fitted to bouts' moments. The Objective's few terms price a path once it is known;
this stands in for the path not searched. The earlier written leaves read only distance and the
next exchange, and a leaf read from bouts went flat past 3 s: this one reads the posture and the
actions that the bout's later seconds turn on. Judged by bars 1 to 4; its time against the tail
(97% of the search) is where "faster" is read.

**The search**, two pieces, each its own kind:

- **The branch kept**: the subtree under what the body did and how it ended becomes the next
  decision's root when the state it reaches is near enough the one modelled, else a tree anew; the
  models asked at a state kept with it. Unlike the warm start, it keeps the states below, not
  the root's worths, so a worth stays tied to the situation it was found at. Judged by bars 1 to 3;
  read first at the same runs (strength), then at fewer runs (speed).
- **PUCT with priors from the Mind's own prices**: each Option's prior its one-step price at the
  state (the valued chooser's), so the runs go first to the Options the Mind already rates. Judged
  on strength at the same runs and at fewer.
