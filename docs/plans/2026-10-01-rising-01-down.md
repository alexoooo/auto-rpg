# Rising 01: down is a reading of the body

## Goal

One reading of whether a body is down, taken from the body each step, in place of the sticky
`fallen` the legs' skill sets while it makes a stance goal. Every fight, page and test reads the
one predicate. And the instrument the rest of the set is measured on: a battery of falls, with the
row of the game as it is.

The rules do not change here: a body that is down is still out of its fight. The bar is today's:
a body is down while its centre of mass is 0.25 m under the height its stance is asked to hold. So
a body that is asked to hold itself low is not down for doing so, which is what lets a crouch, a
kneel or a roll be added later without the riser taking the body from it. What changes is what the
height is measured over: the body's lowest point, where today it is the soles' middles, which a
lying body does not have on the ground. A verdict by a fall can move by a few steps; the commit
carries the fingerprint's arena lines before and after.

## `src/core/control/ground.ts`

```ts
/**
 * How far under the height it is asked to hold a body's centre of mass is when the body is down, m
 * (`docs/reference/rising.md#down`). Set, not swept.
 */
export const FALLEN = 0.25;

/**
 * **How upright a body is, as the body itself can tell**: its centre of mass's height over its
 * lowest point. The ground is level, y up, and a body on it touches it at its lowest point.
 */
export interface Uprightness {
  /** The centre of mass's height over the body's lowest point, m, now. */
  height(): number;
  /** That height in the reference pose, m: the spec's, whatever pose the body is in when this is made. */
  readonly standing: number;
  /**
   * Whether the body is down: its centre of mass `FALLEN` or more under `asked`, the height its
   * mind asks it to hold, m. Its standing height unless given, and no more than it: a body asked
   * to stand taller than it can is not down for failing to.
   */
  down(asked?: number): boolean;
}

export function uprightness(built: BuiltBody): Uprightness
```

- The lowest point is the least over every segment's shape (`SegmentSpec.shape`): a capsule's two
  ends less its radius, a sphere's centre less its radius, a box's eight corners, a hull's points.
  Each is a body-frame point of the reference pose, so now it is `pointOfToRef(segment, point, out)`
  (`support.ts`) and in the reference pose it is the point's own `y`. What a hand holds is not
  read.
- The centre of mass is the segments' `rigid.centre`, weighed by `rigid.mass`: now through
  `centreOfToRef`, in the reference pose the centres' own `y`.
- `standing` is computed from the spec when the reader is made and reads no node: the crypt makes
  a held body's mind afresh in whatever pose it was held in (`DungeonRun.hold`).
- No `Math.hypot` and kin: the module is the core's (`EXACT_MATH`).

## `src/core/body.ts`

`BodyView` gains

```ts
  /** Whether the body is down (`uprightness`, `src/core/control/ground.ts`): true while it is, false once it is up again. */
  readonly down: boolean;
```

`commandMind` makes `const upright = uprightness(built)` and keeps two more fields in its `state`
with the view's: `down: false`, and `asked: null as number | null`, the height the last command's
stance goal asks (`obey` sets it: `command.stance?.height ?? null`). The view has
`get down() { return state.down; }`, and `look` sets
`state.down = upright.down(state.asked ?? undefined)` after `motor.stance.read()`.

The view is the one place `down` is read from: a fight, a page, and (plan 02) a sub-mind all take
the body's mind's own reading, so none of them can disagree with it about a body it holds low on
purpose.

## `research/core-rise-trials.mjs`

The battery, as functions that return plain rows, like `research/core-stance-trials.mjs`.

```js
export const RISE_HARNESS = "Node, core world (src/core/world.ts), Rapier, 120 Hz";
/** How long a fall is watched, s, and how long a body must stay up to have risen, s. */
export const WATCH_SECONDS = 15, UP_SECONDS = 2;

/** When `downs` (one a step from the fall, whether the body is down) first shows `UP_SECONDS` up running: the seconds from the fall to the start of that run, or null. */
export function risenAt(downs, hz)

/** A body standing in guard under `mind` (a `MindConfig`, from plan 02; until then the game's body), shoved at its middle trunk by `impulse` N s a kilogram `degrees` about up, and watched. */
export async function shoved({ model, held, degrees, impulse = 1.5, mind })

/** `recipe`'s bout played to the first step a side is down; the other side is ordered to stand (`STAND_ORDERS`), and the fallen one is watched. Null if nobody falls. */
export async function boutFall({ recipe, mind })
```

A row: `{ fell, risen, seconds, peak, asked }`.

- `fell`: whether the body was down within 3 s of the shove (a shove it holds is no fall, and is
  left out of the rates).
- `risen`, `seconds`: `risenAt` of the view's `down` over `WATCH_SECONDS`.
- `peak`: the fastest any segment's centre of mass moves from 1 s after the fall to the watch's
  end, m/s (`SegmentBody.linearVelocityToRef`): how much it thrashes.
- `asked`: the largest force the stance asks of the ground beyond what its soles give
  (`StanceReading.shortfall.force`) over the same span, in the body's weights; `Infinity` when it
  is not finite.

`shoved` builds its body as the arena does (`createBody` with `SERVO_SECONDS`, `driveBy` with
`fighterTactics("battery", () => STAND_ORDERS)`). `held` is a key of the module's `LOADOUTS`, a
loadout by name: `club` (the wooden club in the right hand, `armed`) and `empty` today, and a
weapon added to the game is a row added there. `boutFall` builds through `buildBout`
(`research/bout.mjs`).

`research/core-rise.mjs` runs the battery on worker threads (one sequential loop in each, results
to the parent by message) and prints the table: for each model (`BODY_MODELS`) and each of club and
empty, the sixteen shoves 22.5 degrees apart; and the nine bouts of the arena's matchups
(`BODY_MODELS` squared, gap 4 m). A line a cell: falls, risen of them, median seconds, median
`peak`, the worst `asked`.

    node research/core-rise.mjs [--workers 12] [--mind '<MindConfig JSON>']

## Tests

`tests/core-ground.test.mjs` (Node stand, Rapier, 120 Hz):

1. **`a body's standing height is its spec's`**: each of `BODY_MODELS`, as built on the ground,
   `height()` within 1 mm of `standing`; the Warrior with the club in hand, the same `standing`.
2. **`a body is down from a quarter metre under the height it is asked, and up again above it`**:
   the Warrior pinned by its lower trunk, no ground; a mind through `embody` folds its hips and
   knees at 1 rad/s and then straightens them. `down()` turns true at the step `standing - height()`
   first passes `FALLEN`, and false at the step it comes back under: both sides of the bar, and it
   is not sticky. `down(standing - 0.2)` turns true 0.2 m later and false 0.2 m sooner;
   `down(standing + 1)` is `down()`. A second reader made while the legs are folded has the first's
   `standing`.
3. **`a shoved body reads down, lying`**: the Warrior under `createBody` and a standing command,
   shoved 120 N s at its middle trunk; within 3 s `body.view.down` is true, and it stays true for
   the 2 s that follow.
4. **`a body asked to hold itself low is not down at that height`**: the Warrior under
   `createBody`, pinned by its lower trunk, no ground; its driver's command folds the legs by the
   posture until `height()` is 0.35 m under `standing`, with a stance goal that bears on no foot
   (`feet: []`, so the legs are the posture's) and asks `height: standing - 0.35`: `view.down` is
   false throughout. The control: the same fold under a goal that asks `standing`, where
   `view.down` turns true.

`tests/research-rise.test.mjs`:

5. **`a rise is two seconds up running`**: `risenAt` on written traces: down then up for
   `UP_SECONDS` gives the start of the run; up for a step short of it and down again gives null;
   never down gives 0. The control of every later riser's row.
6. **`a shove of the battery fells the Warrior, and it does not rise`**: `shoved` forward and to
   the right, the row whole: `fell` true, `risen` false, `seconds` null.

## Mutations, each must go red

- `down()` compares `height() - standing`: tests 2 and 3.
- `down(asked)` ignores `asked`: test 2's lowered bar. It takes `asked` over `standing`: test 2's
  last.
- The lowest point skips boxes (the feet): test 1.
- `standing` is read from the nodes when the reader is made: test 2's second reader.
- `look` does not set `state.down`: test 3, and `a_bout_in_the_arena_runs_to_its_verdict`.
- `look` passes no asked height: test 4.
- `down` latches (`state.down ||= ...`): test 2's second half.
- `risenAt` counts a run of any length: test 5's second trace.
