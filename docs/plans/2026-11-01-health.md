# Health: a life bar and limbs

## Why

The owner (2026-11-01): health should be easy to understand and familiar to players, and keep
severing like Die by the Sword. The pool (`createPool`, `src/core/rules/pool.ts`) is neither:

- **The bar does not show how close a body is to dying.** The Warrior's 6 HP are split over its
  segments by cross-section (`partHitPoints`), and the head holds 0.446 of them, 7 %. Emptying it
  kills (`fatal`), so a body dies with the bar at 93 %. The club's best blow (`CLUB_BEST`, 138 J)
  empties a head three times over, while a bout's blows take about 0.27 HP a side
  ([wounds](../reference/wounds.md#unit)): the bar barely moves, then a fighter drops.
- **Damage moves where nobody can see it.** Excess walks the attached parts, parent first, so a
  blow on a hand drains a trunk.
- **Severing is a hidden calculation**: a part comes off when one blow empties it and goes on
  `severMargin` (half its hit points) past empty. A blow's `clean` route exists and nothing sets
  it (`src/core/rules/blows.ts` passes `clean: false` everywhere).
- **A severed part stays on.** The pool forgets it and blows stop counting on it
  (`inFight`, `blows.ts`), but its joint stays, its muscles drive it and the body uses it.
- **Wounds do nothing to the body**: nothing but the end of the fight follows from one.

A run on the Node stand (Rapier, 120 Hz, Warrior, straight line, calm, no assist, the game's pace
caps) showed that a limb at half strength reads in play without help: with the left hip, knee and
ankle at half their peak torques the walk asked 1 m/s swung the weak leg 0.46 s against 0.29 s,
stepped 0.61 m against 0.34 m and bore 6.4 s against 9.7 s on one leg out of 20; asked 1.4 m/s it
went 0.80 m/s where the whole body went 1.25; no run fell; at 0.5 m/s nothing showed. Only
straight walking was read: not turns, shoves, the stand-up or a fight.

## The design

The owner's option B: one life bar, which is the truth, and a bar on each limb, drawn on the body
diagram the Arena already has (`damageDiagram`, `src/arena/fighter-readout.ts`).

- **Life** is the body's hit points, in the rulebook's unit as now (`Rulebook.unit`, 100 J of
  blunt blow, `owner-damage-unit`): the Warrior 6, the Rogue 4. A page shows it as the bar it shows now, which a
  living body never reads empty. A body is out when its life is spent
  (`slain`), a vital part comes off (`beheaded`) or a limb whose loss ends the fight comes off
  (`maimed`); the drain as now (`GAME_ENDS`, `src/arena/end-rule.ts`, which no fall ends).
- **Every segment is in one group** of its `WoundSpec`: **vital** (a head), **trunk**, or one
  **limb** (`LimbSpec`: an arm is upper arm, forearm and hand; a leg thigh, shank and foot). The
  spec's `whole` names the parts that never come off: every trunk part, and any vital part so named.
- **A blow's damage goes to life times its group's factor** (`Rulebook.life`): vital 2, trunk 1,
  limb 0.5. On a limb it also goes, whole, to that limb's bar.
- **A limb's bar** is a share of the body's life (`LimbSpec.share`). At 0 the limb is
  **crippled**: the muscles of every joint whose child is in the limb at `Rulebook.crippled` of
  their peak torques. A crippled limb takes further blows to life as before; its bar stays at 0.
- **A heavy cut takes a limb off**: an edge or axe blow (`Mechanism`) worth at least
  `Rulebook.severBlow` of the limb's full bar that leaves the bar at 0 -- a crippled limb's bar is
  0 -- takes off the struck segment and every segment beyond it. A blunt blow or a lighter cut
  that empties the bar cripples. A point never severs. A cut costs `Rulebook.severLoss` of full
  life at once, whatever it takes off, beside what the blow did. A limb whose `lost` is `out` ends
  the fight when any of it comes off (`maimed`).
- **A lethal cut beheads**: an edge or axe blow on a vital part not in `whole` that spends life
  takes that part and everything beyond it off, ending `beheaded`; any other way to 0 is `slain`.
- **A point** goes in as now (`pointWound`, `src/core/rules/rulebook.ts`): through a part it deals
  `Rulebook.through` of full life in proportion to its depth, then wounds as any blow (life times
  the group's factor; on a limb, the same whole to its bar). Nearly every held thrust goes
  through: one past about 5.5 J goes through a Warrior's trunk, past 3.9 J through its head, and
  the measured thrusts are 25 to 28 J ([mechanisms](../reference/wounds.md#mechanisms)). So
  under the defaults a thrust through the head takes half the life, through the trunk a quarter,
  and through an arm empties its bar.
- **What a mind sees does not change**: its life as a fraction of its full life (`Senses.life`,
  `Pool.bar`), never another body's limbs. Its own crippled limbs reach it through its body, as
  weaker muscles (step 3).

**Defaults**, each the owner's to change once seen in play. Shares are of the body's full life, so
they hold for every body:

| Number | Default | What it means for the Warrior (6 HP) |
|---|---|---|
| `Rulebook.life` | vital 2, trunk 1, limb 0.5 | the club's best blow (1.38 HP) takes 2.76 HP on the head, 1.38 on the trunk, 0.69 on a limb: three to the head kill, five to the trunk |
| Human limb shares | arm 0.25, leg 0.35 | two best club blows cripple an arm or a leg |
| Human limbs lost | arm `fights on`, leg `out` | a body never stands on one leg |
| `Rulebook.crippled` | 0.5 | the limp read above |
| `Rulebook.severBlow` | 0.5 | a cut worth at least half an arm's bar (0.75 HP, 13 J of an edge at its 5.7 times blunt) takes off an arm it empties |
| `Rulebook.severLoss` | 0.15 | a cut costs 0.9 HP |
| `Rulebook.through` | 0.25 | a point through the head takes 3 HP, through the trunk 1.5 |

The Skeleton's groups follow its family's intent (the owner, 2026-09-22: decapitation not fatal,
the ribcage is, losing a leg ends it, limbs come off easily): its ribcage (`upperTrunk`) is vital
and `whole`; its head is a limb (share 0.2, `fights on`); its arms (0.15, `fights on`) and legs
(0.25, `out`). The Reptile's head and jaw are vital; its trunk is the trunk; each leg (0.2, `out`)
and its tail (`tail.base`, `tail.tip`; 0.15, `fights on`) is a limb.

**Decisions the owner may reverse**: the numbers above; that limb blows count half to life; that
losing a leg ends the fight (Die by the Sword lets a body hop); that a cut costs a fixed loss and
does not bleed over time.

**What must not move**: a mind's contract (`tests/mind-contract.test.mjs`), a direct mind's
actuator model included; a bout replays from its recipe and tape to the bit; the core computes with
exact arithmetic (`EXACT_MATH`).

**Order**: steps 1 and 2 stand alone. Steps 3 to 5 change the mirror, the dynamics and the engine,
which [Minds in parallel](2026-11-01-parallel-minds.md) and
[the core without Babylon](2026-11-01-core-without-babylon.md) change too; they start once those
have landed.

## Steps

Each step lands green on `npm run check`, `npm test` and `npm run build`, with the documents it
makes wrong, and is committed as it lands. A run is priced on its first trials before it runs.

1. **Life and limbs in the pool.** Rules and display only: no muscle and no joint changes.
   - `WoundSpec` (`src/core/spec/body.ts`) becomes `{ hp, vital, whole, limbs }`, a `LimbSpec`
     `{ name, segments, share, lost }`, the trunk every segment in neither; `createPool` refuses a
     spec where a segment is in two groups or a `whole` part in a limb. The families:
     `humanWounds` (`src/core/human/wounds.ts`), given by `figureSpec`
     (`src/core/human/spec.ts`) to the workshop bodies and today the Skeleton, which gets its own
     groups there; the Reptile's (`src/core/reptile/spec.ts`, from `assets/reptile/body.json`);
     the Lab's dummy (`dummySpec`, `src/lab/dummy-spec.ts`), its one part in the group of the
     attacker's part it stands for.
   - `Pool` keeps `total`, `bar()`, `ending()` and `attached(segment)`; `hp(part)` and
     `max(part)` give way to `life()`, `limb(name)` (`{ bar, full, crippled, cut }`) and
     `limbOf(segment)`. `wound({ part, damage, mechanism })` takes the blow's mechanism in place
     of `clean`; its `Wound` names the life taken, the bar taken, whether it crippled, the segments
     it took off and the ending. The walk of excess, `partHitPoints` and `Rulebook.severMargin`
     go. `Ending` becomes `"slain" | "beheaded" | "maimed" | "time"`.
   - `Rulebook` gains `life`, `crippled`, `severBlow`, `severLoss` and `through`, each sourced from
     a new `owner-life` entry in `src/core/sources.ts`: the owner's request of 2026-11-01 and these
     numbers as the owner's decision, its record a new section of the wounds record. The
     `owner-hp-pool` text (the pool's split and spread) and `owner-part-hp-split` go.
   - Readers follow: `blows.ts` (the mechanism to `wound`; `pointWound` given `through` of full
     life); the end rule (`src/arena/ends/damage.ts`: slain, beheaded, maimed); the Crypt's kill
     (`src/evaluation/crypt.ts`); the Lab's targets (`src/lab/targets.ts`); the cues
     (`src/audio/cues.ts`); a blow's worth in life (`blowLife`, `src/core/option/value.ts`, at
     its part's factor); the strike prior (`prior`, `src/core/option/percepts.ts`) and the profile
     reading (`src/core/skills/framework/profile.ts`); the character attribute `hp`
     (`src/core/character-attributes.ts`, `src/core/models.ts`), its text no longer "distributed
     among body parts"; the Crypt's party (`src/dungeon/main.ts`, "% HP"); `research/blow-shares.mjs`,
     which imports `partHitPoints` (`tests/script-links.test.mjs`).
   - The HUD (`src/arena/fighter-readout.ts`, `fighter-hud.ts`): the life bar;
     the diagram's vital and trunk parts shaded by life, each limb by its bar, a crippled limb
     marked, a cut one drawn empty.
   - Documents: the architecture's [Rules and wounds](../architecture.md#rules-and-wounds); the
     wounds record ([Tolerances](../reference/wounds.md#tolerances) becomes what each blow does
     under the defaults, the club's best and a sword's measured cut and thrust; the new section
     `owner-life` cites); `docs/reference/real-functions.md` (cites `partHitPoints`); the README's
     Arena lines. Evaluation records that count `taken` in hit points (the Cover and Strike
     evaluations) are compared again only against records made under these rules.

   Check: the gate; the Arena looked at briefly, bars and diagram moving with blows. One
   `npm run match -- option option` before and after, priced first, its endings and bout times
   beside each other in the commit message, answering one question: whether the defaults leave
   bouts the owner can watch, most ending by wounds and not the drain, before step 2 is spent.

2. **The strike profiles measured again.** A profile's `dealt` is damage over the struck body's
   hit points, and the group factors move every one (`assets/core/strike-profiles.json`,
   `research/strike-profiles.mjs`, read through `src/core/data/strike-profiles.ts`).
   Check: the gate; `npm run match -- option option` against step 1's.

3. **A crippled limb is weaker.** A per-channel strength in the muscles' state (`MuscleDriver`,
   `src/core/muscle/driver.ts`), 1 whole, set by the fight when a limb is crippled, and read
   wherever a peak torque is:
   - `strengthAt` and the tendon's force (driver.ts reads `side.peak` beside it);
   - the foot states (`footStatesOf`, `src/core/control/support.ts`: the ankle's hold,
     `ankleStrength`; the hold's polygons; the swing's torque, `src/core/control/swing-leg.ts`),
     which every caller of `footStatesOf` builds once (today `ground-reading.ts`,
     `stance-state.ts`, `support-readiness.ts`, `recovery-measurement.ts`, `arbiter.ts`,
     `support-fold.ts`, `kick.ts`, and the rise's `limbs.ts`, `lowering.ts`, `staged.ts` and
     `stages.ts`): rebuilt at the next control step after the strength changes, by a flag the
     change sets;
   - the mirror's muscles section (`layoutOf`, `src/core/mind/mirror.ts`; its own `strengthAt`),
     so a mind in a worker plans with the strength its body has; in process the held body
     forwards the driver's (`src/core/mind/held-body.ts`).

   A direct mind's actuator model (`src/core/mind/direct.ts`) stays its body's whole strength:
   its contract does not move, and the driver bounds what it commands.
   The Lab's Character section gains a limb to cripple.
   - Test (`tests/crippled.test.mjs`, under 10 s): a Warrior walking at 1 m/s is saved 1 s in.
     One copy goes on with its left leg crippled through the rule; the save is loaded into a
     Warrior built with the left hip, knee and ankle peaks at half, which goes on. The two follow
     each other to the bit (half is a power of two, so the products agree exactly). A reader that
     keeps what it read before the crippling fails it, as the next one added will.

   Check: the gate; the owner watches the limp in the Lab and a fight in which a limb is crippled.

4. **A cut limb comes off, in the simulation.**
   - **The engine**: each body joint becomes a slot that can be released and restored by a
     snapshot, as a grip is (`save`, `load`, `worldDigest`, `src/core/engine/rapier.ts`); the cut
     parts collide with the body they left (`jointPartners`).
   - **The body reads only what is attached**: the cut set in the pool's state; the uprightness
     (`readUprightness`, `src/core/control/ground.ts`), the foot states, the levels (`setLevel`
     leaves a cut part free), the dynamics (`bodyDynamics`, `src/core/build/dynamics.ts`, and the
     coupled dynamics, masked at the cut and restored by a load from before it), the senses of the
     other bodies (a cut part is no longer the body's) and the mirror (a flag for each cut joint in
     its fixed layout).
   - **The fight**: a cut part's channels are released; an item held in a cut hand goes with it,
     and its blows no longer count (`inFight`). A cut leg ends the fight (`maimed`), so no mind
     stands on one leg; with an arm cut the mind fights on, and a skill refuses an effector cut
     off from its body (`Readiness`, `src/core/skills/framework/action.ts`). The Skeleton fights
     on without its head, the one cut that is not an arm: what a mind reads of its own head is
     found and made to read the body without it before the Skeleton's head can come off.
   - The Lab gains a limb to cut.
   - Test (`tests/severed.test.mjs`, under 10 s): a Warrior's arm cut 1 s into a capped bout, the
     mind running on for 3 s without a throw: the joint is gone from the engine, the arm moves
     free, no command drives its channels; a save from before the cut, loaded and stepped again,
     follows the first run to the bit.

   Check: the gate; the step's cost (`tests/core-step-cost.test.mjs`) as before.

5. **A cut limb is drawn apart.** A fighter is one skinned mesh, each bone carried by its segment
   (`src/render/skin.ts`, `skeleton-skin.ts`, `reptile-skin.ts`), so a part flying free would
   stretch the skin: each skin is split where a limb can come off, the cut piece drawn by its
   own mesh, and the art record says how (`docs/art/`).
   Check: the gate and `npm run build`; the owner watches a sword fight in the Arena with limbs
   coming off.

## Done when

- A body is out exactly when its life bar reads 0, a vital part or a leg comes off, or the drain
  ends it; no body dies with life on its bar.
- A crippled limb is weaker by the rule, and the owner has watched its limp.
- A cut limb leaves the body and lies on the floor, drawn apart from it.
- The owner has watched fights under the defaults and accepted them or named the numbers to change.
