# Punch: robust straight punches that land at human level

## Why

The owner wants punches that land strong, at a person's level, in real fights, not only on one
drill's grid. The Warrior's learned punch lands 92 % of 320 held-out still trials (at 480 Hz 89 %
with the lead hand and 86 % with the rear), and 3 of 56 fight moments
([learned punch](../reference/learned-punch.md)). The owner watched the still rung and said "the
punch actually looks not bad"; it is not accepted.

In a fight the gap is the close range and the foe's guard. The fighters stand guard to guard at
about 0.6 m, nearer than the punch's window (0.89 to 1.01 m): 9 moments never come into it, 16
meet the foe's arms, and 20 of the 23 blows past the arms are weak, mostly on the fist's mass, its
straightness and the elbow's bend. The Rogue has no punch at the bar, and the high mark is refused.

A punch is learned offline as a timed whole-body motion steered as it runs, and plays cheap in the
browser. The body allows it with the arms and trunk at 2.5 times the curves read
([the punch's body](../reference/punch-body.md)); a planner that looks ahead every step is not the
route, since the one-step solve already costs 2.2 to 2.8 ms a body a step.

## Robustness

A punch fitted to a few drill setups fails in fights. These rules hold at every step:

- **Train where it will be used.** Each trial draws its start: the range and bearing, the target's
  height and motion, the body's own motion (standing, mid-step, closing), the body. The punch is
  trained on the Warrior's still rung; fight moments join its training ground in step 4b, under
  the reaction-time rule it will fight under. Trained on both bodies and fight moments at once, it
  landed none of the fights and almost none of the Rogue's still places
  ([tried and not kept](../reference/learned-punch.md#tried-and-not-kept)).
- **Judge on what training never saw.** The trials are split by their keys' digests into a
  training set and a held-out set; a claim is read on the held-out set at 120 and 480 Hz. A punch
  that lands only through 120 Hz's integration error fails at 480.
- **It reacts while it runs.** The punch reads the body and the target as it goes and corrects, or
  ends when the target leaves its reach or its support fails.
- **Failing safely is part of the score.** A punch that meets a moved target, is covered or is
  shoved mid-swing does not fall; falls count against it.
- **No gain by narrowing.** A punch's success is read with its window's coverage beside its landing
  rate: a punch that lands more by refusing more has not improved.
- **One skill for every body.** One punch, its settings and window per body; no controller per
  loadout or hand.
- **Fights last.** The final check is fights against minds it never trained with, under the
  owner's verdict, and the owner watching.

## The bar

Human level is the untrained adult man's, from the sources the project holds
([human strike reference](../reference/human-strike-reference.md#1-punches-straight-jabcross-hook)):
the mean less one SD (`BAR`, `src/evaluation/strike/read.ts`). A punch lands when all of these hold:

| Reading | Rear hand (right) | Lead hand (left) |
|---|---|---|
| Fist speed over its last 0.1 m before contact | 6.8 m/s (8.0 ± 1.2, Adamec) | 5.3 m/s (6.2 ± 0.9, Adamec) |
| Effective mass the fist meets on its own side (`TouchWatch` masses) | 2.12 kg (2.93 ± 0.81) | 2.34 kg (3.27 ± 0.93) |
| Straight: its last 0.1 m within 20° of the surface's normal | yes | yes |
| A punch: its last 0.1 m within 45° of the line from the shoulder to the fist; the elbow within 30° of straight and the chest within 30° of upright at contact | yes | yes |
| On the mark asked, first contact within 0.5 s of the action's start | yes | yes |
| The striker up and its action ended | yes | yes |

Impulse is reported, not gated: the contact model's impulse depends on the rate
([punch](../reference/competencies.md#punch)).

A punch is good enough when 90 % of those thrown land: a blow must pass every reading at once, and
a person's single readings already sit at the mean less one SD, so 95 % asks more than an
untrained adult gives. In a fight the 90 % is of the blows that meet the foe past its arms: a fist
met by the guard (`guarded`) or by nothing as the foe steps off (`missed`) is the foe's defence,
reported beside it; when to throw is judged in matches (step 6).

The bar is the Warrior's. Another body's is that bar scaled by its mass, size and strength, so
that any humanoid's follows from its own numbers (step 4c).

## Steps

A training run is priced on the pool first, from its first decisions' rate.

### 4a. 480 Hz to 90 %

The Warrior's punch at 480 Hz, where the rear hand lands 86 % and the lead 89 %: the misses are
the fist's mass, then the elbow's bend and speed
([why the two rates part](../reference/learned-punch.md#why-the-two-rates-part)). Trained on from
the current network, with the rates' share of episodes and the score's margins as the levers. The
run resumes from `research/runs/punch-ppo14/` (its network, normaliser, environment and held-out
check; kept outside Git).

Its leg pushes and leg steer move nothing while the legs bear: the bearing solve gives every
freedom of a stance limb its own torque (`solveBody`, `src/core/control/solve.ts`), so only the
arm and trunk channels act. Before the fine-tune the owner chooses how the legs serve the punch:
the punch asks the stance for its weight shift, or its leg pushes wait until both feet bear.

Check: the held-out reading (`research/punch-policy-check.mjs`, 320 trials, a fresh seed) at least
90 % for each hand at both rates, none fallen; the still rung re-recorded and its reel watched.

### 4b. Fight moments and the close range

The punch where the fight rung shows its gap: from guard-to-guard range (about 0.6 m), against the
foe's guard, and where the fight never brings it into its window. The punch stays one skill and
widens its entry over the states a fight and footwork leave: mid-step, drifting, closing, nearer
than its window. It takes the legs over at whatever phase they are in and drives its own step where
it needs one (a step back into range is part of its motion, not a walk). Its aim finds the trunk
past the guard. The fight's moments join the training ground beside the still places, split train
and held-out by their digests. A sharp split of its land rate across starts (say, mid-step against
planted) marks a different movement, a skill of its own
([Options](2026-10-24-options.md#the-shape-of-a-skill)).

Before: the reaction-time rule is decided ([Options](2026-10-24-options.md), step 2), so the punch
is trained under the rule it will fight under, and the fight rung read under it.

Check: the fight rung's held-out moments at the bar (of those that meet the foe past its arms),
`outside` and `guarded` beside it, and the land rate by starting state; the still rung holds its 4a
reading; the owner watches a reel of moments.

### 4c. The bar scaled to the body, then the Rogue

`BAR` becomes a function of the striker's body (its mass, dimensions and strength), the Warrior's
values its fixed point. Then the Rogue's punch at its scaled bar: its own schedule searched and its
own window, the same network trained on with the Rogue's starts. The Rogue weighs 0.73 of the
Warrior and its arm 0.65; at the Warrior's bar the most any of its schedules meets is 2.1 kg.

Check: the Warrior's readings unchanged by the scaling; the Rogue's held-out reading at its bar at
both rates; its still rung recorded and watched.

### 4d. The high mark

A punch at the head, which the guard covers: the open lane over the guard is the top of the head,
which a straight punch cannot meet square
([tried and not kept](../reference/learned-punch.md#tried-and-not-kept)). Open:

- **The head behind the guard**: a punch that reaches the head goes around or over the guard, or
  waits for the guard to open; the drill's high mark asks which.
- **The fist's threshold against a head**: at 36.8 J (`Rulebook.fist`) and a 62 % share the
  exchange stops paying at 59 J, and a boxer's blow costs the hand
  ([what a blow is worth](../reference/learned-punch.md#what-a-blow-is-worth)); the rule is read
  again before a punch is trained on the head.

Check: the high mark's held-out reading at the bar on the still rung; the owner watches.

### 5. Moving and fighting targets

The Strike drill's rungs 1 to 5 as the [Drills](2026-10-24-drills.md) plan builds them (the moving
pad, the drill opponent stepping, guarding and jabbing back, punches from a walk and after being
struck) and Stand's shove mid-strike join the training ground. A punch that winds up 0.3 s aims
where the target will be.

Check: each rung's held-out reading against the bar; the owner watches each rung.

### 6. In fights

The game's fighting minds throw the punch as their strike action, under the owner's verdict
(Drills step 7). The learned puncher (`research/learned-puncher.json`) is a drill subject, not a
mind: a committed mind whose strike is the learned punch comes first, its config in
`assets/minds/` or an entrant's. Fight moments are taken again from these bouts, since a better
punch changes the moments a fight makes, and the punch trained on them until its held-out reading
holds.

Check: `npm run match` of each fighter with the punch against the same fighter without, and
against minds it never trained with: blows landed at the bar, damage dealt, falls; the owner
watches bouts.

## Done when

At 120 and 480 Hz, the Warrior's and the Rogue's rear and lead punches (the Rogue's at the scaled
bar), on held-out trials: 90 % of those thrown land at the bar, none falls, and their coverage is at
least today's punch's. Read on at least 300 trials of the still rung, on every held-out moment of
the fight rung (at its present size 192 cases, half held out: two bodies, six foes, moments 1 to 4,
two marks and two hands; moments are added where the rate is near 90 %) and on rungs 1 to 5, each
rate stated with its count. In fights the rate is of the blows that meet the foe past its arms. In
matches, fighters with the punch land blows at the bar and win more than without it. The owner has
accepted the reels and a fight.
