## Steps

1. **The readings.** The rat against the Warrior as a tournament protocol at the scorecard's 24
   gaps, each side, read for the foe wounded and knocked out within 60 s; and the rat in the table,
   a cell for each skill it has (the bite, its gaits, its righting), each drill built from what the
   skill offers.
2. **The bite lands and wounds.** By the skill recipe: a bite scored on what it takes from a
   standing man in its drill, what the rat's body can do with the bite flat out, then its settings
   searched on the rat. The reach (the bite takes a point within 15 cm of the mouth's height) and the
   press (a tooth wounds by its press held, 0.2 HP per N s past 1 N) are the first suspects. A body
   that cannot is changed (jaw, neck), with tables before and after, walking and fights among them.
3. **The rat stays up.** Its righting read in the way-up drill, and its falls traced to what
   precedes them (a kick, a tread, its own gait), the skill mended before the Mind.
4. **Its tactics**, after the desktop's modelling lands, since both touch the Option Mind and its
   fitted value: the rat's value fitted on its own bouts, and whether biting and drawing back out of
   the stomp's reach comes out of the search.
