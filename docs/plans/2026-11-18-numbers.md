## What done is

The agent's bar, until the owner gives one, theirs to change:

1. **No name is a key**: nothing the Mind reads is found by a body's model or an item's name, on
   the striker's side or the struck's; each is read by its numbers (a body's reach, stature, mass;
   what it holds by what it adds to those).
2. **A body or an item added fights before anything is measured for it**, with every Option the
   bodies measured have (the back step and the staggered gait among them), its prices saying they
   are coarse; measuring it adds trials for that one alone, never one set a pairing or a loadout.
   Of every row of the census above: the strikes, the back step's sways, the gait stances, the
   edges. Stated as the count of tables a fourth body and a second weapon ask.
3. **The same of the foe**: its pressure and its steps, every pairing read from measured moments
   by numbers, none left to the rate, or to the Warrior's moments, because it has no table of its
   own.
4. **Read by leaving out**, each with its spread: one body's trials, then the club's, taken away
   and priced from the rest, against its own trials held out; then by bouts, the Mind so priced
   against the same Mind with that body's or item's own tables, on a match set that is not the
   Warrior empty-handed alone (bodies and the club crossed; it does not exist yet and is this
   plan's to make). Met where it is inside the spread of even; where it is not, the plan says by
   how much and stays open.
5. **The ground near as numbers** in the situation (room behind, slope, the nearest wall), read by
   the outcome and by a line's end; the plan says what level the game has to read it on.
6. **The game's Mind unmoved where a kind is unset** (the fingerprint); a kind is the game's only
   on the held-out reading of 4.
7. The plan is deleted when each of 1 to 6 is met or the owner strikes it by name; an item
   written down as not built is not met.
