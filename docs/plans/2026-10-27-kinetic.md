# Kinetic: a mind whose skills ask the body for results

## Why

A strike that coordinates its legs by pushing their muscles cannot be thrown from a walk, and its
leg pushes move nothing while the legs bear:

- **The leg pushes are overwritten.** The bearing solve gives every channel of a bearing leg its
  own torque, over a push (`solveBody`, `src/core/control/solve.ts`). The learned punch's ten leg
  pushes and the steer's ten leg outputs leave the still cases the same to the bit; its trunk and
  arm carry it ([learned punch](../reference/learned-punch.md#without-its-leg-pushes-and-from-a-walk)).
- **A punch on a clock fails from a walk**: asked still walking, 0 of 32 land against 21 of 32
  walked in and settled; the fist is fast (8.1 m/s) and meets half the bar's mass (1.15 kg), with
  a foot in its swing at the start of most.
- **Down, the stand-up takes the whole body**: a sub-mind drives every channel, and the guard and a
  held weapon's arm go with it (`hosting`, `src/core/mind/sub-mind.ts`).
- **The game's skill machinery splits the body by channel** (`action-resources.ts`) and lets a
  skill push any channel it claims, so nothing stops a push the stance will overwrite.

The options fighter at depth 1 ([Options](2026-10-24-options.md)) already has the top half of a
general mind: every option in one shape (entry, exit, model), a `chooser` part role, one registry
of percepts with cues, and a default layer of footwork and cover. Its options answer in an
`Intent` for the skill machinery. Kinetic keeps that top half and gives it a body layer of its
own: an option asks for results by the parts it holds, and one composer makes the step's
`BodyCommand`. It is an entrant beside Options, compared with it by match and drill; both stay.

**On the toed Warrior.** Kinetic is built for the body with toes
([on the game bodies](../reference/toes.md#on-the-game-bodies)), which lands on main with Recovery
step 3. Until then its code is body-agnostic and its readings are taken with the toes applied, and
its punch starts from the learned punch fine-tuned on the toed body. On toes step-up stands none
of the target's falls, so down on the toed body it waits for Recovery's Stand up as every mind does.

## The design

```text
senses ─▶ Options' situation reader (its percepts, once a step) ─▶ situation
situation ─▶ Options' chooser over Options' commitments (follow orders, a strike) ─▶ one, or none
its ask, or the default footwork (the edge of the foe's reach), and cover on each free arm ─▶ intent
intent ─▶ Kinetic's body layer: the actions' lifecycle, each claim taken by the parts it holds ─▶ BodyCommand
down ─▶ the body's stand-up sub-mind (subs)
```

Built (`src/core/entrants/kinetic/`: `composer.ts`, `tactics.ts`, `mind.ts`):

- **Four parts**: the legs, the trunk, the left arm, the right arm; a hand's pose and grip go with
  its arm. An action holds the parts its executor needs (`Executor.needs`); the defaults fill the
  rest each step, so each part has one owner and nothing is arbitrated.
- **The legs only through the stance**: an action that holds the legs asks a walk, a facing turned
  by its `steer`, a height or a footing, which the legs make a stance goal (`Locomotion.goal`);
  a posture or push on a leg's channel is refused. A gait that would drive the legs itself is
  walked in the stance.
- **An arm or the trunk** is asked a result (an effector's path, a point's acceleration, a
  posture) or driven by its own muscles (pushes on that part's channels alone).
- **A refusal is returned, not dropped**: a write on a part the action does not hold, on a leg's
  channel, or for a low support or a delegation is left out and reported (`refused`, in its
  engagement's details).
- **Shared with Options, not copied**: its commitments are Options' own (`followOrders`,
  `strike`, closing by `approach`), chosen by Options' chooser on Options' situation; the body
  layer gives the skills' report (`SkillReport`), so the sight, the percepts, the drill and the
  readouts read it as any mind's; the edge-of-reach walk (`toEdge`) and the movement made a walk
  (`navigated`) are the toolkit's.
- **Its tactics' place**: a config's `tactics` (a drill's) decide in the chooser's place.
- **Left out**: an arbiter, reflexes, low support, a lookahead past Options' depth, a learned
  chooser. Each can come later as a part.

**To build: timing by the gait.** The situation carries the stance's phase (which feet bear, a
foot's swing, the speed); a strike starts and times its drive from it, never from a clock started
when it is asked.

## Steps

### 3. The straight punch, by parts

The learned straight punch's arm and trunk motion (`straightPunchExecutor`,
`src/core/skills/straight-punch.ts`) as a kinetic option: it holds the legs, the trunk and its
arm; its hip turn goes to the stance's heading, its arm and trunk pushes to their parts, its leg
pushes refused; its drive starts when both feet bear. The hips already turn through the stance's
heading (`ActionClaim.steer`), so the still rung moves only by what the composer changes.

The network is retrained here either way: its ten leg outputs never acted, and become asks of the
stance (its heading's turn, and from step 4 its centre's shift), so what it learns of the legs
acts. It was trained from settled stances only, so walking starts join its training cases
(`research/run-train.py --resume`, from the toed body's run).

Check: the Strike drill's still rung and its `walk` rung, paired with the learned puncher on the
same cases at 120 and 480 Hz; the learned puncher's walk rung is record
`2026-10-28-17581557-b5a333fa` (settled 21/32, from ready 1.75 s; walking 0/32, 0.35 s), and
Kinetic's trials there are its own to the bit. Kept where the walk lands more and the still rung no less.

### 4. The weight, asked of the stance

A request that carries the centre of mass toward the target with the drive, as far as the support
holds it, or a step where it does not. A `StanceGoal.centre` moved 4 cm into a placed blow was
tried and not kept ([blows](../reference/blows.md#tried-and-not-kept)); here it is read on the
mass the fist meets.

Check: the mass met and the land rate from a walk and still, paired with step 3. Kept only where
the walk's mass rises and nothing else falls.

### 5. Fighting

Options' chooser over the punch, closing into its window, with the default footwork and cover,
fights already; the punch's profile is hand-built (`Terms`) until its drill measures it. Here it is
read.

Check: `npm run match -- kinetic options` and `npm run match -- kinetic duellist`, paired with the
learned puncher's mind; a bout the owner watches.

## Done when

Kinetic is an entrant on the toed Warrior whose straight punch, on the Strike drill, lands from a
walk within 10 points of its still rate, its median time from the first step ready to contact
under its settled start's (the `walk` rung), and still no less often than the learned puncher's; no request
of it writes a leg channel; and it has played a match against Options that the owner has watched.
