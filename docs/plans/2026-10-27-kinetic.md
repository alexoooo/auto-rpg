# Kinetic: a mind whose skills ask the body for results

## Why

A strike that coordinates its legs by pushing their muscles cannot be thrown from a walk, and its
leg pushes move nothing while the legs bear:

- **The leg pushes are overwritten.** The bearing solve gives every channel of a bearing leg its
  own torque, over a push (`solveBody`, `src/core/control/solve.ts`). The learned punch's ten leg
  pushes and the steer's ten leg outputs leave the still cases the same to the bit; its trunk and
  arm carry it ([learned punch](../reference/learned-punch.md#without-its-leg-pushes-and-from-a-walk)).
- **A punch on a clock fails from a walk**: asked still walking, 0 of 32 land against 21 of 32
  walked in and settled; the fist is fast (8.1 m/s) and meets half the bar's mass (1.15 kg), with
  a foot in its swing at the start of most.
- **Down, the stand-up takes the whole body**: a sub-mind drives every channel, and the guard and a
  held weapon's arm go with it (`hosting`, `src/core/mind/sub-mind.ts`).
- **The game's skill machinery splits the body by channel** (`action-resources.ts`) and lets a
  skill push any channel it claims, so nothing stops a push the stance will overwrite.

The options fighter at depth 1 ([Options](2026-10-24-options.md)) already has the top half of a
general mind: every option in one shape (entry, exit, model), a `chooser` part role, one registry
of percepts with cues, and a default layer of footwork and cover. Its options answer in an
`Intent` for the skill machinery. Kinetic keeps that top half and gives it a body layer of its
own: an option asks for results by the parts it holds, and one composer makes the step's
`BodyCommand`. It is an entrant beside Options, compared with it by match and drill; which body
layer is kept follows from the readings.

## The design

```text
senses ─▶ percepts (Options' PERCEPTS, once a step) ─▶ situation
situation ─▶ chooser (Options' part) ─▶ one commitment, or none
commitment + defaults (footwork on free legs, cover on each free arm) ─▶ requests by part
requests ─▶ composer ─▶ BodyCommand ─▶ the stance and the whole-body solve
down ─▶ the body's stand-up sub-mind (subs)
```

- **Four parts**: the legs, the trunk, the left arm, the right arm; a hand's pose and grip go with
  its arm. An option names the parts it holds (`parts`); the commitment holds its parts, and the
  defaults fill the rest each step, so each part has one owner and nothing is arbitrated.
- **The legs only through the stance**: a request on the legs is footing (`StanceGoal`: the feet
  that bear, the heading, the height, the centre of mass, a step, a walk), never a leg channel.
- **An arm or the trunk** is asked a result (an effector's path, a point's acceleration, a
  posture) or driven by its own muscles (pushes on that part's channels alone).
- **A refusal is returned, not dropped**: the composer refuses a request on a part the option does
  not hold or a push on a leg, and the option's report says so.
- **Timing by the gait**: the situation carries the stance's phase (which feet bear, a foot's
  swing, the speed); an option starts and times its drive from it, never from a clock started
  when it is asked.
- **An option is Options' shape** (`OptionBase`, `src/core/mind/options/option.ts`: entry, exit,
  yields, ended, begin, end, models) with `parts` and a `step` that returns a request.
- **Reused as they are**: Options' chooser and percepts, the locomotion part's stance goal for a
  walk (`Locomotion.goal`, `src/core/skills/locomotion.ts`), the guard's posture, the stand-up as
  a sub-mind in `subs`, the stance and the solve.
- **Left out**: an arbiter, reflexes, a lookahead past Options' depth, a learned chooser. Each can
  come later as a part.

## Steps

### 1. The entrant and its composer

`src/core/entrants/kinetic/` and its line in `MIND_KINDS`: the config (its chooser, locomotion,
guard and `subs` slots), the option type, the composer, and the defaults: footwork that faces the
foe and keeps a range, cover that holds each free arm in the guard. Down, the stand-up sub-mind has
the body.

Check: the mind contract runs it, forks it and replays it; `node scripts/entrant-check.mjs` names
the change the entrant's and the registry line; in the Arena it faces and follows a foe in guard
and gets up when knocked down, looked at in a browser.

### 2. The Strike drill from a walk

Strike's rung 5, struck from a walk ([Drills](2026-10-24-drills.md)): the drill's tactics ask at
the first step the action is ready while walking into the window, and the trial records the
stance's phase and speed when asked. Built here because steps 3 and 4 are read on it.

Check: the learned puncher's 0 of 32 read again on the drill itself; its still rung unmoved.

### 3. The straight punch, by parts

The learned straight punch's arm and trunk motion (`straightPunchExecutor`,
`src/core/skills/straight-punch.ts`) as a kinetic option: it holds the legs, the trunk and its
arm; its hip turn goes to the stance's heading, its arm and trunk pushes to their parts, its leg
pushes refused; its drive starts when both feet bear.

Check: the Strike drill's still rung and its walk, paired with the learned puncher on the same
cases at 120 and 480 Hz. Kept where the walk lands more and the still rung no less.

### 4. The weight, asked of the stance

A request that carries the centre of mass toward the target with the drive, as far as the support
holds it, or a step where it does not. A `StanceGoal.centre` moved 4 cm into a placed blow was
tried and not kept ([blows](../reference/blows.md#tried-and-not-kept)); here it is read on the
mass the fist meets.

Check: the mass met and the land rate from a walk and still, paired with step 3. Kept only where
the walk's mass rises and nothing else falls.

### 5. Fighting

Options' chooser over kinetic's options: the punch, footwork that brings its window, and cover;
the punch's profile hand-built (`Terms`) until its drill measures it.

Check: `npm run match -- kinetic options` and `npm run match -- kinetic duellist`, paired with the
learned puncher's mind; a bout the owner watches.

## Done when

Kinetic is an entrant on the Warrior whose straight punch, on the Strike drill, lands from a walk
within 10 points of its still rate and still no less often than the learned puncher's; no request
of it writes a leg channel; and it has played a match against Options that the owner has watched.
