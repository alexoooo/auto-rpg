# Kinetic: a general mind on the toed Warrior

## Why

Kinetic is the mind the general layers are proved on: one skill shape, one chooser that chains
skills at any depth, one legs goal any stance and walk carry out
([Kinetic, general](2026-10-28-kinetic-general.md)). Standing, walking, punching from either,
rising and combinations are compositions of skills in that shape, never a skill of their own
for a special case. The work here establishes that baseline on the Warrior with toes, then gets
the stand-up and the punch working on it and iterates from there.

- **The body is the toed Warrior.** Toes that bend stand a rise from hands and knees 30 of 32
  times against 0 without ([toes](../reference/toes.md)). The game's bodies keep their feet for
  now: on toes the game's step-up stands none of the target's 64 falls
  ([on the game bodies](../reference/toes.md#on-the-game-bodies)), so the toed Warrior is a body
  of its own, and Kinetic and the minds it is compared with fight on it. The Rogue follows.
- **What Kinetic is today** (`src/core/entrants/kinetic/`): Options' situation, chooser and
  commitments (`followOrders`, `strike`, closing by `approach`), the default footwork at the edge
  of the foe's reach and cover on each free arm, over a composer of its own that owns the body by
  four parts. The composer goes with the general body layer.
- **What its punch does today**: retrained with its leg outputs as asks of the stance and walking
  starts (`research/runs/kinetic-punch`, 50.7 M decisions, toed, 120 Hz), on the Strike drill's
  still rung 30 of 32 and walking 20 of 32, against the learned punch's 24 and 0; from a walk it
  halts and lands 0.19 s from ready against 1.66 s settled. A walking punch's failures are fast
  and light: the fist meets under 1.2 kg.

## Steps

The baseline (steps 1 to 3) is in order. After it, steps 4 to 7 are independent, each paired
