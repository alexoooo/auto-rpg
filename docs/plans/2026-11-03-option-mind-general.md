# Option Mind control and evidence

## Goal

Verify and correct the control contracts beneath Option Mind, then establish paired evidence on
its supported bodies before [skill training](2026-11-03-options-training.md). The shared Mind,
component and prediction contracts are described in the [architecture](../architecture.md#minds).
This plan owns control behavior and its evidence; it trains no skill.

The scope includes every body Option Mind plays, the reptile and rat included. The evaluation
table covers the toed Warrior, Rogue and Skeleton with empty hands, a club, a sword, and each
weapon with a buckler. The Skeleton has no stomp; its applicable acts and settings come from
registered parts and per-body records.

## Work

Establish a baseline from the stand-up and run rows before changing their control paths. Price
fresh readings from their first trials; keep and reuse those rows. Follow the run limits and
paired comparison rules in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#doing-the-work).

Check these contracts at their owners and correct every demonstrated failure:

- A planned walking step lands before its next shift transfers weight to that foot. Read the
  step at its end, then check the walk's pace, stop and run against the locomotion protocol.
- A walk asked to stand at a place asks the centre of mass to hold that place. A strike's entry
  admits the quiet supported body that the corrected walk supplies.
- An approach to a downed target stops within its requested tolerance and keeps its chosen side
  as the target moves. Read the Skeleton's stopping path and the Rogue's side selection through
  the stomping approach.

Judge each correction on its own layer's contract. Correct any defect it exposes above in the
same change. A shared correction is read on every supported body; where a body's reading drops,
search its settings again on the corrected code before deciding whether its controller or record
needs a separate implementation. Keep body-specific settings in their owning records.

Read the resulting Option paths through their actual configured skills and models. Confirm that
walks reach the sites their Options name, strike entry agrees with execution, and recovery prices
and readiness refer to routes the chooser can offer. Read the rat and reptile from side and back
placements through righting and resumed travel. A refusal or an unreached fixture remains explicit.

Compare the supported stand-up and run cells with their baselines, and each other affected cell
with its recorded baseline. The table names the actual body, loadout, protocol and version; stale
rows supply historical evidence rather than a claim about the current version. Human figures
remain a reported scale. Skill-success claims require the watched drill links prescribed by
[AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#minds-and-measurement).

## Validation and completion

Run the checks required by the change. Behavior-preserving changes keep exact fingerprints;
behavior corrections name their changed cases and retain paired readings. Do not replace saved
expectations or choose easier fixtures to make a result pass.

Completion requires the demonstrated control failures and their downstream defects to be fixed,
current paired rows for the affected cells, and stand-up and the run without a remaining setback.
The [body-by-body training plan](2026-11-03-options-training.md) begins from those readings. Only
the owner accepts a drill rung.
