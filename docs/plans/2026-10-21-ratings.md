# Ratings: one way to evaluate, a history that keeps every result

## Why

Every question of how well something does (a mind in the field, a skill at its task, a choice at
a moment) is asked one way, an evaluation, and every result is kept as a record and compared where
comparable ([architecture](../architecture.md#evaluations)). What is left:

- **Each kind of thing rated by the experiment that isolates it**: an item, a character, an
  attribute one at a time (step 9); a skill on its task beside what removing or replacing it does
  in a fight (step 10).
- **A league only when the owner asks** (step 11), and **a live run** once the Ratings screen is in
  use (step 14).

Speed comes from what is asked, under [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md)'s long-run rule. No setting is
chosen by a run played only to choose it: margin and schedule come from the imported 10-hour
rows and the rows reports play anyway.

The owner has decided: Ratings is a top-level screen, linked from the menu and the Arena.
A record is never refused or deleted for being old: the screen says which versions and pool it is
evidence about. How well a mind or skill does is an evaluation's reading, never a test. The full
tournament is not run again.

## Where it starts

Steps 1 to 8, 12 and 13 have landed (step 6 as a finding: no shorter cap is sought, below):
evaluations (`src/evaluation/`, `npm run evaluate`),
the match, the tournament and the unseen report as evaluations, ratings within a pool, the 10-hour
run imported, the panel report, the Ratings screen, a bout case watched in the Arena, the falls,
and a case's code by its closure (`caseCoder`); of step 10, the blade, buckler, court and crowd
batteries with `--ablate`. How they work is the architecture's
([Evaluations](../architecture.md#evaluations)).

The first `tournament` and `unseen` records are the 10-hour run imported: played on `1bacac44`'s
source, 107 entrants, 11,342 bouts; Node, core world, `rapier-coordinate`, 120 Hz. Least squares on
its ratings:

| Model of an entrant's rating | R² | rms left, Elo | Parameters (identified) |
|---|---|---|---|
| mind | 0.13 | 208 | 10 |
| loadout | 0.47 | 162 | 5 |
| mind + character + loadout | 0.61 | 140 | 17 |
| mind + character + left item + right item | 0.61 | 140 | 16 |
| mind + character + loadout + mind x loadout | 0.81 | 97 in sample, about 122 corrected | 39 |

So minds are compared with kits held alike; each hand's item, barely tested by the set (left hand
only empty or buckler), is varied by design (step 9); and ratings stay per entrant, the residual
being above an entrant's own uncertainty (median 95 % interval 126 Elo).

Of the rows' 11,726 bouts (384 of them the unseen report's), 7,409 (63 %) end at the 120 s cap,
taking 247 of 310 simulated hours; there the bars decide (`Duel`, `src/arena/duel.ts`), under 0.05
apart in 3,123 and both full in 837. Once the owner's verdict lands
([Drills](2026-10-24-drills.md), step 7), the drain ends every bout within two minutes, so the
bars decide none; no shorter cap is sought.

## Steps

Each is a toolkit change. Later steps run when their question is asked; 14 when the screen is in use and a
live run would save a trip to the CLI.

### 9. Items, characters and attributes, by design

Each factor is varied with all else held, against reference minds (the panel's that fit, about
three), when its question is asked (an item added or changed, a character's or attribute's
balance), on the subjects it names, within budget; nothing plays the whole catalogue.

| Evaluation | Subjects | A case | Gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| `loadouts` (arity 2) | `LOADOUTS` (`src/core/items/held.ts`), each hand's item a part | two loadouts, one mind and character both sides; conditions: reference minds, characters, gaps where both fit (`modelHolds`, `modelRefusal`, `src/core/models.ts`) | a rating per loadout; the loadout grid (items' balance report); each hand's item by paired swaps of loadouts differing in one hand, same opponents |
| `characters` (arity 2) | `BODY_MODELS` (`src/core/models.ts`) | two characters, one mind and loadout both sides, over reference minds, loadouts, gaps both fit | a rating per character |
| `attributes` (arity 1) | entrants | an attribute (`balance`; `haste` where `modelHastes` allows), value, gap, side: the entrant at that value against itself at its character's own, through `DuelRecipe`'s `balance` and `haste` | score and margin by value: a curve, slope in Elo per 10 % by the rating's logistic; values from the game's range, in the commit message |

Minds, characters and items are compared on common ground: two minds by their entrants' paired
results on kits both fit, coverage beside, never averaged in. The screen shows a mind's attribute
curves beside its loadouts' ratings on one character, in one Elo scale; no budget of points is
built here.

Check: loadout and character cases mirror all but their subjects; conditions skip what does not
fit; an attribute case at the own value plays to the mirror bout's digest.

### 10. A skill's battery beside its ablations

The Ratings screen shows a skill's battery beside its ablations; a battery gain with no gain in
fights is a finding. The other research batteries become drills in the
[Drills](2026-10-24-drills.md) plan (its step 8).

Check: the screen pairs a battery's record with the panel report of the same ablation.

### 11. The sparse league, when asked

Only when the owner asks, within the owner's budget, playing only cases no kept row holds, under
the game's verdict. A new schedule beside `all`, `versus` and `pairs` (`src/evaluation/schedule.ts`):
`sparse { opponents, seed }`, each subject meeting the `opponents` others with the lowest draw of
the digest of the sorted pair and the seed; as a union, between `opponents` and about twice as
many. `opponents` is chosen with no bout played: on the imported games, 8, 16 and 32 on five seeds
by subsampling, reading rank correlation with the full run, largest rating gap, bouts, and held-out
log-loss; the default and table go in the commit message. Shared factors (mind, character, each
hand's item, with an entrant term) join the fit only if they lower held-out log-loss at that
`opponents` and their intervals cover held-out games at their stated rate; otherwise one strength
per entrant, readings given either way, checked against step 9.

Check: `sparse` gives each subject between `opponents` and about twice as many, the same for a
seed.

### 14. Run live (waits)

This waits until the screen is in use and a live run would save a trip to the CLI.

- `src/ratings/case-worker.ts` plays cases with `playCase`; `src/ratings/pool.ts` is the browser
  `spawn()` for `src/evaluation/lanes.ts`; each worker calls `loadData` for its cases' mind data.
- The identity plugin also emits the file table a case's code is drawn from (`fileTable`,
  `scripts/build-identity.mjs`), which `src/build-identity.ts` exports, and applies to workers
  (`worker.plugins`, `vite.config.ts`, whose comment that no page but MuJoCo's threaded build
  makes a worker is corrected).
- The run panel picks evaluation, subjects (a mind from the mind editor by its link config),
  schedule, comparison, budget and lanes (default `navigator.hardwareConcurrency - 2`); reuses rows
  of records whose case code matches; updates rows, table and answer as the complete units come
  in. A live run is unrecorded: its rows save as a working-rows file, and the panel shows the
  command that writes the record.

Check: in a browser, a live case's digest equals Node's for the same case.

## Checks

- Every step: `npm run check`, `npm test`, and `npm run build` where browser code or
  `vite.config.ts` changes; each updates the documents it makes wrong (`AGENTS.md`'s Commands and
  "A claim is a match"; `docs/architecture.md`'s Evaluations; `README.md`).
- No bout, body, mind or rule changes behaviour: a step that claims none reads the fingerprint
  (`scripts/fingerprint.mjs --compare`), and a moved case is a defect.
- Unchanged: `CAP_SECONDS`; the 64-fall stand-up target's
  protocol and reading; `research/stand-up-acceptance.mjs` (an external controller on its own
  apparatus, `recoveryEnvironment` in `research/stand-up-environment.mjs` on `coreStand`; a case
  kind when next worked on); `CORE_ENGINE=<name> npm test`.

## Done when

- A change is read against its previous version and the panel.
- Items, characters and attributes can be rated by design when asked; a skill's battery is read
  beside its ablations on the Ratings screen.
- Every run states its expected time; no setting was chosen by a run played only to choose it.

## Relations

- [Mind data](2026-10-21-mind-data.md): browser workers call `loadData` (step 14).
- The [roadmap](../roadmap.md)'s capability and tactics work are clients, becoming evaluations when
  next worked on.
- Decisions, a later plan when a tactics question needs one: a `point` world kind (a new
  `WorldKind` in `WORLD_BUILDERS`, `src/evaluation/worlds.ts`; arity 0) plays a bout to a step,
  saves it, plays each response for a horizon and reads raw outcomes; values come in analysis, a
  chooser is scored by regret against the best response; the Arena opens one at its step from the
  stored tape, as it opens a bout case from its start. It waits on the common value unit and on how
  a chooser ranks responses (likely Movement's orders: a Commander of one).
