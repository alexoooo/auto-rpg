# Session 07: body release 2

## Goal

Act on session 05's audit, as the owner chooses from its proposals. Then re-measure until the
three orderings hold and every attribute pays.

## What it may do

Each change is chosen by the owner from session 05's list, and each is measured with the expert
before and after:

- **Remove dead-end bodies.** Their module, their registry row and their builds go. Cosmetic art
  goes with them unless another body uses it.
- **Add skill leverage where a check came out missing.** Examples of the kind of change, not a
  commitment to any of them:
  - a stability line that depends on stance and gait phase;
  - a stroke's reaction that reaches the striker's balance;
  - a damage rule that rewards target and edge quality over raw energy;
  - a guard geometry that deflects.

  Each is general physics wherever it can be, per the physical-contact rule: a special rule only
  where the physical way is shown to fail, and then written up with the failure.
- **Redesign attributes.** Rescale an attribute's range, change what it multiplies, split or merge
  two, remove one that does not pay, or add one the audit asks for. Each change carries the
  attribute's argument in its doc comment and its tables, as the existing rows do.
- **Keep the new command surface's channels** that earned their place in session 06.

## Loop

1. Change.
2. Re-run the expert's headroom, the three orderings and the attribute audit, on drills first and
   then in the league.
3. Record the table. Keep the change or revert it.

The loop ends when:

- the three orderings meet the owner's numbers from session 05;
- every remaining body has headroom above the dead-end line;
- every attribute passes its "pays", "used" and "balance" checks.

If a change fails to move the headroom it was meant to, it is reverted rather than tuned around.

## Measure

- Body release 2's fingerprint and its diff from release 1.
- The headroom tables and the three orderings, at release 1 and release 2 side by side.
- The idle-dummy gate with the expert: every body beats every idle dummy.

## Depends on

Sessions 05 and 06.
