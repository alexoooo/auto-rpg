# Blows 06: the guard covers

## Goal

A guarding hand can be told what to cover, and puts what it holds, or itself, between the threat
and the place guarded. The tactics say what; the guard skill says how. A fighter's tactics read
the threat from what they sense. Whether fighters cover by default is decided on a battery of
bouts against the plain guard.

There is no block: a parry with a club, a hand before the face and, later, a shield raised are
this one skill placing different points. What a cover costs is the blows' rule's.

It is built on the hand goals (`HandGoal`, `src/core/control/motor.ts`) and read by the
blows' record (`LandedBlow`, `src/core/rules/blows.ts`), both landed.

## The intent (`intent.ts`)

```ts
/** What a guarding hand covers: where the threat is, and the place of its own body it is kept from; world, m. */
export interface Cover {
  readonly threat: Vec3;
  readonly guarded: Vec3;
}

export type HandAction =
  | { readonly kind: "guard"; readonly cover?: Cover }
  | { readonly kind: "attack"; readonly target: Vec3 };
```

`GUARD_ACTION` stays the plain guard. The comment on `HandAction` says a guard with a cover is
the skill's to carry out, as an attack is.

## What a hand covers with (`body.ts`, `club.ts`)

```ts
  /** The two points of `points` between which the item stops a blow: a parry puts a threat's line across them. */
  readonly cover?: readonly [string, string];
```

An item that states none, and an empty hand, cover with the hand's knuckles.

## The skill (`guard.ts`)

```ts
/**
 * **How a cover is held**: the covering point, or the middle of the two, `out` m from the place
 * guarded toward the threat, reached in `seconds`. Set: `docs/reference/blows.md#cover`.
 */
export const GUARD_COVER = { out: 0.3, seconds: 0.15 } as const;

interface GuardSkill extends Skill {
  /** Each hand's goal this step: a cover's places for a hand that guards with one and that `taken` is not; null otherwise. */
  command(view: BodyView, hands: Readonly<Record<Hand, HandAction>>, taken: Hand | null): Readonly<Record<Hand, HandGoal | null>>;
}

export function guardSkill(spec: BodySpec): GuardSkill;
```

- One point: its place is on the line from `guarded` to `threat`, `out` from `guarded`, or at
  the threat's own distance less a hand's breadth where the threat is nearer than that.
- Two points: their middle is at that place, and their line lies square to the line from
  `guarded` to `threat`, in the direction square to it that is nearest the way the item lies now
  (so it does not swing end over end); each point is half their distance to either side.
- The places are turned into the root's frame (`BodyView.root`).
- A hand whose action has no cover has no goal: its arm is the posture's, `GUARD`.
- It remembers nothing; `resume` does nothing; `state` is empty.

`createSkills`: `all` is `[legs, strikes, guard]`. The strike's hand is `taken`; the command's
`hands` are the strike's goal for its hand and the guard's for the other. The numbers start as
written and are set by the battery below (`SkillOptions.cover` passes an experiment's).

## The threat (`threat.ts`)

```ts
/**
 * **What counts as a threat**: a foe's striking point within `within` m of the head and closing
 * on it at over `closing` m/s. Set: `docs/reference/blows.md#threat`.
 */
export const THREAT = { within: 1.5, closing: 2 } as const;

/**
 * The cover a body's senses ask for: of every other side's body still in the fight, the point
 * each hand strikes with (`aimOf`, placed by the hand's sensed frame, moving with its sensed
 * velocity and spin) that closes fastest on this body's head, if it is a threat; guarded, the
 * head. Null with none.
 */
export function threatOf(view: BodyView): Cover | null;
```

It reads what `BodySense` already carries and says it is for: each segment's frame, velocity and
spin, and the spec with what the body holds. Arithmetic by `src/core/math/real.ts`. What is sensed
is as old as the senses' delay; nothing here corrects for it, and the battery reads what that
costs.

## The tactics (`config.ts`, `fighter.ts`)

```ts
export interface FighterMindConfig {
  readonly kind: "fighter";
  readonly subs: readonly SubMindConfig[];
  /** How a hand that does not attack guards: the pose, or a cover of what threatens (`threatOf`). */
  readonly guard: "pose" | "cover";
}
```

`FIGHTER.guard` is `"pose"` as this lands. `fighterTactics` takes it: under `"cover"`, every
hand it gives `GUARD_ACTION` today it gives `{ kind: "guard", cover }` while `threatOf(view)`
is not null. An attacking hand attacks; the other covers.

The arena's address gains `&guard=cover` (`src/arena/matchup.ts`, beside `&balance=`): both
sides' minds are `FIGHTER` with that guard, through `DuelRecipe.minds`. It is how a bout under
the cover is watched, and a tape of one names it. Anything but `cover` or `pose` is refused as a
balance that is no number is.

## The battery (`research/core-guard.mjs`)

```
node research/core-guard.mjs [--bouts 384] [--delays 0,<the arena's>] [--workers 14]
```

Arena bouts (`research/bout.mjs`, Node, Rapier, 120 Hz, each side's balance named), each matchup
of bare hands and clubs on the Warrior and the Rogue, the left under `guard: "cover"` and the
right under `"pose"`, and the same seeds mirrored; a pose-against-pose cell on the same seeds is
the control. Per cell: the covering side's hit points lost a bout, and where its blows landed
(head, trunk, hand or forearm, its own item); its falls; who won. The comparison is paired by
seed: the mean difference in hit points lost and its effect size (Cohen's d of the paired
differences), on fresh seeds a second time.

**The bar.** Covering loses fewer hit points to the head in every cell, at d over 0.2 on both
sets of seeds, with no more falls. Met, `FIGHTER.guard` becomes `"cover"` in a change of its own
that measures the standing table again. Missed, the skill stays, fighters go on in the pose, and
the record says what was read.

## Tests

`tests/core-guard.test.mjs` (Node core stand, Rapier, 120 Hz):

1. **`a bare hand comes between the threat and the head`**: standing, the left given a cover of
   a point 0.8 m before the face: within 0.3 s the knuckles are within 3 cm of the place on the
   line; the threat moved a quarter round the head, they follow; the cover taken away, the arm is
   back at `GUARD` within the tolerance the posture is held to.
2. **`a club is laid across the threat's line`**: the line from the head to the threat passes
   within the swell's radius of the swell's axis, and the axis is within 0.2 rad of square to it;
   from a threat on the other side it does not turn end over end (the same end stays uppermost).
3. **`the threat is the foe's striking point that closes fastest`**: two bodies in a world: a
   foe's fist driven at the head at 5 m/s from a metre: the cover's threat is at the fist, to a
   centimetre, and guarded is the head; with a club in that hand, at the swell; moving away,
   null; beyond `within`, null; of the body's own side, null; out of the fight, null.
4. `core-orders`, **`a fighter told to cover gives the cover to the hand that does not attack`**,
   and under `"pose"` gives none. The matchup's test: `&guard=cover` gives both sides the config,
   an address without it gives none, and `&guard=shield` is refused.
5. **`a club's blow at the head is met by the club that covers`**: a Warrior throws its club
   recipe at a Warrior ordered to stand under `"cover"`, at three stand-offs: the first blow
   between them has the defender's side's `item` the club, or its `segment` a hand or forearm;
   under `"pose"`, the control, it is the head.
6. `arena-fork`: a fork across a cover.

## Mutations, each must go red

- The place set `out` from the threat, not the guarded: tests 1 and 2.
- The two points' line laid along the threat's line: test 2.
- The guard placing a hand the strike has: a strike-skill test's hand goal, and test 4.
- `threatOf` reads the hand's knuckles when it holds a club: test 3.
- `threatOf` reads its own side: test 3.
- The closing speed's sign turned over: test 3's moving away.
- `fighterTactics` ignores the config: test 4.
