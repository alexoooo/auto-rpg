# Strikes that aim, use the whole body and keep their feet

## Goal

A blow that lands from where a fighter stands, against a target that has moved a little, thrown
with the legs and trunk in it, and that does not put its own body down when it meets nothing. The
blows today are clumsy: they miss, and a miss can tip the thrower over.

Nine ways to get there are ranked below; the owner approved the order on 2026-10-02. Each chunk
lands green and is measured against chunk 0's tables (`research/strike-robustness.mjs`,
`research/strike-bouts.mjs`, [blows](../reference/blows.md#robustness)), which have landed; a gate
says whether the next goes on.

## What the code is

- **A recipe is open loop**: a chamber `Pose` held, then timed `MusclePush`es on trunk and arm
  channels alone (`assets/core/strikes.json`). A pushed channel's torque is fixed in the servo's
  goal (`control`, `src/core/control/motor.ts`), and nothing steers it. It is thrown from one
  start: square feet, `STAND` 1.5 s, the target at `Recipe.place`; its window is 4 to 24 cm
  ([human-and-strikes](../reference/human-and-strikes.md#windows)).
- **The stance takes a push's torque in its own step and plans none ahead.** `heldFreedoms`
  (`bearing.ts`) puts a pushed freedom's acceleration in the root's rows, so the ground is asked
  for the swing as it happens; the plan of the centre of mass (`advancePlan`, `planAcross`,
  `stance.ts`) never sees it coming. Why a blow at nothing puts a body down is not measured.
- **The legs cannot be pushed**: `bearLimbs` sets their torques, after any push. They come into a
  blow only through a `StanceGoal`: `centre`, which no skill sets and which is held inside the
  support shrunk by `SUPPORT_INSET`, and `swing`, a timed step (`seconds`, `lift`, `shift`,
  `transfer`, `capture`) that `Locomotion.place` does not expose. A staggered `Footing` stands; a
  foot lands facing the pelvis, with no yaw of its own.
- **The servo follows a path when given one** (`ServoFeed`, `servo.ts`), and a hand goal is the
  only thing that gives one. `BodyCommand` (`body.ts`) has no joint path.
- **The tactics** walk at the foe to `ATTACK_METRES` and attack its head; the skill walks the rest
  at 0.3 m/s, sets the feet within 2 cm, stands 1.5 s and throws. Nothing reads
  `StrikeReport.reach`, and no side knows the other's reach. A foe's velocity is sensed
  (`BodySense`).
- **A private copy** of one body is about 50 ms a second of it ([oracle](../reference/oracle.md),
  [step cost](../reference/step-cost.md)); `src/core/think/` is designed and not built.
- **A step into a stance before a strike** was tried on an engine that is gone and did not
  converge with the rate ([human-and-strikes](../reference/human-and-strikes.md#stand-time)). It
  has not been read on Rapier.

## The order

| Rank | What | Answers | Gate to go on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The recipe's recorded path tracked by the servo, and steered at the target | misses; a target that moves | tracked keeps 0.8 of the pushed blow's `done` at its place |
| 2 | Recipes searched over starts that vary | misses; less placing | the pilot's window wider at the same net |
| 3 | The weight planned with the blow; the follow-through braked | the step after a blow | 1 landed |
| 4 | Range: held just outside the foe's reach, a blow thrown when the foe enters the window | the walk to a place and the stand | the owner chooses the tactics' shape |
| 5 | A staggered stance and a blow that steps in | power; the look of it | 1 to 3 landed; the stepped stance converges on Rapier |
| 6 | A family of blows by the target's place | less walking | the search's form fixed by 2 or 5 |
| 7 | A blow rehearsed in a private copy before it is thrown | misses at a window's edge | 1 to 3 plateau; `src/core/think/` built |
| 8 | Hand goals with points along the way and a speed at the place, for the cover | the guard, not blows | a guard plan asks for it |
| 9 | A learned controller | everything, in time | 1 to 6 plateau |

Rank 1 is first because a blind blow is the cause of the misses, its seam is narrow, and every
later item is better built on a blow that can be steered. Rank 3 is built on rank 1's recorded
path. Ranks 4 and 5
change what the tactics decide, whose structure is the owner's. A placed blow lands a tenth of a
recipe's energy, so hand goals stay with the cover.

## What chunk 0 found

[blows](../reference/blows.md#robustness) holds the tables; the plan reads them so:

- **A blow in a bout is thrown open loop at a head that sways.** The tactics hold the point they
  attack until the head is 25 cm from it, so a throw is committed a median 11 cm off the head. Aiming
  at the head as it is ([aiming afresh](../reference/blows.md#aiming-afresh)) puts it in the window
  at every commit, but the head moves 5 to 7 cm in a club's chamber, so a club throw is no more
  often on the head; the feet are set again three to five times as often, and the hit points a
  minute fall, club and bare. A blow that cannot be steered cannot use a fresher aim: rank 1
  first, and aiming afresh read again once a blow can follow its target.
- **Across the heading is where a club misses**: twelve centimetres across, a club blow at a head
  keeps 0.17 or less of what it does. Rank 1's steering answers across first.
- **A blow at nothing puts its body down rarely; it makes the stance step.** The capture point
  runs past the outline drawn in by `SUPPORT_INSET` in the follow-through, back and to the
  thrower's right, and the stance's recovery steps it, in ten of twelve recipes; in bouts the
  thrower steps after more than a third of its throws, and is down after four of 384. The step,
  not a fall, is the "falling back" to be seen. Rank 3's first piece is the planned centre and the
  braked follow-through, which keep the capture point inside that outline; a caught miss is the
  stance's already.

## Chunk 1: a tracked, steered blow

1. `research/core-strike-track.mjs` throws each recipe at its place on the stand as the search
   does (`throwBlow`, `watchBlow`, `src/lab/blow.ts`) and records each step, from the chamber to
   the end of the pushes, the angles of its chambered and pushed channels, into
   `assets/core/strikes.json` as the recipe's `track` (`hz`, `channels`, `angles`). `Recipe.track`
   in `src/core/skills/strikes.ts`, mirrored for the left hand as `recipesFor` mirrors channels.
2. `BodyCommand.track` (`src/core/body.ts`): channel to `[angle, rate, acceleration]`, passed by
   `obey` to `MotorControl.setTrack`; `owned(i)` in `motor.ts` reads it before the hand goals, so
   the servo feeds its rate and acceleration as it does a hand goal's, and the stance sees it
   through `work.accel`. Tests: a tracked elbow follows a path's samples on the stand within a
   bound; a channel pushed and tracked is pushed.
3. `SkillOptions.form`, `"pushed"` or `"tracked"`, `"pushed"` until the gate passes. A tracked
   recipe samples its path at `since`, rate and acceleration by central differences, and gives
   `track` with no pushes.
4. Steering: the target in the body frame (`intoFrameToRef`) against the recipe's place. Across,
   the trunk's rotation channels turned by the bearing; up, lumbar and shoulder flexion offset by
   gains read on the stand (the struck point's change per radian, by finite difference), recorded
   in `blows.md` and sourced in `SOURCES`; along stays the feet's. `src/core/math/real.ts` only.
5. Gate: chunk 0's table in both forms. Tracked keeps 0.8 of the pushed `done` at its place and
   widens the window across; if it loses the power, the pushes drive and only the steering
   channels are tracked. The default turns to `"tracked"` in a commit of its own with its table.

## Chunk 2: starts that vary

`core-strike-search.mjs --jitter along,across,up` moves each trial's target within that much, by
the golden ratio's sequence, and scores the mean, in the form chunk 1 settles. A pilot on the
Warrior's club at a head, ±8 cm along and across, against the recipe it would replace by window
and net (`core-strike-window.mjs`); if it passes, the 36 searches (about 4 h on 24 threads),
windows and repertoire, recorded in `blows.md#searched`.

## Chunk 3: the weight with the blow

Each piece a commit with its table:

- the strike sets `StanceGoal.centre` along the tracked path's recorded centre of mass, so the
  capture point stays inside the outline the stance steps from through the follow-through;
- after the window of contact the tracked path returns to the guard, braked by the muscles'
  lengthening branch (`force-velocity.ts`); no strength is raised.

Test: the recipes thrown at nothing that step today (`strike-robustness.mjs`) step in fewer
throws, at 120 and 480 Hz, and none falls.

## Chunk 4: range

The owner chooses the tactics' shape first, put in plain words. The simplest: each side's reach
(its own from `StrikeReport.reach`, the foe's from `BodySense.spec` by the same rule); hold just
outside the foe's; attack when the foe's head is in the blow's window, thrown from where the body
stands; a step back after. Swept as `research/core-aim.mjs` sweeps, gap by gap, by a field of
`FighterMindConfig`.

## Chunk 5: a step in

The stepped stance's rate check read again on Rapier at 120, 480 and 1920 Hz; stop if it does not
converge. Then a staggered guard, a foot's own yaw in the stance, a `swing` that lands as the hand
does, and recipes searched from that start.
