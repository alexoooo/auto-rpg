# Strikes that aim, use the whole body and keep their feet

## Goal

A blow that lands from where a fighter stands, against a target that has moved a little, thrown
with the legs and trunk in it, and that does not put its own body down when it meets nothing. The
blows today are clumsy: they miss, and a miss can tip the thrower over.

Nine ways to get there are ranked below; the owner approved the order on 2026-10-02. Each chunk
lands green and is measured against chunk 0's tables (`research/strike-robustness.mjs`,
`research/strike-bouts.mjs`, [blows](../reference/blows.md#robustness)), which have landed; a gate
says whether the next goes on.

## What the code is

- **A recipe is open loop**: a chamber `Pose` held, then timed `MusclePush`es on trunk and arm
  channels alone (`assets/core/strikes.json`). A pushed channel's torque is fixed in the servo's
  goal (`control`, `src/core/control/motor.ts`), and nothing steers it. It is thrown from one
  start: square feet, `STAND` 1.5 s, the target at `Recipe.place`; its window is 4 to 24 cm
  ([human-and-strikes](../reference/human-and-strikes.md#windows)).
- **The stance takes a push's torque in its own step and plans none ahead.** `heldFreedoms`
  (`bearing.ts`) puts a pushed freedom's acceleration in the root's rows, so the ground is asked
  for the swing as it happens; the plan of the centre of mass (`advancePlan`, `planAcross`,
  `stance.ts`) never sees it coming. Why a blow at nothing puts a body down is not measured.
- **The legs cannot be pushed**: `bearLimbs` sets their torques, after any push. They come into a
  blow only through a `StanceGoal`: `centre`, which no skill sets and which is held inside the
  support shrunk by `SUPPORT_INSET`, and `swing`, a timed step (`seconds`, `lift`, `shift`,
  `transfer`, `capture`) that `Locomotion.place` does not expose. A staggered `Footing` stands; a
  foot lands facing the pelvis, with no yaw of its own.
- **The servo follows a path when given one** (`ServoFeed`, `servo.ts`), and a hand goal is the
  only thing that gives one. `BodyCommand` (`body.ts`) has no joint path.
- **A recipe follows its target across** by turning the stance's heading after its bearing from
  the commit to the end of its pushes, up to `STEER` 0.3 rad, and the fighter aims at the head
  itself once a blow is committed ([steered](../reference/blows.md#steered)). Nothing follows a
  target up or along. Up is the matchup's, the heads' heights, known at the commit
  ([up](../reference/blows.md#up)). The skeleton's limbs are built no wider than clears them
  (`HumanFigure.widest`), so its pelvis turns.
- **The tactics** walk at the foe to `ATTACK_METRES` and attack its head; the skill walks the rest
  at 0.3 m/s, sets the feet within 2 cm, stands 1.5 s and throws. Nothing reads
  `StrikeReport.reach`, and no side knows the other's reach. A foe's velocity is sensed
  (`BodySense`).
- **A private copy** of one body is about 50 ms a second of it ([oracle](../reference/oracle.md),
  [step cost](../reference/step-cost.md)); `src/core/think/` is designed and not built.
- **A step into a staggered stance** converges with the rate on Rapier: each human and the
  skeleton, at 120, 480 and 1920 Hz, stepped 30 cm across and 15 cm back with the bearing foot
  sliding 0.2 cm or less, landing within 2.4 cm of its goal, and stayed up
  ([human-and-strikes](../reference/human-and-strikes.md#stand-time)).

A blind blow was the cause of the misses, and every later item is better built on a blow that can
be steered: across has landed, and up asks little ([up](../reference/blows.md#up)), so it is not
built. Ranks 4 and 5
change what the tactics decide, whose structure is the owner's. A placed blow lands a tenth of a
recipe's energy, so hand goals stay with the cover.

## What chunk 0 found

[blows](../reference/blows.md#robustness) holds the tables; the plan reads them so:

- **A blow in a bout is thrown open loop at a head that sways.** The tactics hold the point they
  attack until the head is 25 cm from it, so a throw is committed a median 11 cm off the head. Aiming
  at the head as it is ([aiming afresh](../reference/blows.md#aiming-afresh)) puts it in the window
  at every commit, but the head moves 5 to 7 cm in a club's chamber, so a club throw is no more
  often on the head; the feet are set again three to five times as often, and the hit points a
  minute fall, club and bare. A blow that cannot be steered cannot use a fresher aim; the
  steered blow aims at the head once committed, and aiming afresh before the commit is not read
  again.
- **Across the heading is where a club misses**: twelve centimetres across, a club blow at a head
  kept 0.17 or less of what it does. Turned after its target it keeps 0.46 to 0.91 there, but for
  the skeleton's, whose pelvis does not turn; in bouts the throws land no more often than a
  placebo's ([steered](../reference/blows.md#steered)).
- **A blow at nothing puts its body down rarely; it makes the stance step.** The capture point
  runs past the outline drawn in by `SUPPORT_INSET` in the follow-through, back and to the
  thrower's right, and the stance's recovery steps it, in ten of twelve recipes; in bouts the
  thrower steps after more than a third of its throws, and is down after four of 384. The step,
  not a fall, is the "falling back" to be seen. A caught miss is the stance's already.

## Chunk 1: the skeleton's legs

Its thighs and upper arms are built no wider than clears each other and its trunk by 4 mm
(`skeleton-limbs-clear`), and `tests/core-human.test.mjs`'s clearance test holds it. What is left:
the [steered](../reference/blows.md#steered) stand table and the bouts read again for the skeleton.

## Chunk 2: starts that vary

Not built: steered, the Warrior's club already lands every throw within 8 cm of its place, and the
jittered search's best does not converge with the rate
([searched with jitter](../reference/blows.md#searched-with-jitter)). `--jitter` stays in the
search.

## Chunk 3: the weight with the blow

A lean held through the throw and a braked follow-through each make the recipes step more
([leaning and following through](../reference/blows.md#leaning-and-following-through)), and are
not kept. A recipe was searched against a target, which stops its arm, and thrown at nothing only
to stand; what it does to its feet on a miss was never scored. So:

- `evaluateBlow` (`research/core-blow.mjs`) reads the stance's own steps (`recoveries`), and
  `core-strike-search.mjs --still` throws each trial at nothing as well and ranks a candidate by
  its steps there before what it does (`candidateScore`).
- The pilot, the Warrior's club at a head, passes but for a window 2 cm narrower along
  ([searched still](../reference/blows.md#searched-still)): it steps in 3 throws of 16 at nothing
  at 120 Hz and none at 480, the recipe in 15 and 16, and keeps 0.90 of its done.
- Every other cell that steps at nothing is searched so from its recipe, one seed
  (`--from <model>:<band> --sigma 0.2 --still`): the Warrior's club at a trunk and fists, the
  Rogue's clubs, and the skeleton's four once its searches on its present body are in; the
  Rogue's fists do not step. A cell keeps the stiller blow where it steps in fewer throws at 120
  and 480 Hz and keeps 0.8 of the recipe's done; then the repertoire and windows are written, and
  `blows.md`'s tables and the bouts read again.

## Chunk 4: range

The skill stands where it is when a recipe's window holds the target as its approach ends, and
sets the feet only when it does not ([thrown from where it stands](../reference/blows.md#thrown-from-where-it-stands)):
a few points off placing, nothing off the set-up, since the approach ends 25 cm from the place.
The set-up shortens only if the foe comes into the window, which is the tactics'.

The owner chooses the tactics' shape first, put in plain words. The simplest: each side's reach
(its own from `StrikeReport.reach`, the foe's from `BodySense.spec` by the same rule); hold just
outside the foe's; attack when the foe's head is in the blow's window, thrown from where the body
stands; a step back after. Swept as `research/core-aim.mjs` sweeps, gap by gap, by a field of
`FighterMindConfig`.

## Chunk 5: a step in

A staggered guard, a foot's own yaw in the stance, a `swing` that lands as the hand does, and
recipes searched from that start.

## Later

Ranks 6 to 9 each take a plan of their own when their gate opens. A recipe's recorded path

pushes cannot take. Rehearsal follows the design in
