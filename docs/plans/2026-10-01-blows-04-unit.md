# Blows 04: a hit point is 100 J

## Goal

The damage unit is 100 J of blunt blow a hit point, the owner's round number, in place of the
Warrior's best club blow (138.26 J). The club's blow stays a measurement, worth 1.38 HP.

**It lands on the owner's yes, and on the option chosen.** The owner asked ("should we just make
it 100J what's so special about 138?"); nothing has been decided.

## The choice

| Option | In the game |
|---|---|
| **100 J, hit points as they are** | Every body is 28 % frailer in joules: the Warrior's 6 HP is 600 J, not 830 J; the Rogue's 4 is 400 J, not 553 J. The club's best blow is 1.38 HP and empties a Warrior's head (0.446 HP) three times over, as it does today (2.2 times). Bouts are shorter by what the table below reads. |
| **100 J, hit points raised to hold the joules** | Nothing changes in a fight; the numbers shown do: Warrior 8.3, Rogue 5.5. |
| **138.26 J kept** | Nothing changes; the unit is a measurement and moves if the club's blow is ever measured again. |

The table that goes with it: `research/bout-baseline.mjs` on the standing table's matchups with
`rulebook("arena", { unit })` at 138.26 and at 100 (Node bouts, Rapier, 120 Hz, each side's
balance named): how each bout ends, its length, hit points lost a bout. It is run and shown
before the owner chooses, and needs no code but the override the rulebook already takes.

## Tests

1. `core-rules`, **`the club's best blow is worth 1.38 hit points`**: `blowDamage(rules, "blunt",
   CLUB_BEST.value)` is 1.3826; "the unit's own blow, replayed, is worth one hit point" becomes
   this.
2. `core-rules`: 100 J of blunt blow is one hit point; an edge's 100 J is 5.73.
3. `core-strike-skill`, `the_repertoires_club_blow_is_the_damage_units`: renamed for what it
   holds now, the repertoire's club blow replayed against `CLUB_BEST`.
4. `core-spec`: `owner-damage-unit` is complete and its record's heading exists.

## Mutations, each must go red
