# The braced walk

## Why

The braced walk (the walk and run told it may not run) is the walk every fight and every crowd
moves by, and strikes and a stand-up end standing on it. Given orders that change, it falls: its
catch steps chain without settling, it holds 0.5 m/s where a person walks 1.4, and the run's late
braking sizes the 11 m braced rule. Each step here is one question, read paired against the
commit before it on a fast battery beside a placebo.

## Steps

0. **Movement B3** ([its plan](2026-10-21-movement-and-command.md)): the crowd battery passes.
   Check: `npm run evaluate -- crowd`, kept with `--write`.
1. **The walk's target**: what the braced walk holds under orders, a shove while it walks
   included, written as a battery and its bar. Check: the battery reads the commit it lands at.
2. **Catches that converge**: each kind of catch chain that grows (a step back short of the
   capture point, the pelvis carried past its held facing, a catch that does not crouch for its
   reach) fixed at its root or recorded as tried. Check: the orders battery
   (`research/core-stance-sweep.mjs --batteries orders`) on fresh seeds, beside a placebo.
3. **A faster walk**: the fastest walk held every way raised from 0.5 m/s, the swing's share of
   the step first. Check: the gait battery, then the stance envelope measured again
   (`research/core-stance-envelope.mjs --write`).
4. **Faster braking**: the walk and run brakes harder than 0.15 m/s² with no fall more, and the
   braced rule's 11 m is sized to it. Check: the gait battery's stops and the crowd battery.

Each step lands under the normal gate with the fingerprint cases it moves named.

## Done when

Step 1's bar is met, and the walk is faster and brakes harder than now, each with its record in
[locomotion](../reference/locomotion.md). What is built then goes into the architecture, and this
file is deleted.
