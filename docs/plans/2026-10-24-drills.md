# Drills: skills proved one rung at a time, in plain sight

## Why

The owner wants two fighters to have a good fight in the Arena. Today an empty-handed Duellist
against Vanguard mostly punches empty air, and other matchups close in and flail. The research
has judged nearly every change by fight wins on fresh cases. That signal is slow, noisy and
dominated by falls (the oracle's ceiling is not falling,
[the tables](../reference/oracle.md#the-tables)). It also mixes the layers, so a gain or a loss
names no layer, and a skill tuned through fights is tuned through spacing and target choices it
may not be able to use.

So the work is split three ways, and each part gets its own fast, visible signal:

| Layer | Question | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Skill | Can the body do it? | a drill: a rung of a ladder, seconds a trial |
| Proficiency | How well, where? | the skill's profile: its drill readings over its entry condition |
| Use | What should a fighter do with the skills it has? | tactics that read the profiles |

A skill here has the shape the [Kinetic](2026-10-27-kinetic.md#the-shape-of-a-skill) plan gives
it: a name and goal, an entry condition, resources and a profile. A drill samples a skill's entry condition and a little
beyond it, so the edge is measured, and the profile is indexed by the same variables.

The fight is judged by the owner's verdict alone (step 7), a black box. A bout is the check that a
gain carries into fights, not the place a skill is tuned. Each skill's or mind's developer picks
any sub-goals it likes inside its own layer. A sub-goal stays only while improving it moves the
verdict or what the owner sees. No sub-goal enters the verdict or a rating.

## What the owner sees

Visibility comes first. A number is only as good as the trial behind it, and a claimed skill has
often looked broken when watched.

- **A trial is a bout world**: the drilled mind under drill tactics (`drill-tactics.ts`) against a
  target, so the Arena plays a trial as it was scored, to the bit in Node and the page.
- **The strip**, on a drill subject's Ratings page: a square a trial, coloured by its outcome, a
  row for each condition but range, a column for each range; a click plays the strip in the Arena
  from that trial. Each outcome in the legend plays that outcome's trials.
- **The reel**: every failure, the rarest outcome's first, then five passes chosen by their keys'
  digests, so nobody picks what is shown. The Arena plays a reel in place, each trial as long as it
  was scored, the next after a second with auto-play on (the default), the camera where the owner
  left it.
- **Overlays** (step 2): the aim, the target's path, the effector's path, each contact; for the
  trial, why it failed (outside the entry condition, the target moved, short of the aim, blocked,
  balance lost).
- **Side by side** (step 2): `&previous=<version>` plays the same trial under both versions in step.
- **The owner's acceptance**: a rung is passed only when the owner has watched it and said so. The
  agent writes it beside the battery's records (`assets/evaluations/<id>/accepted.json`: rung,
  subject, version, date, what was said), tied to the version, so a changed skill is a new version
  and is not accepted. The subject's page shows it. A trial that lands but looks broken is a
  failure the owner marks.

The rules for claims, the reel and acceptance are `AGENTS.md`'s
([Checking a change](../../AGENTS.md#checking-a-change)).

## The drills

Every drill takes the same subject options: the body (Warrior, Rogue, Skeleton; the Reptile later),
the loadout, the character (physique, Balance, Haste), the skill's version and `previous`, the
rate (120 Hz; 480 Hz to check a reading is physical) and a few seeds a condition, a seed moving the
start a little. Each family is one battery (`src/evaluation/<family>/`, arity 1), its rungs its
protocols; Ratings lists them under Skills. A rung starts a skill from each state its entry
covers (at rest, mid-step, closing), and its readings are given by starting state, so a widened
entry and a split between movements both show.

**Strike**, one drill per strike skill (jab, cross, the walking jab, front kick, club blow, sword
cut, sword thrust), on the same rungs:

| Rung | Target | Options | Readings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | a Warrior standing in guard (`still`) | range, mark, offset across, hand or foot | landed, time to contact, speed and the mass met, upright, time back to guard |
| fight | the striker's own bout against a game mind, the drill throwing in place of its tactics' `moment`th action (`fight`) | foe, moment, mark, hand | the same, and guarded |
| 1 | pad moving in a line | and its speed and direction (toward, away, across) | the same |
| 2 | drill opponent stepping | range, rhythm, region (head, trunk, legs) | and the wound dealt |
| 3 | drill opponent guarding | and its guard | and blocked or through |
| 4 | drill opponent jabbing back | and its rhythm | and the wound taken, falls |
| 5 | struck from motion | from a walk (`walk`: asked settled or still walking), after a step, after being hit | the same, and the time from the first step ready |

**Footwork**, getting to a range and keeping it:

| Rung | Target | Options | Readings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | still | start distance, bearing, range wanted, height | time to the range, overshoot, falls |
| 1 | moving in a line | its speed and direction | range error over time, falls |
| 2 | drill opponent stepping in and out | rhythm, step | the same |
| 3 | circling at range | way, speed | and facing error |
| 4 | in, then out | range in, range out, time inside | time each phase, falls |
| 5 | among obstacles | layout | the same |

**Stand**: a shove standing in guard, then walking, then mid-strike (size, direction, height;
upright, steps, time to settle; mid-strike, whether the strike finished). **Combination**: a strike
handed over at its stroke's end to the next (the one-two, [Kinetic](2026-10-27-kinetic.md)
step 9), on Strike's rungs 0 to 3, beside its parts thrown in turn (both landed, the time between).
**Cover**: rung 0 a swinging club, rung 1 a drill opponent striking on its script, rung 2 cover
and a counter (side, height, speed, warning; covered, wound taken, counter landed). **Rise**
(`rise`, built): each edge of the staged route from its posture placed and in motion after the
edges before it ([the Rise drill](../reference/rising.md#the-rise-drill)), then the owner's 64
falls (`rising-target`), armed, then with an opponent near. **Run**: straight, then turns and
stops.

Two fixtures serve most rungs:

- **The moving target**: a pad on a rail, by path (a line, back and forth, a circle), speed and
  height.
- **The drill opponent**: a fighter body under a script of orders, as a bout's recipe and tape
  give it (`Duel.tape`). Its scripts: stand, step in and out, sidestep, circle, guard, jab on a
  rhythm, press forward; by rhythm, step, loadout and body.

A skill not built (a dodge, a clinch, fighting from the ground) brings its drill when it comes.

## Where it starts

- **The Strike drill** (`strike`, `src/evaluation/strike/`): the drill tactics
  (`drill-tactics.ts`), which walk into the action's window at `pace` and settle before asking, or
  ask still walking; rungs `still`, `walk` (rung 5 from a walk) and `fight`; the strip, the reel and the acceptance on the Ratings. Its subjects are
  a mind and one of its strike actions (`puncher/strike`, `duellist/swing high`,
  `vanguard-warrior/strike`), and the learned puncher given as a subject file
  (`research/learned-puncher.json`). Kinetic's `tactics` take the drill in its chooser's place
  (`kinetic/strike`). A mind with no tactics slot (`options`, `tempo`) is not drilled yet.
- **The Rise drill** (`rise`) and the `rising-target` and `equipped-rise` evaluations; the
  ground edges' profiles regenerate from the Rise drill's records
  (`assets/core/edge-profiles.json`, `research/edge-profiles.mjs`).
- **Fixtures with no drill yet**: the competency suite (`research/competencies.mjs`,
  [competencies](../reference/competencies.md)), the punch and kick stands (`punchStand`,
  `research/punch-calibration.mjs`; `research/front-kicks.mjs`), the shove and walk trials
  (`research/core-stance-trials.mjs`), the swinging target and the guard probe
  (`src/core/tasks/swing-target.ts`, `defense.ts`); the `blade`, `buckler` and `court` batteries
  ([evaluations](../architecture.md#evaluations)); the Lab's Attack scenario, free play at moving
  targets, not graded cases.

Footwork's drills wait on the legs' skills in the shape ([Kinetic](2026-10-27-kinetic.md), step 11).

Check: each script plays as written in an empty arena (its body's path against the script's);
the owner watches each rung.

### 6. Profiles that tactics read

A skill's profile is its battery record summarised over a few of its entry's percepts (range and
closing speed, not every percept): done, seconds and where it ends, with payoff (hit chance, the
mass and speed met, falls). Its type is the one the [Kinetic](2026-10-27-kinetic.md#the-profile)
plan grows from the ground edges' `EdgeProfile`, and `assets/core/edge-profiles.json` is the
precedent: a profile file regenerated from drill records by a script that names them. It is
written as data a skill or an entrant ships (`assets/core/`, `assets/entrants/<name>/`). A tactics
part reads the entry conditions and the profiles to choose when and which skill to use, never the
skill's code. A changed body or skill is measured again, not searched again.

Check: a profile regenerates from a record; a tactics part choosing by profile against the same
tactics without it, by `npm run match`.

### 7. The owner's verdict

A bout lasts at most two minutes; from the first minute each side loses 1/60 of its full life a
second; a side is out when its life reaches zero. There is no other rule: a fall ends nothing,
and the cap judges nothing, since by two minutes the drain has emptied every life (both out on one
step is a draw, as now). The game's end rules (`GAME_ENDS`, `src/arena/end-rule.ts`) become the
damage and the drain; the cap is 120 s. Records read under the old verdict are read again before
any comparison.

Check: `npm run check`, `npm test`; a bout of two untouched fighters ends at 120 s drawn; the
owner watches a bout in the Arena.

### 8. The other families, as their skills are worked on

Stand, Combination, Cover and Run, each wrapping its existing fixture where there is one:
Run the gait battery of `research/locomotion-run.mjs`, and each family what the capabilities
`tests/*-capability.test.mjs@2a1b29ac` read. Check: each gives, for its reference subject, the
summary its script gave at the commit before.

## Done when

Strike and Footwork each have their drills with the owner's acceptance at rung 4, and Cover at
rung 2, for at least one skill; a fighter's tactics choose by the entry conditions and profiles;
and the owner watches two such fighters fight in the Arena under the verdict and accepts it.
