# Session 06 -- broad-form blockout

## Outcome

Create a production-coherent full-body blockout whose anatomy, stance, equipment
projection, torso/rear volume, and major contacts are visibly closer in all
eight views before any formal phase-02 experiment is consumed.

## Implementation

- Use the residual atlas to rank broad structural subsystems by visible area,
  cross-view consistency, expected calibrated gain, and representation
  readiness.
- Solve skeleton proportions/pose and rigid shield/sword transforms across all
  views before small armour detail.
- Author complete torso/rear and waist transitions rather than adding strips or
  rivets to the phase-01 egg mass.
- Produce two or three materially distinct blockout candidates outside the
  experiment counter. Reject gaps, floating hardware, repeated primitive
  language, implausible articulation, and inconsistent equipment contact.
- Run blinded human A/B comparison against phase 01 and between variants. Carry
  only the preferred mechanically valid blockout forward.

## Verification

All region/contact diagnostics expected to move must improve coherently in at
least two adjacent views, with bounded spill elsewhere. The session does not
claim final similarity and does not change the frozen ruler.
