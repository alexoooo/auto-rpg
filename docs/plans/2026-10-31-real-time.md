# Ten fighters in real time

## Why

The owner wants 60 fps, at least 30, with 10 or more fighters and richer minds. The world now
steps in a worker and the page draws between its frames
([the world in a worker](../architecture.md#the-world-in-a-worker)), so the page holds its frame
rate whatever a step costs. What still shows is the world falling behind real time: the page
draws one step behind the newest frame (`src/worker/client.ts`), so a world more than a step late
holds its picture until the next frame comes, and a world more than `CATCH_UP_SECONDS` late
(`src/worker/serve.ts`) loses that time for good and plays in slow motion.

Where it falls behind, at `003a413f` (Node, core world, `rapier-coordinate`, 120 Hz, pinned to
the performance cores, the least of each step over the playings):

- **The crowd fight** (`crowdFight`, `src/arena/team-bout.ts`), its first 20 s: a mean step of
  8.83 ms against the 8.33 that 120 Hz allows, so it never keeps real time; past the first 2 s,
  1293 of 2160 steps over 8.33 ms, the dearest 17.4. A playing collected garbage with a
  mark-compact of 28 ms once and a scavenge every 21 steps (1.25 ms). Under the CPU profiler:
  Rapier's step 23.6 %; navigation 11.9 % (`crowdWalk` 6.9, `reachOf` 4.2, the clear-step sweep
  3.6, `src/core/navigation/`); Option Mind's tactics 15.7 % (the chooser's appraisal of each
  foe 7.2, the strike option's offer 6.5); motor control 13.1 %; the ground's read
  (`src/core/control/ground.ts`) about 4; the dynamics' update 4.4; touches 3.9.
- **The Crypt** builds each foe inside a step as the party nears (`DungeonRun.build`,
  `src/dungeon/run.ts`): its dearest steps past 2 s were 142 ms at seed 10 and 92 at seed 25
  (`8d0db179`). Warm, a body's build takes 2 to 4 ms, its Option Mind's making 20 to 25 ms on a
  Warrior and 11 to 13 on a Skeleton (`createMind`), and its first step about 10 ms more than the
  next. Of making a Warrior's mind, about 30 % is the rise's ground executor (`groundExecutor`,
  `src/core/recovery/ground/held.ts`), 19 points of it checking the recovery network
  (`networkFaults`, `src/core/learning/recovery-network.ts`), and 22 % the strike executors
  (`configuredExecutors`, `src/core/tactics/action-config.ts`): each made again for every body
  from the same frozen config and spec.
- **The reference bouts** (sword against club and bare hands at seed 0): 22 and 26 steps over
  8.33 ms, the dearest 10.3 and 10.8 (`8d0db179`); none is more than a step behind.

## The bar

A world's **lag** is how late its newest frame is, read from its steps' times: after step k,
`lag = max(0, lag + t_k - dt)`, where `t_k` is the step's time and `dt` the step (1/120 s). A lag
under one step is never seen: the page is drawing a step behind anyway. A step dearer than 8.33 ms
that the steps after it make up is no fault; a run of them that keeps the world more than a step
behind is.

Lag is read on each playing as a page would see it, collections included, pinned to the
performance cores, and the least of three playings' worst lag is the figure.

## Steps

Speed rewrites (steps 2 and 3, and step 6 where it can be one) do the same operations in the same
order: no fingerprint case moves (`scripts/fingerprint.mjs`), the time before and after in
[step cost](../reference/step-cost.md). The other steps change what bodies do: each names its
moved fingerprint cases, is read against the version before on the drills it touches
(`--previous`) and in a match (`npm run match`), and is watched by the owner where it changes
motion.

1. **Lag measured.** `research/step-time.mjs` and `research/crypt-step.mjs` report each playing's
   worst lag and the step it peaks at, and what ran in the steps that built it: decisions made
   (`driveBy`'s), bodies built, collections. Read on the crowd fight to its verdict, the two
   reference bouts and the Crypt at seeds 10 and 25, recorded in step cost. Check: the readings
   above, read again as lag. Built (`lagOf`, `research/lag.mjs`; bodies built and collections,
   not decisions): [lag behind real time](../reference/step-cost.md#lag-behind-real-time).
2. **A mind's constants made once.** What making a mind derives from its config and its body's
   spec alone (the recovery network checked, the rise's routes and stages, the strike executors'
   tables, the strike measures and the opening selector's tables) is derived once for each config
   and spec, frozen, and shared by every body made from them; a body's own state stays its own
   (`src/core/state.ts`). Check: a warm Warrior's Option Mind made in under 5 ms; no fingerprint
   case moves. Built: 5.3 to 5.7 ms the median, 4.0 the least, from 19 to 21 (15.8); what is left
   is each body's own work; no case moved ([making a mind](../reference/step-cost.md#making-a-mind)).
3. **A body's first step.** Find what a body's first step makes that later steps do not (about
   10 ms), and make it at the build or once for each spec. Check: a new body's first step costs
   what its second does; no fingerprint case moves. Built: the first step is 0.5 ms over the
   second on the stand, 0.6 to 2 in the Crypt; a Crypt foe's build is shared by spec too
   ([making a mind](../reference/step-cost.md#making-a-mind)).
4. **Bodies built ahead, one a step.** The Crypt builds a foe when the party comes within a wider
   reach than the one it needs it at, at most one body a step, held until its level asks for it
   (`LEVELS`, `src/dungeon/run.ts`); each model's mind constants are made while the run loads. Check: the Crypt at seeds
   10 and 25 never a step behind; `research/crypt-step.mjs`'s held and built counts beside the
   step's; the moved Crypt cases named; on the owner's machine, the page's frames while a foe's
   puppet is made (a page this machine's automation opens gets no frames to read). Built: no
   build in a step that puts the run behind; seed 10 within a step, seed 25 23 ms behind over five
   steps of a fight, which is steps 5 and 6's; no case moved
   ([bodies built ahead](../reference/step-cost.md#bodies-built-ahead)); the page's frames wait on
   the owner.
5. **Steering held.** The walk `crowdWalk` chooses among its turns is a held decision
   (`src/core/mind/held.ts`): made again when the way asked turns or its speed changes past a
   tolerance, a body near it changes its velocity past one or comes near, the held walk's own
   check finds it now comes within another body's room inside the horizon (one walk checked, not
   every turn), or a quarter second has passed at the body's phase. What every walker reads of
   the others (`reachOf`, the sensed bounds, `src/core/navigation/sensed-bounds.ts`) is read once a
   step for all. Two bodies that meet still pass each to its right (`CROWD.left`). Check: the
   crowd fight's step and lag; no two bodies of a side within each other's room more often than
   before; the Gait drill and the locomotion evaluation's cases as before. Built so far: what every
   walker reads of the others is read once a step, crowd steering from 7.3 % of the crowd fight's
   step to 2.6 %, no case moved
   ([what every walker reads of the others](../reference/step-cost.md#what-every-walker-reads-of-the-others)).
   The held walk is not built: what it could take off is that 2.6 %.
6. **The rest, by the profile.** Each next dearest share in the crowd fight's profile, read where
   step 1's attribution puts it: the chooser's appraisal of each foe but the focus held until that
   foe's situation drifts (`appraiseAt`, `src/core/mind/choice.ts`), the strike option's offer
   (`offer`, `src/core/option/strike.ts`), the ground's read, the dynamics' update, touches; a
   reading with more than one caller a step read once, as the readings already are
   ([readings once a step](../reference/step-cost.md#readings-once-a-step)). Collections are the
   [garbage-free step](2026-10-30-garbage-free-step.md)'s: a mark-compact that alone puts the
   crowd fight more than a step behind is read with its steps. Check: each change's time and lag
   before and after; a change of behaviour as above.
7. **The mind at 30 Hz, where the bar still needs it.** Option Mind's decider deciding every step
   while one of its own blows is under way and at its rate otherwise (`Pace`,
   `src/core/mind/decider.ts`), then 30 Hz the presets' rate if the club blow and the stomp drill
   as at 120 Hz, which they did not with the intent held through the blow
   ([the mind at 30 Hz](../reference/step-cost.md#the-mind-at-30-hz)). Check: the baseline
   battery, the punch, club and stomp drills, the match against 120 Hz, the crowd fight's lag.

## Done when

- The crowd fight to its verdict, the reference bouts and the Crypt at seeds 10 and 25 are never
  more than a step behind real time (Node, core world, `rapier-coordinate`, 120 Hz, pinned, the
  least of three playings' worst lag).
- The owner plays the crowd fight and the Crypt and sees 60 fps, never below 30, and no slow motion.
