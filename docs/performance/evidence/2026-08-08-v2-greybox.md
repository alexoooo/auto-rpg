## Interpretation and remaining controls

The initial Canvas2D control and WebGPU capture are accepted as ordered slots 1 and 2
of 4; WebGPU fails its written p95 threshold. The earlier WebGL2 file is a
retained diagnostic, not an accepted WebGL2 run in the v2 greybox performance
decision, and the early Canvas file is not slot 4. Before the owner waiver, the
remaining required series was a fresh forced WebGL2 capture followed by a later
repeated Canvas2D drift control. The repository may say
that this out-of-order diagnostic met the standalone WebGL2 threshold but may not
claim an accepted WebGL2 matrix pass, a paired backend pass, or a baseline-drift
interpretation.

## Owner decision and protocol waiver

On 2026-08-08 the project owner accepted this evidence set as sufficient because it
measures every shipped renderer at least once and directed the project to proceed.
That decision waives the missing ordered WebGL2 recapture and later Canvas2D drift
control for v2-08. It does not alter the measurements or retroactively satisfy the
predeclared protocol: only slots 1 and 2 were captured in order, the WebGPU p95 of
16.80 ms still fails the written 16.67 ms target by 0.13 ms, the earlier WebGL2 file
remains out of order, and the early Canvas repeat is not a valid final drift control.

The v2-08 phase decision is therefore **proceed/pass with a measured exception**:
keep Babylon as the reversible presentation bet and carry the WebGPU threshold miss
as known evidence. It is not a claim that the numerical WebGPU threshold passed or
that Canvas drift was bounded by the original four-run method.
