## Stop rules

- Stop early if a calibrated absolute band is reached.
- Stop a representation family after two production failures.
- Stop the ten-run block if three consecutive candidates fail their unscored
  readiness predictions; repair screening before consuming another ID.
- After 0094, archive and debrief before any continuation. At least one accepted
  representation-level change is required to call the block meaningful.
