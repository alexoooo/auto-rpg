## High-value ideas

1. **A coherent multi-parameter body solve.** Fit height, shoulder/hip width,
   torso length, limb lengths, stance, and equipment transforms together against
   reviewed structural masks. Earlier one-axis changes failed because each
   exposed neighboring compensations.
2. **A consistent target/oracle layer.** Use vision-assisted masks with manual
   correction and a view-specific 2D oracle to distinguish ruler/ontology error
   from 3D unattainability. Optionally synthesize one human-approved consistent
   turnaround from the concept sheets; never silently replace the target.
3. **Complete authored armour systems.** Replace torso plus waist transition,
   shoulder plus arm articulation, or head plus hair as connected meshes. Do not
   attach another badge, bead, tube, or torus to the old mass.
4. **Contact-first equipment.** Solve hand, shield, and sword transforms as a
   contact graph before modeling finger detail. A grasp cannot be repaired by a
   larger fist if the grip is staged elsewhere.
5. **Texture after alignment.** Once masks align, bake scratched plate, edge
   wear, mail, leather, skin, and hair response into export-parity UV maps and
   score them inside eroded material regions.
6. **Cheap batch search before neural scoring.** Evaluate dozens of candidates
   with silhouette, reviewed regions, bounds, and contacts; render neural terms
   only for the Pareto frontier; assign a formal ID only after full-resolution
   review.
