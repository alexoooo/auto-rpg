# Authored v3 torso-to-waist screen

## What this session did

It built the authored `v3` authority the
[session 05 handoff](../plans/warrior-authored-search-05-next-agent-handoff.md)
asked for, authored three torso-to-waist subsystems inside it, and ran them
through the unscored readiness funnel and the classical `rigid-v5` screen. No
formal experiment identifier was spent. The canonical asset, the accepted
checkpoint, and the frozen ruler are untouched.

## The authority

- `asset-src/v3/warrior_v3_plates.py` -- the authoring toolkit. Every surface is
  explicit vertex and face data lofted between authored cross-sections, trimmed
  along its own boundary, solidified into a real thickness, and creased by
  dihedral angle. Nothing in it calls a primitive operator.
- `asset-src/v3/warrior_v3_torso.py` -- the three designs, as authored section
  tables and column ranges.
- `asset-src/v3/export_warrior_v3.py` -- deterministic build, contract
  publication, the eight fixed review angles, and export.
- `asset-src/v3/warrior-v3.contract.json` -- root, semantic nodes, retired
  control nodes, regions, material classes, material slots, sockets, bounds and
  payload limits.
- `asset-src/v3/warrior-v3.blend` -- the editable source, saved with quads and a
  live modifier stack.
- `scripts/build-warrior-v3.mjs`, `scripts/validate-warrior-v3.mjs` and
  `scripts/compose-v3-screen.mjs`, exposed as `asset:v3:build`,
  `asset:v3:validate`, `asset:v3:screen:authored` and the screening sheet.

The subsystem takes ownership of sixteen control nodes: the padded torso, the
cuirass mass, the flat breastplate badge and its shadow, four cuirass ridges,
the belt and buckle, and three faulds with their three edges. Head, arms, legs,
gorget, cross-body strap, cloth and equipment stay on the v1 control, and the
accepted 0085 floor-pivoted root scale is reapplied exactly as the control
applies it.

## The three designs

| variant | construction |
| --- | --- |
| `forged-cuirass` | raised centre keel, breastplate lapping over a plackart, one integrated fauld of three lames, two hip tassets |
| `lamellar-waist` | sternum groove and pectoral planes, tighter waist, three waist lames with polished rolled edges making the over/under order legible |
| `battleworn-plate` | slab section, two front plates with the seam carried off the centre line, a scavenged reinforcing plate, a canted belt, two lames, asymmetric tassets |

All three carry front, back, shoulder and armhole transition, lower rib and
waist transition, a belted waist, and mail closing the crotch and upper thigh.

The accepted control is the top row. Full-resolution frames for every angle are
regenerated by `npm run asset:v3:screen:authored`.

## Readiness funnel

All three passed. Every authored plate closed into a manifold with no loose
vertices and its own arc-length UV island; all eight angles rendered; the
exported binary carries twenty-four regions, nine material classes, three
sockets and zero validator issues, inside the bounds envelope. There is no open
crotch, no floating plate and no swallowed thigh.

Two defects were found and fixed during authoring rather than shipped:

- trimming the armhole moved the boundary vertices straight down instead of
  along the body, folding the panel and exploding the solidify step. The trim
  now shortens the patch and redistributes its rows, so every vertex still lies
  on the authored surface.
- exporting real tangents exposed that the control parts carried a flat planar
  projection whose faces collapse to zero area, which is a hard validation
  error once tangents exist. Control parts are now box projected after their
  bevels are applied, and degenerate faces are dissolved at export.

## Classical rigid-v5 screen

The control row was re-rendered from the accepted source and measured
`0.7712`, matching the documented accepted classical distance, so the deltas
below are comparable within the frozen profile.

| variant | distance | delta | structure | silhouette | material |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| accepted control | 0.771220 | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| `forged-cuirass` | 0.766782 | -0.004438 | -0.003714 | -0.002093 | -0.007153 |
| `lamellar-waist` | 0.765676 | -0.005544 | -0.005715 | -0.006370 | -0.007488 |
| `battleworn-plate` | 0.770071 | -0.001149 | +0.007446 | -0.010506 | -0.004786 |

Landmark residuals were identical across all three, confirming that no
landmark-bearing geometry moved.

Per view, `lamellar-waist` is the only candidate that improves or holds
everywhere: its worst view is `left` at `+0.000499`, which is noise, and it
improves `right` by `-0.013537` and `front_right` by `-0.011636`.
`forged-cuirass` gives back `+0.007813` on `front`, the most important angle.
`battleworn-plate` is below the checkpoint margin, and its aggregate gain comes
entirely from silhouette while structure regresses, so it fails the screen.

`lamellar-waist` is therefore the finalist and the contract primary variant.

## What this does not claim

This is a screen, not an experiment. The numbers above are the classical
smoke score, not a full neural score, and they are candidate-minus-baseline
deltas inside `rigid-v5` only. Nothing here may be compared with a `rigid-v4`,
`rigid-v3` or `rigid-v2` absolute.

The subsystem also does not close the gap to the concept on its own. The head,
hair, hands, arms and legs are still the primitive control, and the burgundy
cloth is still the ignored-cloth legacy of the older cloth-bearing reference
rather than anything the rigid target contains. Those are the next distinct
subsystems, not defects in this one.
