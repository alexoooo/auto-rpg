Sword prototype

The blade is a physical object. There is no attack button — you swing by moving your arm, and you cut by driving the edge through something at speed.

Mouse
moves the sword arm — the cursor is where the hand goes
W / S
walk forward and back
A / D
turn
Q / E
strafe
Left button
thrust — extend the reach
Right button
pull the guard in close
Wheel
roll the wrist — turns the edge
Space
reset the dummy
Tab
toggle the physics readout
Esc
pause
