Sword prototype

The blade is a physical object. There is no attack button — you swing by moving your arm, and you cut by driving the edge through something at speed.

Mouse
moves the sword arm — the cursor is where the hand goes
W / S
walk forward and back
A / D
strafe
Q / E
turn
Z / X
roll the wrist — turns the edge into the cut
Left button
thrust — drive the point out
Right button
guard — pull the blade in close
L
lock on, then click an enemy — strafe to circle it
Wheel
zoom
Space
reset the dummy
Tab
toggle the physics readout
Esc
pause
