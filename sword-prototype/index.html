Sword prototype

The blade is a physical object. There is no attack button — you swing by moving your arm, and you cut by driving the edge through something at speed. Two fighters of one kind stand in the ring. Choose who drives each of them: a policy, or you — and change your mind mid-fight with C, which lets you step into either body without the blade jumping.

Idle stands there and can be cut apart. Swinger walks in and cuts on a fixed cadence, never once looking at your guard — so its openings are learnable, which is the whole of how you beat it. Duelist holds measure, guards between exchanges, and commits when your point leaves the line.

Controls

The mouse belongs to the arm, not to the camera. Where the cursor sits in the window is where that hand is asked to be, so the middle of the window is always centre guard and the arm has a home you can find again.

Mouse
moves one arm — the cursor is where that hand goes
W / S
walk forward and back
A / D
strafe
Q / E
turn
Z / X
roll the wrist — turns the edge into the cut
Left button
thrust — drive the point out
Right button
guard — pull the blade in close
Middle button / L
lock on, then click an enemy — strafe to circle it
F
the mouse changes hands — the one it leaves goes back to its policy
C
take a body, then click either fighter — the one you leave picks its policy back up
Wheel
zoom
V
camera — Overhead behind the fighter, or Fixed on a world bearing
[ / ]
swing the Fixed camera round the arena
Space / Esc
pause, and resume — the same key both ways
R
this bout again — and the setup screen once one has been decided
Tab
toggle the physics readout
G
the rig — collision shapes, anchors, joints and contacts
