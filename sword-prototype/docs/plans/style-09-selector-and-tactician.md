# Session 09 -- two table-fitted minds: `golem-selector` and `golem-tactician`

**Status (2026-09-07): implemented; the human gate is open. The selector leads the best style
on random pairs and trails it on mirrored, and the mechanical bar is missed by a quarter of a
standard error.**

## Outcome

Two minds fitted from Session 08's runs as tables. The selector reads, at its first view, its own
arm class and the opponent's weapon kind and reach band, looks up which registered mind wins that
cell, and is that mind for the bout. The tactician is the matchup set's planner rebuilt over the
new executor: a finer state, the fifteen options, tables fitted from the styles' exchange log.

## Frozen choices

### The selector

- The cell key is my arm class and theirs, both what `armClass` in `../../src/golem/champion.ts`
  reads off a view: weapon kind and reach band, `paired-` for me from my capabilities and for
  them read as both hands holding one kind, which the opponent view shows.
- Fixed at the first ask for the bout. Switching a director per ask is trivial for minds that
  are all directors over one executor, but the per-cell samples do not support a contextual
  bandit yet, and the close-out says so.
- Fitted by points a bout with shrinkage, cell toward (my class, mind) toward the mind's
  marginal, pseudo-count 32 bouts, in the table's header. A cell's winner plays only if it beats
  the marginal winner by 0.03 after shrinkage; otherwise the marginal winner plays. That is the
  winner's-curse rule the matchup set's tuning session learned across eight classes, applied
  across sixty cells.
- The candidates are every registered mind but the selector itself; the fit refuses a name the
  registry lacks. The table is src/golem/selector-table.ts, generated by a new script
  scripts/fit-selector.mjs from tournament rows, which already carry both sides' arm classes.
- The mind, in src/golem/selector.ts, builds its chosen mind lazily at the first view as
  `golemChampionMind` does, publishes the executor as a getter and the chosen name for a test.

### The tactician

- `../../src/golem/duel-model.ts` is parametrised over the option list and the state factory,
  so `fitDuelModel`, `observe` and `planOption` serve both vocabularies; the v2 tables in
  `../../src/golem/duel-model-tables.ts` re-render byte-identical under the refactor, and the
  existing tests say so.
- The new state, in src/golem/style-model.ts, gains the reach pair (shorter, equal, longer at
  the fencer's `reachEdge`) beside the gap band, their phase, my phase and the heavy pair:
  144 states per weapon pair, the fifteen options as actions, the shrinkage of the matchup set,
  successors seen under twenty times dropped. Tables in src/golem/style-model-tables.ts, fitted
  by a new script scripts/calibrate-style-model.mjs from Session 08's exchange log.
- The mind is `planOption` over those tables with the planner's lead-weighted dealt and taken
  from `../../src/golem/planner.ts`; the replan cost is measured against the 5 ms budget, with
  about 0.3 ms on a quiet host expected.

## Implement

1. The selector module, its fit script, its table, its registration.
2. The duel-model refactor, the style model, its calibration script, its tables, the tactician
   and its registration.
3. Runs: the selector's fit tournament on a seed apart from the evaluation seed, random pairs,
   cross, every hand-coded mind (eight, so fifty-six ordered pairs), 24,576 bouts with
   `--random 60` so more cells are populated (about 75 minutes); then the confirmation on the
   held-out seed through `evaluate` with the selector, the tactician, the best style, the fencer
   and the champion each as a `{policy}` contender on the same schedule, 1,536 bouts each,
   mirrored and random (about 40 minutes).
4. Tests, in new files tests/selector.test.mjs and tests/style-model.test.mjs: synthetic rows
   where one mind wins cell A and another wins cell B and a three-bout cell falls to the
   marginal; refusal by version and by an unregistered mind; a real 3 s bout where the chosen
   name is the cell read off the first view and the mind after is that policy's; the tactician
   picks `parry` against a read commit and `cut` into a read recover on synthetic tables; the
   picker loads both.
5. The entry: the cell table with counts and the choice per cell, the two confirmations with the
   structural columns, the replan cost. `../design.md` gains the selector and the tactician.

## Human gate

Selector at or above the best style plus 0.03 over random pairs with the columns in band, else
reported as level and kept as a policy. The tactician reported wherever it lands. And the owner
watches the selector on three random matchups: does a mind that picks minds look like one mind.
Verdict into this file's status line.
