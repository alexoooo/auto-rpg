# Session 21 -- adversarial close-out of the expanded arena and AI

## Outcome

Exercise every lifecycle, control, unit, art and promoted-AI path together; run an independent
adversarial review; record durable evidence; then delete this plan set. A green compile is not
acceptance for pause, camera, armour, body feel or intelligent engagement.

This session cannot begin without a passing session-19 candidate and completed session 20.
If research is still open, the plan set stays.

## Automated integration

1. Extend `tests/integration.test.mjs` to build every registered unit on both sides with every
   supported loadout/policy, refuse every incompatible cell by name, step a complete bout and
   dispose to baseline.
2. Run 25 restarts per compatible unit/loadout, 100 pooled arrows across flying/spent/parked
   states, every camera gesture/ownership toggle, every severable body and every adaptive
   controller payload. Census meshes, materials, textures, instances, bodies, constraints,
   active observers, particles, trails, DOM/pointer listeners and policy buffers/state.
3. Compare uninterrupted seeded AI bouts with pause/resume inserted at three timestamps;
   removing wall time yields the same fight record. Camera gestures and costumes enabled or
   disabled must also be record-identical.
4. Run the frozen tournament manifest again in verification mode: it reads the checked-in raw
   report and recomputes job identities/artifact verdict without spending or replacing test
   rows. Artifact, report and manifest digests must form one chain.
5. Predeclare visible seeds for bare Duelists, shield/buckler-versus-archer,
   Broot-versus-Warrior, Centipede and `adaptive-v1` on both sides. Record outcome, duration,
   opportunities, attacks, contacts, blocks, stalls, posture, vitality and unit events.

## Visible matrix

In Fixed and Overhead views, both zoom clamps and both sides:

- pause during walk, strike, arrow flight and post-verdict fall; restart each state;
- winner arm settling, twenty-arrow floor behavior, shield/buckler interception and repeated
  fist exchanges;
- wall approaches/corners, middle orbit/pan/cancel, lock on `L`, and every direct body/wrist
  control across takeover and hand swap;
- Warrior/Broot/Centipede silhouette, targeting, collision, death and severing;
- fallback versus imported armour while standing, walking, crouching, leaning and twisting;
- adaptive approach from outside reach, first attack inside opportunity window, contact,
  defense, recovery, crowding response and immediate verdict stop for every compatible body;
- the still-open historical checks: rig overlay, body-relative Fixed-camera aim, corpse 0.08
  versus 0.3, broader blood scale, bow pressure, axe thrust and normal-rate arrow trace.

Frame cost uses bracketed control -> subject -> control rounds on two visible machines and
reports median per-round differences with range. A hidden/software browser is not evidence.

restart disagreement, stale keyframed velocity, asymmetric collision masks, camera/hand input
leaks, hard-coded Warrior anatomy, cosmetic physics/provenance, resource census omissions,

restoration. Findings are fixed or named open; "no issue noticed" is not an adversarial result.
